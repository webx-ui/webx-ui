# @webx-ui/module-orders

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 31c9f61: «New order» in the orders' panel (spec §29.2): a manager with `orders.manage` finds products by a word, picks a variant, sets the quantities and the buyer's contacts, sees the order's own totals and warnings counted live, and creates the order — `placeByManager()` with the source `panel`; the card and the list mark it «Created in the panel» and the list filters `source = panel`. `Orders::previewByManager()` takes `contacts: false` to count the lines alone. module-cart: the optional provider contract `FindsPurchasables` (`find()` → `PurchasableChoice` with `ChoiceOption`s) and `Purchasables::find()`, which asks only the providers that implement it. module-catalog: `catalog.product` is found by name, article number, barcode or id, the unpublished marked unavailable.

### Patch Changes

- 31c9f61: module-orders: the buyer and the visitor (spec §13). The buyer's page of an order `/order/{number}` behind a signed link without an end (`Site\OrderLinks::page()`, the letters' `{{public_url}}`), in the language of the order behind its prefix, a wrong signature a 404, `no-store` and `noindex`: the public status with its description and the scale of the public ones, the marks, the lines, the money, the delivery and payment lines, the contacts — never the internal status or the manager's note; view `webx-orders::order.show`, layout `webx-orders.layout`, styles `resources/theme/orders.css`, the statuses in the cache group `order-statuses`. With `webx-ui/module-users`: «My orders» in the account (`webx.orders.account.*`, another visitor's order a 404) with the badge of the orders in progress, the warning before an account goes and its orders let go on `VisitorDeleting`, the tab «Orders» and the count on the visitor's card, and linking an order to a visitor — `Orders::linkVisitor()` / `unlinkVisitor()` with a history line, the journal and `OrderDetailsChanged`, in the card's tab «Buyer» with a search of the customers and as `orders_link_visitor`. module-users: `UserVisitors::search()` — the visitors by a part of the name, the e-mail or the phone, as «Customers» finds them.
- 31c9f61: module-orders: the checkout's groundwork — the switch `webx-orders.checkout.enabled` (`WEBX_ORDERS_CHECKOUT`, on by default) with the `checkout` section of the config, the table `order_checkout_drafts`, `webx:orders:checkout-prune [--dry-run]` on the daily schedule, the dictionary group `checkout.*`, and the tab «Checkout» of the order settings behind the new permission `orders.checkout.settings` (contact fields, recipient, comment, terms). Off — no command, no tab, no draft carried at sign-in. module-cart: `Events\CartsMerging(guestCartId, accountCartId)`, dispatched inside the sign-in merge before the guest's cart goes; module-orders moves the guest's checkout draft to the account's cart with it.
- 31c9f61: module-orders: demo orders (`webx:demo --module=orders`) — twelve orders through `Orders`, in every public status, with a refund, a cancellation, a rule's gift, a wallet's tender and, with visitors installed, three of the visitor's; no notification goes out. Guide `guide/orders.md`. module-orders panel: the dialog «Link to a visitor» keeps the preview's place with a hint before a pick, so the list does not slide from under the pointer. module-notifications: a letter to an address from the event no longer carries the variable (`{{customer_email}}`) as the recipient's name.
- 31c9f61: module-orders: notifications of the orders (spec §10), with `webx-ui/module-notifications` installed. Seven events — `orders.placed`, `orders.public_status`, `orders.paid`, `orders.payment_failed`, `orders.received`, `orders.cancelled`, `orders.refunded` — with the values of an order (number, links to the panel and to the buyer's page, contacts, the lines as a list, the money as strings in the order's currency, delivery and payment, the public status and, for the staff, the internal one) and default templates for e-mail, Telegram and any other provider in ten languages. A milestone's event goes to the channels chosen in the order settings (`orders.notify.*`), entering a public status to the channels of that status — chosen in its dialog; a move between the stages of one public status tells nobody. A site starts with three channels, made once by `migrate`: «Order accepted» and «Payment received» to the buyer, «New order» to the first e-mail of the site's contacts — switched off without one, and `webx:doctor` says so; without the notifications module the orders work as before and `webx:doctor` notes that nobody is told. The card shows what was sent about the order under its history, `orders_history` answers it as `notifications`, `orders_public_statuses_save` takes `notify_channels`, and `orders://catalog` lists the events. module-notifications: a variable may be declared `internal` — a channel that writes to an addressee and uses one saves with a warning.
- 31c9f61: module-orders: the panel's list and card of an order — views, search and filters, the selection moved to a status or cancelled with a preview, the card `orders.show` with the status, the marks, cancelling and refunds through dialogs that show the service's dry run, the contacts and notes; the ready-made role «Order manager».
- 31c9f61: module-orders: «Buy in one click» (spec §29). `<x-webx-orders::checkout.quick-button type= ref= />` beside «Buy» opens a dialog — a phone, a name if given, the agreement as the tab «Checkout» asks — that posts to `POST /checkout/quick` (`Checkout\Http\QuickController`, no CSRF: the product's page may be cached whole). `Checkout\QuickOrders` puts the line into a service cart no cookie carries (`CartService::add(null, …)`), places the order of it with `source = quick` and clears that cart — one transaction, the buyer's cart and checkout draft untouched; an order without a name is «Buyer» in its language. Antispam: the mark of time, the honeypot, the origin, 3 orders an hour an address and a phone (`webx-orders.checkout.throttle.quick_per_hour`); the same dialog twice within ten minutes is one order. Settings `orders.checkout.quick.enabled` (off) and `orders.checkout.quick.message` (a text a language) on the tab «Checkout». The list of orders filters by `source` (the milestone «placed»; `orders_list` too), a one-click order carries the mark «One-click order» in the list and the card.
- 31c9f61: module-orders: the screens «Orders → Statuses» and «Orders → Settings». Both levels of the statuses with the orders in each, dragged into order and into another public group — with the buyers who will see a new name counted first — written in every language, deleted softly with the consequences said first and brought back; refused when an order would have nowhere to go (the initial status, the last active stage of a group with orders, a public status with stages still in it). `OrderStatuses` does the same in code. The settings keep the number format with its trial number and the initial statuses in `module-settings`, the config `webx-orders` deciding without it. A move into a status that cancels now needs a reason from the panel and an agent, as `cancel()` does — in the dialogs of one order and of a selection too.
