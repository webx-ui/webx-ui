# @webx-ui/module-payments

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- cd801c7: `webx-ui/module-payments`: the groups of methods in the panel. The screen of methods lists the groups in their order with their methods: drag a group, drag a method inside its group or into another. «Edit» changes a group's title and description by language and its pictogram, as edits over `webx-payments.groups`: the config is not touched, and the panel has nothing to create or remove a group with. The form of a method gets the field «Group», the way to move it from the keyboard: it goes to the end of the group chosen, and the group of its type stores nothing. Two enabled methods of one group with the same marks are warned of in the list, over the form and after a save, which is never refused. New routes `GET groups` and `PUT groups/{key}`, behind `payments.view` and `payments.manage`; `PUT methods/order` also takes `{ groups: [{ key, methods }] }`, and `ids` still serves a site without groups. A row of `GET methods` has `group`, `group_key`, `marks` and `similar_to`, and the answer has `marks`.

### Patch Changes

- cd801c7: Покрытие справки панели включено: `AssertsHelp` и `HelpCoverageTest` в каждом пакете с экраном, патчем или разделом, `HelpPackagesCoverageTest`, `scripts/help-coverage.test.mjs`, команда `webx:help --check`, гайд `panel-help`. Всё, что ещё без текста, перечислено в `help-pending.json` и вычёркивается волнами наполнения. Пакеты регистрируют `resources/help`, у всех маршрутов панели есть `meta.help`, формы в панели списка передают ключ своей шапке. Кнопка появится, когда у экрана будет текст.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 460a04e: `webx-ui/module-payments`: `GatewayStatus` gets `declined` — the bank turned the card down and the gateway keeps the payment open for another try. The buyer reads «The payment did not go through: the bank declined the card» with the reason, «Pay again» and «Choose another method» instead of «We are checking»; the attempt stays `pending` to its deadline, a late payment counts, the page stops refreshing itself, and `/api/payments/{uuid}/status` answers `declined`. «Choose another method» is one button over every method of the order by its sum and delivery, on receipt and bank transfer too. An order awaiting an online payment says «Awaiting payment» on its page with «Pay» and «Choose another method» (`BlockDefinition::$prompt`, `Orders::closeNote()` in `webx-ui/module-orders`). A capture or a release the gateway refused reads «Capture refused» / «Release refused» in the journal and tells the gateway's reason in the panel; the note «Money held …» leaves the notes when the hold is over. `webx-ui/payment-liqpay`: `try_again` answers `declined`.

  **Behaviour change for themes:** a theme that overrides `webx-orders/order/body.blade.php` has to add the block `prompt` (`$order['prompt']['title']` and `$order['prompt']['html']`), or the buyer reads the old status with no buttons to pay; one that overrides `webx-payments/pay/page.blade.php` has to handle the state `declined`, or the buyer the bank turned down reads nothing. `webx:doctor` names such a copy; the payments guide says what to add.

- 460a04e: `webx-ui/module-payments`: optional type contract `ValidatesSettings` — `MethodForm::save()` asks the type about the settings as they will be stored and refuses by field, in the panel and in `payments_methods_save` alike. A field of `settings()` may carry `off_with` and `off_help`: `MethodPane` turns it off and empties it while the named switch is on, and says why. Gateway A's card gets the setting `instalments`, ruled out by a hold, as the example.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 9c212d0: Payments, stage P8: an online method in the checkout opens its attempt right after the order and sends the buyer to the gateway (`PaymentsCheckoutSlot::next()`, never throws; `payAgainUrl()` — the signed page of payment while the order awaits its money). The tab «Payment» captures money held — all of it, or a part where the gateway takes one — or releases the hold, each with a `dry_run` line first (`POST …/orders/{order}/attempts/{payment}/capture|void`, `orders.manage`); MCP `payments_capture` and `payments_void`. The order's bank details say «Purpose of payment» without the form's hint; the journal's «Signature invalid» stays on one line, its filters reset with «Reset filters». Orders: the checkout's antispam mark is the session's first drawing of the confirmation, so the column drawn again after a choice no longer refuses the first «Place the order». Core: `WxDatePicker` and `WxDateRangePicker` teleport their calendar into the dialog or popover they sit in — a day clicked in a modal dialog is chosen instead of closing the calendar.
- 9c212d0: Payments, stage P9: the guides «Payments» (the shop) and «Payment gateways» (a satellite and its contract test), the package in the registry, `webx:demo --module=payments` switches gateway A's three methods on where the fake gateway is. After the check of P8 on lab: an attempt paid short of its amount reads «Captured 50,00 € of 92,90 €», not «Paid», on the order's tab, in the attempts and the attempt's window; the order's history writes such a capture as its own line `payment_part` («Captured … of …», by the method), not «Top-up»; an open attempt's head has square bottom corners. Money held after a failed try takes «Payment failed» down (`Orders::paymentHeld()`). The checkout's «Place the order» saves a step left unsaved first, as its «Save» would, and goes on after the redraw; a step the server refuses keeps its errors at its own fields and no order is sent.
- 9c212d0: Payments, stage P8 after the check on lab. A hold captured in part sets the milestone «paid» only where it covers what the order owes; less is money in without the milestone (`Orders::recordPayment(…, part: true)`), the rest to pay — the capture dialog and its `dry_run` say so. A hold released by the shop is no failed payment: the order awaits payment as before, and the order's strip shows one mark of the payment, never «Awaiting payment» beside «Payment failed». The journal names the base's decisions `internal/captured` («Captured») and `internal/released` («Hold released») with the manager's name; the requests are «Capture request» / «Release request»; the order's history answers «Money held … capture or release it» in the manager's name. «Thank you» and the order's page do not say «waiting for payment» while the money is held (`BlockDefinition::$held`). The order's history names `payment:<key>` by the method's title (`SourceNames`, `source_title`). A rejected notification's description is short; its claim is the event's mark only, not on the attempt's head. The order's number in the journal and the attempts links to the order's tab «Payment» with the attempt open. The sum to capture is prefilled as the panel's language writes it; the attempt's events have no frame of their own and a gap under the head.
- 9c212d0: The payments panel after its review on a live site (payments spec, decisions 54–58). A payment takes the order out of «Awaiting payment»: an order still in that status moves to the new setting `orders.status_after_payment` (the status of a new order by default), whether a gateway or a manager marked it; one a manager moved on by hand stays. «Mark as paid» is one action: the card's button asks the document and the day and, with the payments installed, goes through the new orders contract `MarksPaidByHand` — the attempt, the journal event and the milestone together; the tab «Payment» has no button of its own and reads itself again after the head of the card acts (`HistorySubject.version`). The poll asks an unfinished attempt less and less often (10, 20, 40… minutes, at most six hours) instead of every five minutes until its deadline. A notification with a bad signature may say which attempt it claims (`InvalidNotification::signature(uuid:, gatewayRef:)`): linked to that attempt and order, believed in nothing, shown in red. The screens: tables of events with fixed columns that wrap instead of scrolling sideways, the time in one line, a signing key folded under its request, moments of a decision in local words, the gateway's and the method's titles beside their keys, an administrator's name instead of «admin #N», «how it was paid» on the tab (method, document, date, who) also for an order placed without a method; the tabs of «Attempts» on the line of the search, the filters of the exchange in the card's rhythm; the method's key removed after a confirmation, «Cancel» instead of «Keep», and no return address to copy into a gateway's account — it goes with every payment. The block «Payment» is no longer repeated on «Items». «Summary» shortens a gateway's payment id and links it to the tab «Payment» with the attempt open (`?tab=payment&attempt=`): a page can hold the selection of a screen's tabs through `screenTabKey` of `@webx-ui/schema`.
- 9c212d0: The section «Payments» of the panel (payments spec §13, §20.7, stage П6) and its npm half, the new package `@webx-ui/module-payments`: «Methods» in the order of the checkout with a method's words, rule of delivery, limits of the amount, the settings of its type and gateway, the hold switch where the type can capture later, and the gateway's keys as «set / replace / remove» — a key never reaches the browser, a blank field keeps it, a key in the site's environment is shown as such; «Test the connection» asks the gateway with the stored keys and anything typed over them, journaled as `out/connection`; the addresses of notifications and return to copy into the gateway's account. «Attempts» — Laravel pages with the tabs «All», «In progress», «Failed», «Problems», a method and a period; an attempt with its events and «Check with the gateway». «Gateway exchange» — every event newest first in keyset pages, filtered by gateway, method, attempt, order, kind, direction, signature errors, errors and period; an event shows its headers and bodies (formatted on screen only) and «Download» as an attachment only for the new permission `payments.exchange` (super-admin by default), and the other events of its trace; no route changes or removes an event. The tab «Payment» of the order's card — the method with its bank details, the state, the attempts with their events by time, and for `orders.manage` «Mark as paid», «Check with the gateway», «Change the method», the payment link with the amount the buyer sees, and «Problem resolved». The ready-made role «Order manager» gets `payments.view`. The demo of the module switches the two built-in methods on.
