# @webx-ui/module-delivery

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 9c212d0: Delivery: a carrier's settings take their options from its own account. A carrier implements `ProvidesSettingOptions` and marks a field `options: 'carrier'` (with `depends` and `search`): the carrier's dialog draws dependent selects — sender → contact, town → branch of sending — waits for the key and for the parent, empties the children when a parent changes, searches as the admin types, keeps the label of the chosen value in `settings._labels` (shown when the carrier is down) and says a value the account no longer has; «Refresh lists» goes past the ten-minute cache. A save asks the carrier again and takes only a value its list has. Agents: `delivery_carriers_options` (behind `delivery.manage`), and `delivery_carriers_update` checks as the panel does. The waybill: `WaybillRequest` has the recipient's first and last name apart (`recipientName()` joins them), `carrierLocality` — the carrier's own id of the town — and `extra`; a type that implements `DescribesWaybill` adds its own fields to the dialog and to `delivery_shipments_create`, and `supportsCod(): false` hides and refuses the cash on delivery. `CarrierLocalities::externalIds()` lets a satellite walk its towns. Breaking for a satellite reading `WaybillRequest::$recipientName`: it is `recipientName()` now; the order tab and the tool take `recipient_first_name` and `recipient_last_name` in place of `recipient_name`. A key the carrier gave no `label` is shown as «Access key», never by its code name.
- 9c212d0: The delivery in the order's card: the price of delivery is a row of its own in «Summary» («Delivery» between the discounts and the total; «Free» and «By carrier rates» in words), and «Items» no longer repeats the delivery block or shows its raw data — a block registered with `view` lives on its own tab. «Change delivery» on the tab «Delivery» (and `delivery_order_change` for agents, with `dry_run`): the town, the method, the point or the address checked as at the checkout, a new snapshot, the price in place of the old one with the total and the balance counted again, a history line «was → is»; behind `orders.manage`, not while a waybill stands. `module-orders`: `Orders::changeBlock()`, `BlockDefinition` `charge`, `price` and `view`, the history kind `block_changed`. `module-admin`: `HistorySubject.reload` — a node patched onto a screen asks its host to read the record again.
- 9c212d0: Delivery panel after review. Regions of a country have an order of their own (`geo_regions.position`, a new region goes last): dragged by a grip like the countries, kept by `PUT …/geography/regions/order`, and read in that order by the list, the locality form and the filters. A locality row has a `···` menu — edit, switch off or on (`POST …/geography/localities/{id}/deactivate`), delete by decision 42 (switched off when something holds it, the dialog says which beforehand). The locality search says «+ Add «typed»» inside its own list when nothing is found, opening the short form with that name; the locality of a point goes back to the one chosen before on Esc or «Cancel». The point and locality dialogs hold their fields without a card inside, latitude and longitude side by side once the dialog has room (a container query on the row). «New kind of points» is a link beside its select; the methods list keeps off the top of its card; the `···` of a country and the bin of a popular town stand off the row's edge.
- 9c212d0: Delivery panel in plain words. Carriers, method types and locality types are named by their titles, never by their keys: the optional contract `LabelledMethodType` (`title()`, `description()`) names a type, and without it the panel builds the name from the kind after the dot or from what the type asks, with the carrier's title. «Add a method» is in the head and asks the type from a list with one line of what each is. The kind of points is the field `wx-delivery-point-kind` with «New kind of points» right under it, for the shop's pick-up only; a carrier's type with one kind no longer shows the field and keeps the carrier's kind. Own points: a table sorted by name, kind, locality or state, with a `···` menu (edit, delete with a confirmation) and a paragraph of what a point is. Regions: a sorted table with a `···` menu and one dialog to add and edit. A country is removed from its `···` — deleted while nothing is in it, otherwise only switched off (`Editor::removeCountry()`, `POST …/geography/countries/{code}/remove`). The leading carrier and the binding queue say what they are; a popular town is removed after a confirmation. Buttons in the heads carry no icons.
- 9c212d0: Delivery in the panel (delivery spec §14, decisions 40 and 42). Six sections in the group «Delivery»: the methods in the checkout's order (dragged; a new one starts from its type; the kind of points, or a new kind, for a pick-up; the localities of the shop's courier; the price in units), the shop's own points (a table by kind and state; the hours of the week and the exceptions), the carriers (each directory with its last success, a run going on, a held pass, staleness, records and remarks; «Run» to the queue, «Confirm removal»; settings and keys — a key is never sent back, only «set», and typed anew to replace), the geography by hand (countries switched on from the ISO list and ordered, the leading carrier of a country; regions; localities by country, region, source and state — made, edited, switched off, or deleted when nothing holds them; a locality like an existing one is offered instead, 409 with the similar ones, or made anyway), the popular towns of the checkout's dialog (added by search, dragged, removed) and the binding queue (bind, make a shared locality or leave unbound — by the rows ticked or by the whole filter). A town the search does not find is added right from the point's form and the courier's list. Permissions `delivery.view`, `delivery.manage`, `delivery.ship` (the word «ship» in `webx-auth::permissions`); described screens `delivery.method-form`, `delivery.point-form`, `delivery.locality-form` with the fields `wx-delivery-locality`, `wx-delivery-localities`, `wx-delivery-region`, `wx-delivery-country`, `wx-delivery-schedule`. The npm half is the new package `@webx-ui/module-delivery`: `...delivery()` in the panel.
- 9c212d0: Delivery: the order keeps the snapshot of §11.1 in every language with the payer of the waybill;
  waybills and tracking of carriers' methods (`CreatesWaybills`, `TracksShipments`,
  `delivery_shipments`, `webx:delivery:track` every 30 minutes), «received» as the order's milestone,
  and the tab «Delivery» on the order's card. The bin of the popular towns and of the opening hours'
  exceptions is the panel's red one. The order's card gives its id to the nodes its neighbours patch
  onto it.

### Patch Changes

- 9c212d0: Delivery panel after the walk-through on a real carrier: the list of localities with no country
  shows every country that is on, and an empty search says where the towns are (another country, or
  one switched off); the remarks of a pass come in words from the server (`NamesSkipReasons`, the
  base's «Skipped, for another reason: N» for a reason nobody names); a carrier whose point names
  carry the street drops the address line under them (`NamesPointLines`); a locality that is its own
  region is named once; the carrier's settings show their defaults as placeholders; «By hand only»
  is words, not code; a method named after its type has no echo line; and a toast over an open
  dialog goes to the top instead of covering its footer.
