# about

Banners stand in places: a place is a spot on the site that a template asks for by its key, like the
slider on the home page. On the left are the places, on the right the banners of the open one, and
their order here is their order on the site. A banner has no page of its own and is seen only where
the site's template prints its place.

# howto

## How to add a banner

1. Choose the place on the left.
2. Press «New banner».
3. Choose the «Picture», write the words and add buttons.
4. Turn on the «On» switch: a new banner starts off.
5. Press «Save» and check the place on the site.

## How to change the order of the banners

1. Open the place.
2. Drag a banner by its handle.
3. The order saves itself and is on the site at once.

## How to take a banner off the site for a while

1. In the menu of the row press «Turn off». The row dims and gets the mark «Off».
2. To bring the banner back, press «Turn on» in the same menu.

There is no schedule: a banner stays on until you turn it off.

## How to delete a banner and bring it back

1. In the menu of the row press «Delete» and confirm. The banner leaves the site.
2. To bring it back, switch the view over the list to «Bin».
3. In the menu of the row press «Restore». The banner returns to its place.

## How to add a place of your own

1. Press «New place».
2. Fill in the «Name» and the «Key» and press «Create».
3. Ask whoever maintains the site's templates to print this key.

Until a template asks for the key, the banners of this place are not seen anywhere.

## How to delete a place

1. Open the place. If its «Bin» has banners, press «Restore» on each: the bin counts.
2. Open each banner, choose another «Place» and press «Save».
3. When the place is empty, press «Delete» in its menu and confirm.

A place with a lock comes from the site's configuration: a template asks for it, so it cannot be
renamed or deleted here.

# fields

## Key

It links the place to the site's template, which is why it cannot be changed after creation. The
panel shows how a template asks for it, for example `banners('hero')`. Visitors do not see it.

## One banner, A random one, Slider

This is how the place shows its banners. It is set by the site, not in the panel.

- «One banner»: only the first one in the list is seen, so keep the one you need on top.
- «A random one»: every view picks one of the banners that are on.
- «Slider»: all of them in turn, in the order of the list.
