# about

A banner is a picture, and if you want a video, words and up to three buttons. It has no draft and
no schedule: «Save» changes the site at once if the banner is on. «Place» above the form decides
where on the site it stands.

# howto

## How to put a banner on the site

1. Choose the «Place» and the «Picture».
2. Write the «Title» and the «Text» and add buttons if you need them.
3. Turn on the «On» switch and press «Save».
4. Open the site and check the banner on a computer and on a phone.

## How to show a banner in one language only

1. Write the «Title» and the «Text» in that language only.
2. Leave them empty in the others, and press «Save».

To show a banner in every language, either write its words in all of them or leave them empty in
all of them.

## How to move a banner to another place

1. Choose another «Place» above the form.
2. Press «Save». The banner goes to the end of the new place.
3. Reorder it there in the list of banners if it should stand higher.

## How to arrange the words on the picture

1. Open a banner in a place that has the «Layers» card.
2. Add a «Text», «Picture» or «Button» layer, or use the three layers it starts with.
3. Drag the layer on the picture and set its size and look on the right.
4. Switch to «Phone» and arrange the layers for the phone's picture.
5. Press «Save».

# fields

## image

The place crops it to its frame, so keep what matters near the middle. If the file is deleted from
the «Files» section, the banner quietly drops off the site. A place of words only, such as an
announcement bar, works without a picture.

## image-mobile

A wide picture cropped for a phone often loses what it shows, so make this one narrow, with the
words large enough to read. Left empty, phones get the main picture, cropped.

## video

By default phones, and visitors who asked for less motion, get the picture, and the video is not
even loaded. A video cannot be saved without a picture. There is no separate video for phones.

## layers

The card appears only in places that print layers. What is shaded is cut off by the frame, and a
layer there is not seen on the site. «Computer» and «Phone» are kept apart, and whether the banner
is shown in a language is still decided by its «Title» and «Text», not by the words of a layer.

## title

There is no substitute from another language: a banner written only in one language stays off the
pages of the others. The panel also uses the title to name the banner in the list.

## text

Printed under the title in the language of the page; shortcodes work here. With the «Title» it
decides the languages the banner is shown in, so writing text in one more language adds the banner
to it.

## buttons

A button whose page has been taken off the site or deleted disappears by itself, with no warning in
the panel. Drag the rows to change the order of the buttons on the banner.

## button-label

The word on the button, written for each language: there is no substitute from another one. A row
with neither a label nor a link is dropped on saving.

## button-link

Where the button leads. Without it the button is not saved, and a link to a page lets the address
of that page change without breaking the button.

## button-variant

The list comes from the site, and the template turns the choice into a look of its own. Left empty,
the first look of the list is used, and the same happens if a chosen look later leaves the list.

## enabled

Switched off, the banner stays in the place with all its settings and only leaves the site. The
same switch is in the menu of the row in the list of banners.
