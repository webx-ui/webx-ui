# about

The buttons under the search in the checkout dialog «Where to deliver the order?»: one tap instead of
typing. There is one list for the whole site, and a buyer sees only the towns of the chosen country,
in the order of this list. With an empty list, the dialog shows the search alone.

# howto

## How to add a town

1. Start typing in the field «Add a town».
2. Pick the town from the suggestions.
3. If nothing is found, pick the line «Add» with the name you typed and fill in the short form.

The town goes to the end of the list at once. Drag it by its handle to move it up.

## How to remove a town

1. Find the town in the list.
2. Press the bin at the end of its row.
3. Press «Remove» in the question.

The town loses only its button: it stays on the site, and buyers still find it in the search.

## How to deal with a town marked «Not shown: the locality is off»

1. Decide whether the shop still delivers there.
2. If it does, switch the town on in «Geography».
3. If it does not, remove it from this list.

Until then the town keeps its place here, so a town switched on again comes back to the same spot.
