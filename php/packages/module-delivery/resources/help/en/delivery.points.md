# about

The places where buyers collect orders themselves: your stores and pick-up points. A point reaches
the checkout through a pick-up method of the same kind, and only for a buyer who picked its town.
Branches of a carrier are not listed here: they come from the carrier.

# howto

## How to add a point

1. Press «Add a point».
2. Choose the «Kind» and the «Locality», and fill in the «Address».
3. Add the «Name», the «Phone» and the «Opening hours».
4. Press «Create».
5. If there is no pick-up method of this kind yet, add one in «Methods».

## How to close a point for a while

1. Click the point in the list.
2. Switch off «Active».
3. Press «Save».

A buyer who had chosen the point chooses again. To open it, switch «Active» back on: nothing else has
to be filled in again.

## How to delete a point

1. Press «···» at the end of the row.
2. Press «Delete».
3. Confirm.

The point is gone for good, and only orders keep what was chosen in them. If you may need the point
again, switch it off instead.
