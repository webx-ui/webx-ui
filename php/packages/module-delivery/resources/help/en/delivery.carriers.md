# about

A carrier is a delivery company connected to the site: its towns, branches and streets arrive by
themselves and stay up to date. A site may have none: the page then says «No carriers are installed»,
and nothing is missing, because the shop's own pick-up and courier work without one. Adding a carrier
to the site is a job for its developer.

# howto

## How to connect a carrier, if the site has one

1. On the carrier's card, press «Settings».
2. If the dialog has «Keys», type the key from your account with the carrier and press «Check the
   connection».
3. Fill in the rest and press «Save».
4. Turn on the switch on the card.
5. If the card lists «Localities», press «Run» beside it: the points and the streets follow.
6. In «Methods», switch on or add the carrier's methods.

A key is never shown again: the dialog says only «Set» or «Not set». An empty field leaves the key as
it is, and «Clear» removes it. If the card says «Waiting for a binding», the arrow beside it leads to
the towns of this carrier in «Binding queue».

## How to deal with a list marked «Held»

1. See how many entries the mark says are to go.
2. Check with the carrier that they really are gone.
3. If they are, press «Confirm removal».

The update stops here when it would remove more than a tenth of the carrier's list, unless the site is
set otherwise. This protects buyers from a bad answer of the carrier: nothing goes until you confirm.

## How to find out why a list is out of date

1. On a list marked «Stale» or «Failed», press «Settings» on its card.
2. Under «Runs», read the error of the last one.
3. Fix what it names, such as the key, and press «Save».
4. Press «Run» beside the list.

«Stale» means that three scheduled updates in a row brought no success, or that there has never been
one. Buyers keep getting the last complete list. A list on «By hand only» updates only with «Run» and
is never marked stale.

## How to take a carrier out of the checkout

1. Turn off the switch on the carrier's card.
2. Check in «Methods» that the shop has other methods left for its towns.
3. If you plan to come back, leave the keys as they are.

All the methods of this carrier stop being offered at once, and «Run» stops working until you switch
it on again. Orders already placed do not change.
