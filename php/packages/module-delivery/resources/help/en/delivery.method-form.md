# about

One method of the checkout: what it is called, what it costs and where it works. The fields depend on
the type chosen when the method was added, and the type cannot be changed afterwards. Orders already
placed keep what was chosen in them.

# howto

## How to offer a new kind of points

1. In a pick-up method, under «Kind of points», press «New kind of points».
2. Fill in «Name» and «Code», and press «Add».
3. Save the method with this kind.
4. In «Own points», give the same kind to the points this method should offer.

## How to add a town that is not on the site yet

1. Under «Localities», type the name of the town.
2. If nothing is found, pick the line «Add» with the name you typed.
3. Fill in the short form and press «Create». The town goes straight into the list.
4. Press «Save» to keep the method.

# fields

## titles

The buyer reads it in the list of methods in the checkout, and it stays in the order. A new method
starts with the name of its type, so rename it in the buyer's words: two methods with one name cannot
be told apart.

## descriptions

Without it the buyer sees only the name and the price. Write what helps to choose between methods,
such as «Next working day», and not a repeat of the name.

## price

One price for every town the method works in, and it is added to the order total. On a carrier's
method an empty field works differently: the checkout says «By carrier rates» and the buyer pays the
carrier on receipt. A number there is taken by the shop, which then pays the carrier itself; only 0
makes such a method free.

## point_kind

One method offers one kind, so a store and a pick-up point are two methods. The method appears only
in towns that have an active point of this kind. A carrier's method lists the carrier's own kinds, and
you cannot add to them.

## localities

A buyer in any other town does not see this method. A town switched off in «Geography» stays here,
marked, but buyers cannot choose it.

## countries

It matters when the shop delivers to several countries: tick some, and buyers in the others do not get
this method. The list holds the countries switched on in «Geography». A carrier's method can also be
held to the countries the carrier works in.

## honours_paid_by_store

The buyer sees the old price crossed out and «Free», and the shop pays for the delivery itself. Switch
it off for a method the shop cannot afford to pay for. If the site has no such rule, this switch
changes nothing.

## enabled

This is the way to pause a method without deleting it: it stays in the list marked «Off», with all its
settings. A carrier's method also needs the carrier itself switched on in «Carriers».
