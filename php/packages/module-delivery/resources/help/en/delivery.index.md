# about

Methods are what a buyer chooses from in the checkout: collect the order at your point, have your
courier bring it or, if a carrier is installed, send it by that carrier. The buyer picks a town first
and sees only the methods that work there. The order of this list is the order in the checkout.

# howto

## How to offer pick-up at your own points

1. In «Own points», add the points where buyers will collect orders.
2. Back in «Methods», press «Add a method», choose «Pick-up at the shop's points» and press «Add».
3. Fill in «Name» and «Price», and choose the «Kind of points».
4. Press «Save».

The method appears in the towns that have an active point of this kind. A store and a pick-up point
are two methods, each with its own name and price.

## How to offer delivery by your own courier

1. Press «Add a method», choose «The shop's courier» and press «Add».
2. Under «Localities», add every town the courier goes to.
3. Set the «Price»: it is the same in all of these towns.
4. Press «Save».

## How to stop offering a method for a while

1. Click the method in the list.
2. Switch off «On».
3. Press «Save».

The method stays in the list marked «Off», with all its settings. Orders that already used it do not
change.

## How to find out why a town does not get a method

1. Open the method and check that «On» is on and that «Countries» does not leave the town's country
   out.
2. For a pick-up method, check in «Own points» that the town has an active point of this kind.
3. For the shop's courier, check that the town is under «Localities».
4. For a carrier's method, if you have one, check in «Carriers» that the carrier is switched on.

A method marked «Its type is not installed» is offered nowhere: what it was built on has been removed
from the site.
