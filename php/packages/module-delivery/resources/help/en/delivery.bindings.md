# about

Towns a carrier sent and could not place in your geography by itself: until someone decides, the
carrier's branches there are not offered. The list fills only while a carrier is connected and
updates its towns. On a site with no carrier it stays empty, and «The queue is empty» means there is
nothing to do.

# howto

## How to decide what to do with a town

1. Look at «Reason» and «Candidates»: the places that came close, with the distance to them.
2. For «Several namesakes» or «Another type», if it is a place you already have, tick the row, press
   «Bind», find the place and press «Bind».
3. For «Not found» or «Unknown region», if the place really is new, tick the row and press «Make a
   shared locality». A missing region is made along with it.
4. If the shop does not need this place, tick the row and press «Do not bind».

After «Bind», the carrier's branches there are offered at once, and the next update recognises the same
spelling by itself. «Do not bind» takes the row away for good: it will not come back here, and the
carrier's points there stay unoffered.

## How to decide for many rows at once

1. Narrow the list with the filters «Carrier» and «Reason».
2. Tick «Apply to the whole filter».
3. Press the button you want.
4. If a message says what is left, press it again for the next rows.

One press handles up to 500 rows. Take care with «Bind» here: every row of the filter goes to the one
place you pick. Rows that could not be done stay in the list.
