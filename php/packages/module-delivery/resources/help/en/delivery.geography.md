# about

The countries, regions and towns the whole delivery stands on: a buyer picks a town from here, and
every point and courier is tied to one. Towns are made by hand or, if a carrier is connected, arrive
with it. A town in a country that is off reaches no buyer.

# howto

## How to start delivering to a country

1. Open «Countries».
2. Choose the country in the field and press «Switch on».
3. If there are several countries, drag them into the order you want in the checkout.

With only one country on, the checkout does not ask the buyer for it.

## How to add a region

1. Open «Regions» and choose the country.
2. Press «Add a region».
3. Fill in «Name» and press «Add». «Code» can stay empty.

Drag the regions to set the order of the region lists in the panel. Only a region with no towns in it
can be deleted.

## How to add a town

1. On «Localities», press «Add a locality».
2. Fill in the form and press «Create».
3. If the form says «A locality like this already exists», check the towns it lists.
4. Press «Pick it» to do without a new town, or «Create anyway» if this really is another place.

## How to remove a town

1. Press «···» at the end of its row and then «Delete».
2. Read the question: it says whether the town will be deleted or only switched off.
3. Confirm.

A town that something refers to is switched off instead of deleted, and it leaves the suggestions. To
bring it back, find it in the list and press «Switch on».

## How to stop delivering to a country

1. Open «Countries».
2. Turn off the switch in the country's row.
3. To take a country out altogether, press «···» and «Remove».

The checkout stops offering the country at once. «Remove» deletes only an empty country: one with
regions or towns is switched off, and its towns stay where they are used.

## How to choose the leading carrier, if the site has a carrier

1. Open «Countries».
2. In the country's row, choose a carrier under «Leading carrier».
3. Leave it at «The first to sync» if it makes no difference to you.

The leading carrier makes the shared towns of the country, and the others bind to them. The field
appears only with a carrier installed, and it cannot be changed if the site's config sets it.
