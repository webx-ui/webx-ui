# about

Each recipe is a page of the site with a gallery, ingredients, a method and nutrition. Recipes have
one order, the same in every category and on every page of the site, and it is set here. What you
write waits in a draft and reaches the site with «Publish».

# howto

## How to add a recipe

1. Press «New recipe».
2. Fill in the «Title» and press «Create». The recipe opens.
3. Fill in the tabs and press «Save».
4. Press «Publish» and confirm.

## How to change the order of the recipes

1. Clear the search and the filters: a narrowed list cannot be dragged.
2. Drag the recipe to its new place.
3. The new order saves itself.

The catalogue, the category pages and the blocks all follow this order.

## How to find recipes with edits not yet on the site

1. Open the «Live» view.
2. Look for the mark «edits» in the row.
3. Open the recipe and press «Publish», or «Discard changes» in the «···» menu.

Until then visitors see the version published last.

## How to take a recipe off the site

1. Press «···» at the end of the row.
2. Press «Take off the site». Its page stops opening at once.
3. To return it, press «···» and then «Publish».

The recipe stays in the «Off the site» view with everything written in it.

## How to delete a recipe and bring it back

1. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
2. Confirm. The recipe leaves the site, and another recipe or category may take its address.
3. To bring it back, open «Bin», press «···» in its row and then «Restore».

If somebody has taken the address meanwhile, the restore is refused until it is free again.
