# about

One word of the list «Rich in»: its title and whether visitors see it. A save reaches the site at
once, with no publishing. Which recipes it is on is chosen in each recipe.

# fields

## title

The word on the chip and on the button of the filter, for example «Iron». Rename it and every recipe
that has it changes at once, with no need to publish them.

## is-visible

The word stays on the recipes in the panel, so switching it back on returns the chips and the button
just as they were.
