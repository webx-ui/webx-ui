# about

A category's page: its name, address, introduction and cover, with its recipes listed below. There
is no draft: «Save» puts the changes on the site at once. Which recipes are in it is chosen in each
recipe.

# howto

## How to hide a category for a while

1. Switch off «Shown on the site».
2. Press «Save».

Its page stops opening, the recipes lose the link to it and it leaves their breadcrumbs. The recipes
themselves stay on the site. To return it, switch it back on.

# fields

## title

The heading of the category's page and the word on the links to it, on recipes and cards. While the
SEO tab is empty, it also names the page in search engines. A new title does not change the address.

## slug

Left empty, it is made from the title. It stands on one level with the recipes, so a recipe and a
category cannot share one. If you change it, the old address leads to the new one from the moment of
the save, so links keep working.

## is-visible

The category disappears from the recipes too: its link leaves their pages and cards. Nothing else is
lost, and switching it back on returns the page with its address.

## lead

Keep it short: the recipes come right after it, and a long text pushes them down the page. Left
empty, the page goes from the title straight to the recipes.

## cover

The picture stands at the top of the category's page, under the title and above the introduction. A
wide one suits it best. Without it the page simply has no picture.
