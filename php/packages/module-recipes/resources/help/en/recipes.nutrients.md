# about

The list of what a recipe can be rich in: iron, fibre, protein. Each word is a chip on the recipes
and a button of the filter in the catalogue, with no page of its own. A save reaches the site at
once.

# howto

## How to add a nutrient

1. Press «New nutrient».
2. Fill in the «Title» and press «Create».
3. Check «Shown on the site» and press «Save».
4. Open a recipe, choose the word in «Rich in» and publish the recipe.

The button of the filter appears on the site together with the chip.

## How to change the order of the chips

1. Drag the word to its new place.
2. The new order saves itself.

The chips on recipes and the buttons of the filter follow it.

## How to see the recipes marked with a word

1. Press «···» at the end of the row.
2. Press «Show its recipes». The list of recipes opens, narrowed to this word.

## How to delete a word

1. Take the word off its recipes and publish them: the row shows how many are left.
2. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
3. Confirm.

A word still on recipes is not deleted. This list has no «Restore», so to bring a word back, make it
again. To take it off the site for a while, switch off «Shown on the site» instead.
