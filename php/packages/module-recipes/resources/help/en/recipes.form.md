# about

One recipe, on one page of the site with a fixed layout: you fill it in, the site lays it out. All
you change here, the categories and the links included, waits in a draft and reaches the site with
«Publish».

# howto

## How to put a recipe on the site

1. Fill in the «Recipe» tab.
2. On the «Settings» tab check the «Title», the «Address» and the «Categories».
3. Press «Save».
4. If the form has «Preview», look at the page first.
5. Press «Publish» and confirm.

## How to change a recipe that is already on the site

1. Make the changes and press «Save». The site keeps showing the old version.
2. Press «Publish» when ready.
3. Changed your mind? Open «···» and press «Discard changes».

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab.
2. Find the version and press «Restore».
3. Confirm. The version becomes the draft, and the site does not change yet.
4. Press «Publish».

## How to publish a recipe in another language

1. Switch the form to the language.
2. Fill in the «Title» and the «Address» on the «Settings» tab.
3. Write the «Ingredients», the «Method» and the nutrition on the «Recipe» tab.
4. Press «Save» and then «Publish».

Without an address in a language, the recipe has no page there and is not listed.

## How to take a recipe off the site

1. Press «Take off the site» at the top of the form.
2. Confirm. Its page stops opening at once.

Nothing written is lost: «Publish» returns it.

## What to do when somebody changed the same places as you

1. Read the notice above the form: everything else is already merged.
2. For each place choose «Yours» or «Theirs».
3. Press «Save my choices».
4. Or press «Take theirs everywhere»: what you typed since your last save goes.

# fields

## gallery

The other photos stand in a strip under the cover. Without photos the page starts with the title,
and the card in lists has no picture, so a recipe without one looks unfinished.

## ingredients

Printed on the page as written here, under a heading of its own. Left empty in a language, the page
has no such section in that language.

## method

Printed on the page as written here, under the ingredients. Left empty in a language, the page has
no such section in that language.

## nutrition-calories

Printed as you type it, unit included: «320 kcal». An empty line is not shown, and no other language
stands in for it, so fill it in for each language of the recipe.

## nutrition-protein

A line in the table «Nutrition per serving», printed as you type it, with the unit. Search engines
read it too. An empty line is not shown.

## nutrition-fat

A line in the table «Nutrition per serving», printed as you type it, with the unit: «12 g». An empty
line is not shown, and the rest of the table stays.

## nutrition-carbohydrates

A line in the table «Nutrition per serving»: write the number with the unit. If all five lines are
empty in a language, the page has no table there.

## nutrition-fiber

The last line of the table «Nutrition per serving». Write it with the unit, per serving, like the
others. An empty line is not shown.

## nutrients

The words come from the section «Rich in». A visitor filters the catalogue by them, so pick only
what is really worth searching for. The choice reaches the site with «Publish».

## title

The heading of the page and the name on the card; while the SEO tab is empty, it also names the page
in search engines. A new title does not change the address.

## slug

A change reaches the site with «Publish», and from then on the old address leads to the new one.
Links keep working, but avoid changing it for no reason. A language without an address has no page
and no card in the lists.

## lead

Keep it to a sentence or two: it is the first thing a visitor reads under the title. A description
filled in on the SEO tab replaces it for search engines.

## total-minutes

Printed on the page and on the card as «45 min» or «1 h 15 min». Search engines read it as the time
of the recipe. Left empty, no time is shown.

## servings

Printed on the page as «Servings: 4». Search engines read it too, and the nutrition is per serving,
so keep the two in step. Left empty, the line is not shown.

## categories

A recipe keeps its one address however many categories it is in. The card in lists shows the main
category. A hidden category is not printed, and the recipe still opens at its address.

## services

This field exists only on a site with services. Only services that are on the site are printed, in
the order chosen here, and the choice reaches the site with «Publish».

## related

Chosen by hand, exactly these are shown, in this order: two chosen is two shown. Automatically, a
few recipes are picked, three by default. A recipe that is not on the site is left out.
