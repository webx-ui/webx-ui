# about

A category is a section of the recipes with a page of its own, at an address on one level with the
recipes. A recipe can be in several categories. Unlike recipes, categories have no draft: a save
reaches the site at once.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category».
2. Fill in the «Title» and press «Create». The category opens.
3. Add the «Introduction» and the «Cover» and press «Save».
4. Open a recipe, choose this category in «Categories» and publish the recipe.

## How to change the order of the categories

1. Drag the category to its new place.
2. The new order saves itself.

The recipes inside a category are not touched: they keep the one order recipes have.

## How to see the recipes of a category

1. Press «···» at the end of the row.
2. Press «Show its recipes». The list of recipes opens, narrowed to this category.

## How to delete a category

1. Take the category off its recipes and publish them: the row shows how many are left.
2. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
3. Confirm.

A category that still holds recipes is not deleted. Once deleted, its page stops opening, and this
list has no «Restore»: to bring it back, make it again. If it is only needed off the site for a
while, switch off «Shown on the site» instead.
