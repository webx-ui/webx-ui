# about

The site keeps ready-made pieces of its pages, such as the menu, the settings and the addresses, so
that it does not build them for every visitor. A save in the panel refreshes what it touched by
itself, so this section is rarely needed: it is for the day the site still shows what the panel no
longer says.

# howto

## How to fix a page that shows old information

1. Open the page in a private window, to be sure it is the site and not your own browser.
2. In «Groups», find the group the old information belongs to and press «Reset» in the «···» menu of
   its row.
3. Confirm and open the page again.
4. If that did not help, or you do not know the group, press «Reset the site cache».
5. Tell whoever maintains the site which page was out of date: a page that needed this has a fault
   to fix.

A reset changes no content. If the page is wrong in the panel too, correct it there, and if a change
has not reached the site, check that it is published. A photo or a style that looks old is usually
your browser's copy.

# fields

## Reset the site cache

Nothing disappears: nobody is signed out, and pages say what they said. The first visits afterwards
are slower, because the site builds again what it had ready. Avoid the busiest hour, and reset one
group if you know which.

## Store

Where the site keeps these pieces: of interest to whoever maintains it. A warning here means the
site builds every page from scratch each time. It still works, only slower, so pass the warning on.

## Entries live

Even with no reset, a piece older than this is built anew, so anything out of date goes away by
itself within this time.

## Version

A counter that goes up with each reset of the group, by you or by a save. Its number means nothing
else.

## Reset by

What resets the group by itself on a save, in the site's internal names. «Only by hand» means there
is no such list, and not that the group never refreshes: languages, addresses and the theme reset
themselves when they change.

## Resets

Who reset what and when, by hand or through an agent. Many entries for one group point at a fault
worth showing to whoever maintains the site.
