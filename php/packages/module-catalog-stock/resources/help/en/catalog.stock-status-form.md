# about

A stock status tells buyers whether they can get the product now: «In stock», «Out of stock», «On
order». Here you set the status itself: what it is called, which tone it is shown in and whether a
product in it can be bought. The product takes its status in its own form.

# howto

## How to make a status a product cannot be bought in

1. In the list of statuses, press «New status».
2. Fill in «Name»: for example, «Discontinued».
3. Switch off «Can be bought».
4. Save the status and give it to the products in their forms.

On the site, these products show the name of the status instead of the buy button.

## How to change the status products get by themselves

1. Open the status that should be the one.
2. Switch on «Default» and save. The previous status loses this mark.

Products whose status was chosen by hand keep it. The new one goes to the products nobody chose a
status for, and to every new product.

## How to take a status out of the filter but keep it on the site

1. Open the status and switch off «In the filter».
2. Save.

Products still show the status, but buyers cannot pick it in the catalogue filter.

# fields

## title

The word buyers see beside the product and in the filter. When buying is off, it also stands in place
of the buy button, so keep it short and clear: «Out of stock», not «Status 3».

## code

Type it any way you like: the field turns what you type into a code straight away — «Brake pads»
becomes `brake-pads`, words in another alphabet are written in Latin letters.

Do not change the code of a working status without need: links to the filter that are already in
adverts and bookmarks will stop picking this status. Buyers see the code only in the page address.

## color

The tone gives away the meaning at a glance: usually «Success» for «In stock» and «Danger» for «Out
of stock». Nothing but the look depends on it. There is no colour of your own here, so that «in stock»
looks the same across the whole site.

## is-purchasable

The most important switch of a status: it decides whether a product has a buy button at all. Switch
it off only for statuses where the product really is not available. The change reaches every product
in this status at once.

## is-default

Handy when almost the whole catalogue is in one status: nobody has to set it on each product. There
is always exactly one such status, and it cannot be deleted.

## is-visible

Switch it off for a status buyers do not search by: a service «Archive», for example. The order in
the filter is the order of the list of statuses.
