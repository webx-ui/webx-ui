# about

The statuses that tell buyers whether a product can be had now: in stock, out of stock, on order.
Here you add a status, decide which one products get by themselves and set the order of the filter.
A product takes its status in its own form.

# howto

## How to add a status

1. Press «New status», fill in the name and save.
2. Open the products that need it and choose the status in «Stock».

## How to change the order in the filter

1. Drag the statuses in the list.

The catalogue filter shows them in the same order at once.

## How to delete a status

1. Press «Show its products» and move them to another status.
2. Delete the status. It goes to the bin.

A status that products are in cannot be deleted, and neither can the default one.
