# about

Deleted products and categories wait here, newest first. Nothing here is on the site, and nothing is
lost: a product keeps its pictures and returns under its old address. There is no way to delete for
good, because links from past orders have to keep working.

# howto

## How to bring back a product

1. Open the «Products» tab and find the product by its name.
2. Press «Restore» in its row.

If the row says the product comes back without a category, its category is deleted too. Such a
product returns unpublished. Restore the category first, and the product will return to it.

## How to bring back a category

1. Open the «Categories» tab and find the category.
2. Press «Restore» in its row.

The category returns to its place and its address. If the category it was inside is deleted too,
restore that one as well: until then buyers will not see it.

## What happens to the address of a deleted product

While a product is deleted, its address leads to its category, so a buyer with an old link still
lands on something useful. After «Restore» the address opens the product again.
