# about

Everything the shop sells, published or not. Here you find a product, add a new one and change many
products at once. A row shows whether buyers can see the product right now.

# howto

## How to add a product

1. Press «New product», type the name and press «Create».
2. On the page that opens, choose the «Main category» and fill in the price.
3. Add pictures on the «Pictures» tab.
4. Switch on «Published» and press «Save».

## How to change many products at once

1. Tick the products in the list. To take everything a search or a filter found, press «Select
   everything found».
2. Choose what to do in «Actions»: publish, take off the site, change a category or delete.
3. Confirm. A long run goes on in the background and shows how far it has got.

The window says at the end which products were refused and why. The others are changed anyway.

## How to find out why a published product is not on the site

1. Open the product and look at the mark beside its name.
2. If it says that no category of the product is on the site, open «Categories».
3. Publish the category, or the category above it, or give the product another one.

A product is seen only through a category that is on the site itself.

## How to find a deleted product

1. Press «···» at the top of the list and open «Deleted».
2. Find the product by its name and press «Restore».
