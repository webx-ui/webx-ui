# about

One shelf of the catalogue: its name, its address, the text at the top of its page and the filters
buyers get there. Where the category sits in the tree is set by dragging it in the list of
categories, not here.

# howto

## How to write a category page that search engines like

1. Fill in «Text» with a few sentences about what the category holds and how to choose in it.
2. Add a «Picture».
3. On the «SEO» tab, change the title only if the name of the category is not enough.
4. Press «Save».

## How to choose the filters of a category

1. Open the «Filters» tab and switch on «Own setting».
2. Leave on the filters that help in this category and drag them into order.
3. Press «Save».

Categories under it take the same setting, unless they have their own. If the form has no such
tab, every category shows all the filters.

# fields

## name

The heading of the category page and its name in the breadcrumbs and the filter of the catalogue. Short and plain works best: «Brake pads», not «Our wide range of brake pads».

## slug

It has to be unique across the whole site, pages included: an address that is taken is refused and
the panel says who holds it. If you change it, the old address keeps working and leads to the new
one. Moving the category in the tree does not touch the address.

## is_published

Products that also sit in another category on the site stay visible there. The others leave the site
together with the category. The address of a category taken off the site answers that the page is
not found.

## cover

Used as the picture of the category when a link to it is shared. Your template may also show it in
the lists of categories.

## description

Printed on the category page, above the products. Search engines value it: a page with nothing but a
list says little about itself. Also used as the description in search results until the «SEO» tab
says otherwise.

## facets

Decides what buyers can narrow the list by in this category: a filter by voltage makes no sense
among tyres. Until you switch on your own setting, the category takes the setting of the nearest one
above it. A filter added to the shop later stays hidden here until you turn it on.

## seo

How the category looks in search results and when a link to it is shared. Left empty, the name, the
text and the picture are used, which is enough for most categories.
