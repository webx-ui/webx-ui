# about

Everything about one product: what buyers read, what it costs, where it sits in the catalogue and
which pictures it has. One «Save» keeps all the tabs. Only the pictures are different: they are
saved the moment you change them.

# howto

## How to put a product on sale

1. Fill in «Name» and choose the «Main category».
2. Fill in «Price».
3. On the «Pictures» tab, add at least one picture.
4. Switch on «Published» and press «Save».

If the mark beside the name warns that nobody sees the product, its category is not on the site.

## How to show a discount

1. Put the price the buyer pays now into «Price».
2. Put the earlier price into «Old price» and press «Save».

To end the discount, put the usual price back into «Price» and empty «Old price».

## How to add and order pictures

1. On the «Pictures» tab, press «Add pictures», or upload one from an address.
2. Drag the pictures into order. The first one is the main one: buyers see it on the product card.
3. Fill in «Alternative text» beside each picture: say what it shows.

Deleting a picture cannot be undone: the file is removed at once, without waiting for «Save».

## How to see who changed a product

1. Open the «History» tab.
2. Find the change by its date. It shows who made it and what changed.

# fields

## name

The heading of the product page and the name on cards, in search and in filters. Buyers look for
products by these words, so write what the product is called, not a code from your accounts.

## is_published

Switched off, the product leaves lists, search and filters. Its address still opens a short page
saying it is no longer sold, so old links do not end in an error. A product cannot be published
without a main category.

## slug

There is rarely a reason to change it. If you do, the old address keeps working and leads to the new
one, so links in adverts and bookmarks stay alive.

## sku

Buyers see it on the product page and can find the product by it in the search. Two products cannot
share one, even if the other is in «Deleted». If your imports find products by article number,
changing it here means the next import will not recognise this product.

## mpn

The manufacturer's own code for this product. Buyers do not see it on the product page. It goes out
in exports and helps match the product with a supplier's list.

## barcode

Type the code from the packaging exactly. Search engines receive it with the product and use it to
recognise the same product in other shops. The search on the site finds the product by it too.

## barcodes

For a product with several packagings or several codes. Buyers do not see them, but the search on
the site finds the product by them. An import never does: it looks only at the main «Barcode».

## price

The amount the buyer pays. Left empty, the site shows «Price on request» in place of the price.

## old_price

Shown crossed out beside «Price», so the buyer sees the discount. Fill it in only while there is a
discount: it stays on the site until you empty it.

## unit

Printed after the price on the product page: per piece, per kilogram, per metre. Choose what the
price is really for, or the buyer will misread it.

## category_id

Besides the breadcrumbs, this is where the buyer lands if the product is deleted later: its old
address leads here. Changing the category does not change the address of the product.

## categories

The product also appears in the lists and filters of these categories, with no copies to keep. It
stays on the site as long as at least one of its categories is there.

## summary

Keep it to a sentence or two about what matters most for choosing. It is printed at the top of the
product page, above the full description.

## description

The full text of the product page: what it is, what it fits, what is in the box. Search engines read
it too, so a text of your own works better than one copied from a supplier.

## seo

How the product looks in search results and when a link to it is shared. Left empty, the name, the
summary and the main picture are used, which is enough for most products.

## priority

Use it to lift a few products to the top of a category. Most products can stay at 0. Buyers do not
see the number, and when they choose another order on the site, it no longer matters.
