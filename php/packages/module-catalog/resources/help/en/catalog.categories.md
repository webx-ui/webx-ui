# about

Categories are the shelves of the catalogue: buyers walk through them, and every product sits on at
least one. Here you build the tree, put it in the order the site shows and see how many products
each branch holds.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category», type the name and choose where it goes in «Inside».
2. Press «Create». The page of the category opens.
3. Fill in its text and picture, switch on «Published» and press «Save».

To add a category straight inside another one, choose «Add a subcategory» in the row of that one.

## How to change the order or move a category

1. Drag the row to its new place, between others or inside another category.

The address of the category stays the same, so links do not break. Only its breadcrumbs change.

## How to hide a category for a while

1. In its row, press «···» and choose «Take off the site».

Everything under it goes too. A product that has another category on the site stays visible there.

## How to delete a category

1. Move its products and subcategories elsewhere: it has to be empty.
2. In its row, press «···» and choose «Delete».

If it is not empty, the panel says how many products or subcategories are in the way. To see them,
choose «Show its products». A deleted category waits in «Deleted», and its address answers that the
page is gone.
