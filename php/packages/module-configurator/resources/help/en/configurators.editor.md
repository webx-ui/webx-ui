# about

Here you build what a visitor goes through: steps, options, sizes and the prices behind them. The
preview on the right works the way the site does. The draft saves by itself, and visitors see nothing
new until you press «Publish».

# howto

## How to add a step

1. On the «Builder» tab, press «Add a step» and choose its type.
2. Fill in the short name and the heading.
3. Add the options or the size fields, with their prices.

## How to show a step only for some choices

1. Open the step and add a condition under «Shown when».
2. Choose a step above it and the options it depends on.

A condition can only look at the steps above.

## How to publish changes

1. Check the list of problems over the steps. Click one to go to it.
2. Press «Publish» and confirm.

The draft is saved with its problems, but it cannot be published until they are fixed.

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab and find the version.
2. Press «Put back». It becomes the draft, and the site does not change yet.
3. Press «Publish».

# fields

## Currency

All the prices of this configurator are in it. It is not converted: changing it does not change the
numbers.

## Round the total to

Buyers see a tidy total instead of an exact one. The breakdown says that the price was rounded.

## Configurator page

Without it, catalogue products have nowhere to lead: the button «Calculate in the configurator» does
not appear.

## Request form

Requests arrive in the Inbox with the chosen configuration. With no form, they have nowhere to go.

## Give a discount

The discount is shown in the breakdown of the price and ends by itself after its last day.
