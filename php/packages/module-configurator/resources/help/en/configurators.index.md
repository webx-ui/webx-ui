# about

A configurator lets a visitor build a product to size: choose options, enter dimensions and see the
price at once. Here you see all configurators, whether each is on the site and which form receives its
requests.

# howto

## How to make a configurator

1. Press «New configurator», fill in the name and the key, and press «Create».
2. Add the steps in the editor that opens.
3. Press «Publish».
4. Add its block to a page of the site.

## How to bring back a deleted configurator

1. Open «Bin» over the list.
2. Press «Restore».

It comes back as it was, under the same key.
