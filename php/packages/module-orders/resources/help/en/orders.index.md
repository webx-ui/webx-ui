# about

All the orders of the site: what was bought, by whom, for how much and where each one stands. The
list opens on «New» while any order stands in the status of a new order, and on «All» otherwise.
The figures above it count the orders, the revenue and what awaits payment for today, 7 or 30 days.

# howto

## How to find an order

1. Type a number, a phone, an e-mail or a name into the search.
2. If that finds too many, press «Filters» and choose a «Status», «Placed through» or the dates.
3. Expand a row to see what the order holds without leaving the list.
4. Click the row to open the order.

The search and the filters stay in the address, so coming back from an order lands you in the same
list.

## How to find orders whose payment does not match

1. Press «Filters».
2. In «Payment differs», choose «Waiting for a top-up» or «Paid over».
3. Open an order: its «Summary» says how much is owed or has to be given back.

## How to change the status of several orders

1. Tick the orders. If the list has no ticks, your role only lets you read orders.
2. Press «Change status» over the list.
3. Choose the status and read the count: orders to move, buyers who will see a new status, messages
   to send.
4. Press «Apply».

Orders that cannot go there stay as they were, and the dialog counts them too.

## How to cancel several orders

1. Tick the orders.
2. Press «Cancel order» over the list.
3. Read how many of them are paid, and fill in «Reason».
4. Press «Cancel the order».

Cancelling gives no money back: open each paid order and press «Record a refund» there.

# fields

## New

The orders standing in the status new orders come into. An order leaves this tab as soon as it is
moved on, so an empty tab means that nothing is waiting to be taken.

## Awaiting payment

Orders the buyer is to pay online and has not paid yet; cancelled ones are left out. On a site that
takes no online payment this tab stays empty.

## Payment failed

Orders from «Awaiting payment» where a try to pay did not go through. The buyer can still pay, and
this is usually the moment to call.

## Marks

The icons of a row are facts: awaiting payment, payment failed, paid, received, cancelled, has a
refund. Point at one to read it. They do not follow the status: an order can be paid and still
stand in a stage nobody has moved it out of.
