# about

Here a manager places an order taken by phone. Prices and discounts are the site's own: nobody types
them, and «Totals» is counted again on every change. The result is an ordinary order, marked
«Created in the panel» in the list.

# howto

## How to take an order by phone

1. In «Products», find a product by its name or article number and pick it from the list.
2. If the product has options, select them under «Choose», then set the quantity.
3. Check «Totals» and any warning under it.
4. Under «Buyer», fill in «First name» and a phone or an e-mail.
5. Press «Create order». The order opens, and the list has it among the others.

«Create order» stays grey until every line can be sold. A product marked «Not for sale now» cannot
be picked. If the site sends letters, the buyer gets the same ones as after an order on the site.

Delivery and payment are not chosen on this page. If the order has the tabs «Delivery» and
«Payment», add them there once it is created.

## How to make an order for somebody else

1. Under «Buyer», fill in the contacts of the person who is ordering.
2. Switch on «Somebody else receives it».
3. Fill in the first name of the recipient and, if you have it, the phone.

## How to choose the language of the order

1. Find «Language of the order» under «Buyer». It is there only on a site with several languages.
2. Choose the language the buyer reads.
3. Create the order: the names of the products and the letters come in that language.
