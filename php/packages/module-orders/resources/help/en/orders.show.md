# about

Everything about one order: what was bought, who it is for, the money and what has happened to it.
«Status» is the shop's own stage, «The buyer sees» is the public status, and the badges beside them
are marks: facts such as «Paid», set once. The order keeps the prices it was placed at, whatever
happens to the products later.

# howto

## How to move an order to another status

1. Press «Change status» or the pencil beside the status.
2. Choose the status and read what it will do: what the buyer will see, which message goes out,
   which mark it sets.
3. Add a «Note» for the history if it helps.
4. Press «Apply».

A status that cancels the order asks for a reason instead of a note.

## How to mark a payment taken at the till or by a transfer

1. Press «Mark as paid». The button is there until the order is paid.
2. Fill in «Operation number», if there is one, and «When the money came».
3. Press «Mark as paid» in the dialog.

A mark is set once and cannot be taken back. An order still waiting in the status for unpaid orders
moves on by itself.

## How to change what the buyer ordered

1. On the tab «Items», press «Change the lines».
2. Change a quantity, take a line out or find a new product.
3. Check «After the edit» and what the dialog says about the payment.
4. Fill in «Reason» for your colleagues and, if you like, «Message to the buyer».
5. Press «Save the lines». On a paid order the button says «Save and recount the payment».

Lines keep the price they were ordered at, and a product added comes at today's price. A received,
cancelled or fully refunded order cannot be changed.

## How to cancel an order

1. Press «Cancel order» and read what the dialog says.
2. Fill in «Reason».
3. Press «Cancel the order».
4. If the order was paid, give the money back and press «Record a refund»: cancelling does not do it.

## How to give money back

1. Give the money back outside the site, by the bank or at the till. Nothing is sent to a payment
   service from here.
2. Under «···», press «Record a refund». Only an administrator with the right to refunds has it.
3. Fill in «Money back» or press «Everything left», and «Units returned» if goods came back.
4. Press «Record the refund».

Once everything is back, the order gets the mark «Refunded». If a refund was entered by mistake, press
«Reverse the record» beside it in «Summary» and enter the right one.

## How to take the money a paid order still owes

1. Look at «Summary»: «Top-up due» says how much is owed.
2. Once the money has come, press «Record a top-up» there.
3. Check «Amount» and «When the money came».
4. Press «Record the top-up».

## How to leave a word for the next manager

1. Under the tabs, in «Notes», type the note and press «Add a note».
2. For what everybody must read first, press «Pin a note» instead.
3. Save the pinned note. To unpin it, empty the field and save.

Buyers see neither. A note cannot be edited or deleted: follow a wrong one with a right one.

## How to send the buyer a letter again

1. If the site sends notifications, press «Send the buyer a letter» under «···».
2. Choose «What the letter is about» and read the letter.
3. Press «Send».

The letter goes to the e-mail the order has now. A line of «Notifications» on the tab «History» has its
own «Send again».

## How to correct the buyer's contacts

1. Open the tab «Buyer».
2. Change the fields.
3. Press «Save».

The buyer gets no letter about it, and «History» shows who changed what.

## How to fill in the extra fields of an order

1. Open the tab «Buyer» and find the card «Additional».
2. Press the pencil beside its title.
3. Tick, type or choose what the buyer told you. Empty a field to take its answer away.
4. Press «Save».

Nothing here is required: the card shows every field the checkout asks, filled or not, also on a
one-click order. A field marked «No longer asked at the checkout» keeps the words the buyer saw; a
line or a text in it still changes, a checkbox and a list can only be cleared. The buyer gets no
letter, and «History» shows who changed what.

# fields

## buyer-first-name

An order cannot be left without it. A one-click order placed without a name has the word «Buyer» here:
replace it once you know who called.

## buyer-last-name

It may stay empty. The list and the page of the order for the buyer show it after the first name.

## buyer-phone

In the list it is a link: a click calls the number. Clear it only if the order has an e-mail, because
an order needs one or the other.

## buyer-email

If the site sends letters, the next one goes to the new address; those already sent are not repeated.
Clear it only if the order has a phone. On a site with accounts, changing it does not move the order
to another account.

## recipient-first-name

Required as soon as anything is filled in this card. Empty all three fields and save, and the buyer
is the one who takes the order again.

## recipient-last-name

It may stay empty. The buyer sees it after the first name on the page of the order.

## recipient-phone

The checkout always asks for it, but here it may stay empty. The buyer sees it on the page of the
order.

## comment

What the buyer wrote when ordering. The buyer sees this text on the page of the order, also after you
edit it, so words for your colleagues belong in «Notes».
