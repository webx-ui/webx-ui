# about

One page of the site: its content made of blocks, its title and address, what it says to search
engines and its history. Everything you change here lands in the draft and is saved by itself.
Visitors see it only after «Publish».

# howto

## How to change a published page

1. On the «Content» tab, select the block and change its fields.
2. Check the result in the preview beside it, or press «Preview» for the whole page.
3. Press «Publish».

Until then the site shows the old version, and the list marks the page «Edited».

## How to change the address of a page

1. On the «Settings» tab, change «Address» in each language it should change in.
2. Press «Publish»: the new address starts working only then.
3. Read the question, which names the new address, and confirm.

The old address keeps working and leads to the new one. The addresses of the pages inside change
too.

## How to move a page inside another

1. On the «Settings» tab, choose another page in «Inside».
2. If there are pages inside it, check how many addresses change and press «Move».

This happens at once, without «Publish». The old addresses lead to the new ones. The home page
cannot be moved.

## How to throw away changes you do not need

1. Open the ··· menu at the top of the page.
2. Press «Discard changes» and confirm.

The page goes back to the published version, and everything changed since is lost for good. The item
is there only while the page is marked «Edited».

## How to go back to an old version

1. On the «History» tab, find the publication you need.
2. Press «Restore» and confirm: that version becomes the draft.
3. Check the page and press «Publish».

The site keeps showing the current version until you publish. Only publications are listed here, not
every save.

## How to take a page off the site

1. Press «Take off the site» at the top of the page.
2. Confirm: visitors now get «not found» at its address.
3. To bring it back at the same address, press «Publish».

Nothing written is lost. To remove the page itself, use «Delete» at the bottom of the «Settings»
tab: the page goes to the bin with everything inside it.

# fields

## blocks

Everything a visitor reads on the page is these blocks, in this order. Hiding a block with the eye
takes it off the site, along with what is nested in it, and keeps it here to come back. Which blocks
can be added depends on the site.

## title

Visitors see it in the browser tab, unless the SEO card sets its own, and in the breadcrumbs. The
content does not print it by itself, so the big heading on the page is a block. It cannot be empty
in the main language of the site.

## slug

Change it on a working page only for a good reason: people have the old link, even though it will
lead to the new one. A taken address is refused, not quietly changed. If a page above has no address
in a language, this one has none there either. The home page has no such field.
