# about

All the pages of the site in one tree: a page can stand inside another, and its address is built
from the pages above it. The home page is pinned at the top, and everything else stands inside it.
The tabs over the list show the drafts, the published pages, the pages with edits waiting and the
bin.

# howto

## How to add a page

1. Press «New page», or «Add a page inside» in the ··· menu of the page it should stand in.
2. Fill in «Title». Left empty, «Address» is made from the title.
3. Check «Inside» and press «Create and open».
4. Fill the page with blocks and press «Publish».

Until then the page is a draft, and visitors cannot open it.

## How to move a page to another place

1. Drag the row to its new place, or press «Move…» in its ··· menu.
2. In the dialog, choose the page it goes inside.
3. If there are pages inside it, check how many addresses change and press «Move».

This happens at once, not at publishing. Everything inside moves too, and the old addresses lead to
the new ones. Changing the order among neighbours leaves the addresses alone.

## How to make a copy of a page

1. Press «Duplicate» in the ··· menu. The copy appears right after the original, with «(copy)» in
   its title.
2. Open the copy and change its title, address and content.
3. Fill in its SEO card: that is not copied, so the copy does not pose as the original.
4. Press «Publish».

Only the page itself is copied, not the pages inside it.

## How to delete a page and bring it back

1. Press «Delete» in the ··· menu and check how many pages go with it.
2. Confirm: the page and everything inside it go to the bin and stop opening on the site.
3. To bring it back, open the «Bin» tab and press «Restore» in the ··· menu of the page.

What went to the bin with the page comes back with it. If somebody took its address in the meantime,
the restore is refused until that address is free. «Delete for good» and «Empty the bin» cannot be
undone: the addresses, the SEO card and the history go too.

## How to find a page in a large tree

1. Type part of the title or the address into the search box.
2. Read the address under each result to tell similar pages apart.
3. Clear the box to get the tree back.

The search looks in every language of the site, so a page is also found by a title in another
language.

# fields

## Status

«Draft»: never published, visitors cannot open it. «Published»: the site shows what you see in the
editor. «Edited»: the site still shows the published version, and the changes wait for «Publish».

## Address

Where the page opens on the site, in the language the panel is showing. A draft shows the address it
is going to have, so it is not a link yet. A page without an address in a language does not open in
that language.
