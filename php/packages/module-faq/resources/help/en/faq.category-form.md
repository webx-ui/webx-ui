# about

A category is a button of the filter in a FAQ block and a way to pick questions for it. It has no
page of its own and no draft: what you save is on the site at once.

# howto

## How to make a category only for picking questions

1. Fill in «Title»: «On the home page», for example.
2. Switch off «Shown on the site» and press «Save».
3. Put the questions into it, then choose this category in the block.

Visitors never see such a category as a button, and the block still shows its questions.

# fields

## title

The word visitors see on the button of the filter, so keep it short: «Payment», not «Questions about
payment». With the filter off, it does not appear on the site.

## is-visible

Switch it off for a category that only picks questions for a block. Whether the questions themselves
are published does not change.
