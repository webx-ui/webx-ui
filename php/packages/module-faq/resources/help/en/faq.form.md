# about

The form of one question. There is no draft here: whatever you save on a published question is on
the site at once. Below the fields stands «Link», the permanent anchor of the question.

# howto

## How to prepare a question in advance

1. Fill in «Question» and «Answer» and leave «Published» off.
2. Press «Save».
3. When it is time, switch on «Published» and save again.

## How to translate a question

1. Switch the form to the language.
2. Fill in both «Question» and «Answer».
3. Press «Save».

## How to copy the link to a question

1. Save the question at least once: the link is made by the first save.
2. Press «Copy the link» beside «Link».

Rewording the question does not touch the link, and a question restored from the bin keeps it.

# fields

## question

Visitors see this line closed, and open the answer by pressing it, so write it the way they would
ask. The link to the question is made once from the first wording in the default language.

## answer

Visitors read it under the question once they open it. No other language stands in for a missing
one: the question simply does not appear there. If the block marks its questions up for search
engines, an answer with no words in it, only a picture for example, stays out of that markup.

## published

Switch it off to take the question off every page at once: it stays in the panel with the mark «Not
published». There is no draft, so edits to a published question are not previewed anywhere first.

## categories

A question with no category is seen only in a block showing all of them. A hidden category still
picks questions for a block, it just gets no button. Each category keeps its own order of questions.
