# about

All the questions and answers of the site: the list on one side, the open question on the other. A
question has no page of its own: visitors see it in a FAQ block on a page of the site. The block
shows every question or only the categories chosen in it.

# howto

## How to add a question

1. Press «New question» at the top of the list.
2. Fill in «Question» and «Answer».
3. Choose «Categories» and switch on «Published».
4. Press «Save».

The question appears at once in every block that shows its categories.

## How to show questions on a page of the site

1. Open the page and add the FAQ block.
2. In «Categories», choose the ones you need, or leave the field empty to show every question.
3. To give visitors buttons by category, switch on «A filter by category above the list».
4. Save the page the way it is saved.

If the block is not in the list, ask a developer to install it: a new site may not have it yet.

## How to change the order of questions

1. Clear the search.
2. Leave «Category» empty to change the order of the whole list, or pick a category to change the
   order inside it.
3. Drag a question by its grip. The new order saves itself.

A block with one category shows that category's order. A block with none or several shows the order
of the whole list, each question once.

## How to give somebody a link to a question

1. In the menu of the question, press «Copy the link».
2. Add what was copied to the address of the page with the block: `/faq#paying-by-card`.

Only the ending is copied, because the question can stand on any page. When the link is opened, the
block opens this question.

## How to find out why a question is not on the site

1. If the row says «Not published», open the question and switch on «Published». The same mark with
   a tooltip on a published question means it has no language with both halves.
2. Check that both «Question» and «Answer» are filled in, in the language of the page.
3. Check that the block on the page shows a category of this question.

## How to delete a question and bring it back

1. In the menu of the question, press «Delete» and confirm.
2. To bring it back, open the «Bin» view and press «Restore» in its menu.

The question leaves every block on the site. Restored, it comes back with the same link.
