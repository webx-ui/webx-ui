# about

A category groups questions: a FAQ block shows the chosen ones, and in its filter each category is a
button. A category has no page or address of its own. The order of this list is the order of the
filter buttons on the site.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category».
2. Fill in «Title» and press «Create».
3. Put questions into it with the «Categories» field of each question.

## How to change the order of the filter buttons

1. Drag a category by its grip.
2. The new order saves itself and is on the site at once.

## How to change the order of questions inside a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its questions».
2. In the list that opens, drag the questions by their grips.

Blocks showing exactly this one category follow this order.

## How to delete a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its questions» and take the category off each of them.
2. Back here, press «Delete» in its menu and confirm.

A category with questions in it cannot be deleted. Its button leaves every filter, and this section
has no way to bring a deleted category back.
