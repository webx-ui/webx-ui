# about

Here are the people who have an account on the site: they registered, came through a social network
or were added by hand. Customers are separate from the administrators, and a customer never gets
into the panel. The list is for finding a person: everything about them is changed in their card.

# howto

## How to find a customer

1. Type a part of the name, the e-mail or the phone into the search.
2. To narrow the list down, open «Filters» and choose a group, the state of the address, the way of
   signing in, a block or the dates of registration.
3. Click the row to open the card.

A phone is found by its digits, however it was written. To see who is active, choose «Signed in
lately» under «Order»: customers who never signed in go to the end.

## How to remind customers who have not confirmed their address

1. Open «Filters» and choose «Not confirmed» under «Address».
2. Open a customer from the list.
3. In the «···» menu at the top of the card, choose «Send the confirmation letter».

Such customers have a warning mark beside the e-mail. They can sign in all the same, and the site
reminds them on every page.

## How to find the customers of one social network

1. Open «Filters» and choose the network under «Signs in with».
2. Look at the column «Sign-in». A crossed-out network is switched off, and nobody gets in with it.
3. If a customer has nothing else there, open the card and press «Send the invitation again»: the
   customer will set a password.

The buttons of the card are there for those who may change customers.
