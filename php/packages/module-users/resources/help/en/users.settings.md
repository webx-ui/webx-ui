# about

Here you decide who can get an account on the site, what its forms ask for and which social networks
customers sign in with. The fields at the top have one «Save», and each network has its own on its
card. If a line stands in place of the fields, the site keeps no settings, and these things are
decided where the site is configured.

# howto

## How to allow accounts by invitation only

1. Set «Registration on the site» to «Closed».
2. Press «Save» under the fields.
3. Add each new customer with «New customer» in the section «Customers», with the invitation on.

## How to let customers sign in with a social network

1. In the console of the network, create an application.
2. On the card of the network, press «Copy» beside «Return address» and paste the address into the
   application.
3. Copy the keys of the application into the fields of the card.
4. Switch on «Enabled» and press «Save» on the card.
5. Check that the mark on the card says «Shown on the site». If not, «Missing» says what is empty.

Google joins a customer to the account with the same confirmed address by itself. With Facebook, the
customer has to sign in with the password once for the network to be linked.

## How to switch a social network off

1. On the card of the network, read how many customers sign in only with it, if it says so.
2. Switch off «Enabled» and press «Save» on the card.
3. Tell those customers to use «Forgot your password?», or send them a link from their cards.

The button of the network disappears from the site. Its keys and the links of customers stay, so
switching it on again brings everything back.

## How to change the secret of a social network

1. Find the field that says «Set. Type a new value to replace it.»
2. Type the new value.
3. Press «Save» on the card.

The secret is never shown again, here or anywhere. «Forget» removes it at once. If no secret is left
at all, the network leaves the site.

## How to check that letters reach customers

1. Find the card «Letters» at the end of the page.
2. A plain note means the site has a way to send letters.
3. A warning means that nobody gets them: no confirmations, password links or invitations. Ask
   whoever maintains the site to connect a mail server: nothing on this page can fix it.

# fields

## registration

Customers who already have an account are not affected, those with a social network included, and
you can still add new ones by hand. A person with no account who comes through a social network is
told that registration is closed.

## require-first-name

«Required» holds up the registration until the field is filled in, and a customer who came through a
social network without it is asked on the page «Almost done». Customers who registered earlier are
asked only when they save their profile. The card in the panel does not change: the field stays
there and may be empty.

## require-last-name

It works the same way as the first name. With both hidden, new customers have no name at all: letters
greet them by the part of the address before «@», and the list of customers says «No name».

## require-phone

Google and Facebook do not give a phone, so with «Required» every new customer who comes through
them stops on the page «Almost done» to type one. With «Hidden», numbers saved earlier stay in the cards
in the panel, and customers no longer see them on the site.
