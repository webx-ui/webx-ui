# about

The card of one customer: what the site knows about them, their groups, how they sign in and, on the
tab «History», who changed what. There is no password here: nobody in the panel sees or sets it, and
the customer sets their own through a letter. Without the right to change customers the card opens
for reading only, with no buttons.

# howto

## How to add a customer by hand

1. In the list of customers, press «New customer».
2. Fill in «E-mail» and whatever else you know.
3. Leave «Send an invitation» ticked, unless the customer should not get a letter yet.
4. Press «Create».

The customer can sign in only after setting a password. The link in the invitation also confirms the
address, so nothing else is needed.

## How to help a customer who cannot sign in

1. Open the card and look at the mark beside the name. If it says «Blocked», the block is the reason.
2. Look at «Ways to sign in» in the card «Account». A network marked «Not shown» is switched off, and
   nobody gets in with it.
3. Press «Send a password link», or «Send the invitation again» if the customer has no password.
4. Ask the customer to follow the link and set a password.

By default a password link works for an hour, and an invitation for 7 days. Careful with an address
that is not confirmed: setting a password confirms it and unlinks every social network from the
account. If you know the address is the customer's own, choose «Confirm the address by hand» first,
and the networks stay.

## How to confirm a customer's address

1. Open the card with the mark «Not confirmed».
2. In the «···» menu at the top of the card, choose «Send the confirmation letter». By default the
   link in it works for 72 hours.
3. If the customer cannot get the letter and you know the address is theirs, choose «Confirm the
   address by hand» in the same menu.

Once confirmed, the site stops reminding the customer on every page.

## How to stop a customer signing in

1. Open the card.
2. In the «···» menu at the top of the card, choose «Block».
3. Confirm in the dialog.

Nothing is deleted. The customer is signed out on the next page and is told that the account is
blocked, with the password and with a social network alike, and password links are not sent. To let
the customer back in, press «Unblock».

## How to delete a customer

1. Open the card.
2. In the «···» menu at the top of the card, choose «Delete».
3. Read the dialog: it says what else will be lost, then confirm.

This cannot be undone. The customer gets a letter, and the same address can register again as a new
customer. If the site takes orders, they stay in the panel with their contacts, but no longer belong
to any account.

## How to unlink a social network

1. Find the network under «Ways to sign in» in the card «Account».
2. If «Unlink» is off, this is the last way to sign in: first let the customer set a password with
   the button at the top of the card.
3. Press «Unlink» and confirm.

The customer can link the network again in their account.

# fields

## first-name

Customers see it in their account, and letters greet them by first and last name. With no name at
all, a letter uses the part of the address before «@». The panel lets you leave it empty, whatever
the settings ask of the site's forms.

## last-name

Together with the first name it makes the name in the list of customers and in letters. Without
either, the list says «No name».

## email

An address that belongs to another customer is refused: one address, one account. The customer keeps
the password and signs in with the new address right away. A password link sent to the old address
stops working.

## phone

The panel takes the number as typed, but the site's forms accept only digits, spaces, brackets,
hyphens and a plus at the start, like +800 1234 5678. A number written any other way can stop the
customer from saving their profile until it is corrected.

## groups

A customer can be in several groups and never sees them on the site. A group changes nothing by
itself: it matters only where something on the site has been set up to read it. If there is nothing
to tick here, the site has no groups yet.
