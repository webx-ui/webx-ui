# about

Here you keep the magazines, papers and sites that wrote about you, each with the articles it ran.
The order of this list is the order of the outlets on the site: in the strip of logos and in the
cards. A visitor sees an outlet only in the languages it has an article to show in.

# howto

## How to add an outlet

1. Press «New outlet», the first line of the list.
2. Fill in the «Name» and choose the «Logo».
3. On the «Articles» tab add what the outlet printed.
4. Switch on «Published».
5. Press «Save». The outlet is created by this first save.

## How to change the order of the outlets

1. Clear the search: while words narrow the list, it cannot be dragged.
2. Drag the outlet to its new place.
3. The new order saves itself.

The strip of logos and the cards on the site follow at once.

## How to find out why an outlet is not on the site

1. Look at its row. A plain «Not published» means the «Published» switch is off.
2. A «Not published» in the warning colour means the switch is on, but no language has an article to
   show.
3. Open the outlet and go to the «Articles» tab.
4. Check that its articles have a «Title» and that «Do not show» is off.

The languages at the end of a row are the ones it has an article to show in.

## How to delete an outlet and bring it back

1. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
2. Confirm. The outlet goes to the bin with its articles and leaves the site and every block.
3. To bring it back, open «Bin», press «···» in its row and then «Restore».

Its links stop working while it is in the bin. A restored outlet comes back with its articles and
its address.
