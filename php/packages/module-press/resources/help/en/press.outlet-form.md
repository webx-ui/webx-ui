# about

An outlet is one magazine, paper or site that wrote about you: its logo, a few words about it and
the articles it ran. There is no draft here: what you save reaches the site at once. A visitor sees
the outlet only in the languages it has an article to show in.

# howto

## How to add an article

1. Open the «Articles» tab and press «Add».
2. Fill in the «Title» in the language it was printed in.
3. Fill in the «Link», choose a «PDF», or both.
4. Choose the «Kind» and set the «Date» with «Known to».
5. Press «Save».

If an article is refused, its card opens by itself with the error under the field, and nothing of
this save is written.

## How to show an outlet in another language

1. Switch the form to that language.
2. On the «Articles» tab write the «Title» of at least one article.
3. Add «About the outlet» in that language if you have the words.
4. Press «Save».

The page in that language appears by itself. With its own «Address» left empty, it takes the address
from another language.

## How to hide an article without losing it

1. Open its card on the «Articles» tab.
2. Switch on «Do not show».
3. Press «Save».

If that was the last article seen in a language, the outlet leaves the site in that language too:
its page there stops opening, and its blocks no longer show it.

## How to put an outlet in the strip of logos

1. Switch on «In the strip of logos».
2. Press «Save». A star appears in the row of the list.
3. Check that the logos block on the page is set to the marked outlets only.

The logos stand in the order of the list of outlets, so change that order to move one.

## How to take an outlet off the site

1. Switch off «Published».
2. Press «Save».

Its page stops opening and it leaves every block. The articles stay here, and switching it back on
returns everything.

# fields

## logo

The logo stands in the strip of logos, in the cards of outlets and at the top of the outlet's page,
where it takes the place of the name. In the strip all logos are held to one height, so a wide one
and a square one stand side by side. If the SEO tab has no picture of its own, it also goes to
social networks.

## title

A visitor reads it in the cards of outlets and beside articles in the feed. Write it the way the
outlet writes itself: it is a proper name, so one spelling is usually enough. While the SEO tab is
empty, it also names the page in search engines.

## slug

Change it on a live page only if you must: the old address keeps leading to the new one, but it is
one more step for every link already out there. A language has a page only while the outlet has an
article to show in it, however the address is filled in.

## website-url

On the outlet's page it becomes the link «Visit the website», opened in a new window. Without it
there is simply no such link. If the site has no pages for outlets, the logo leads here instead.

## summary

The same text stands in the cards of outlets. Keep it to a sentence or two; in a language it is not
written in, nothing is printed in its place. A description filled in on the SEO tab replaces it for
search engines.

## published

Switched off, the outlet's page stops opening and it leaves every block; nothing is lost and it can
be switched back on. Switched on with no article to show, the outlet is still not seen anywhere, and
the list warns about it.

## featured

Off, the outlet stays on the site: it only leaves a strip that is set to the marked outlets. A strip
set to every outlet, the cards and the outlet's own page do not look at this switch.

## articles

Drag the cards to change the order on the outlet's page: the date does not sort them there. Removing
a card deletes the article for good at the next save; to keep it, use «Do not show» instead.

## article-title

Write the headline as the outlet printed it. It is a link: it leads out to the article or the PDF,
in a new window. No other language stands in for a missing title, and an article without a title in
any language is not saved.

## article-excerpt

A sentence or two under the title, on the outlet's page and in the block of latest articles. In a
language it is not written in, the article is shown without it.

## article-kind

Printed beside the date: «Interview», «Mention» and the like. Blocks can group the outlets by kind
or show only some kinds; an article with no kind stays on the outlet's page but falls out of them.
The list of kinds is the same for the whole site.

## article-published-on

Printed beside the kind, in the language of the page. In the block of latest articles the date sets
the order, and articles without one come last. If you know only the month, pick any day of it and
set «Known to».

## article-date-precision

The day you did not know stays off the site, even though the date field needs one. Search engines
are given the date only when it is known to the day.

## article-url

It has to start with http:// or https://, or the save is refused. An article needs a «Link» or a
«PDF», otherwise it is not saved. With both, the title leads here and the PDF becomes a second link.

## article-file

With no «Link», the title leads to this file and is marked PDF. With a «Link», the file is a
separate link beside it. Only a PDF is accepted, and the file stays in the library if the article
goes.

## article-hidden

For an article you want to set aside without deleting it. It is not counted among the outlet's
articles, and it stays hidden in every language. If every article in a language is hidden, the
outlet leaves the site in that language.
