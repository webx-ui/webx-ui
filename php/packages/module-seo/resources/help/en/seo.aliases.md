# about

When a page is renamed or moved, the site keeps its old address and sends visitors on to the new one
by itself, so links and search results keep working. This list shows those old addresses and where
they lead today. There is nothing to fill in here: it is where to look when you are chasing an old
link.

# howto

## How to find where an old address leads

1. Type a part of the address into the search above the list.
2. Read «Destination»: the address the visitor lands on today.
3. Click it to open that page in a new tab.

## How to send an old address somewhere else

1. Open the «Redirects» tab and press «New redirect».
2. Type the old address into «Address». A warning says where it leads now.
3. Fill in «Destination» and press «New redirect» at the bottom of the window.

Your redirect is tried first, and the row here stays in the list. Do not point a redirect from the
page's present address back to the old one: the two will send visitors round in a circle.

# fields

## Leads nowhere

The page this address led to is gone, and a visitor gets «not found». If people still come by this
address, write a redirect for it on the «Redirects» tab.

## Language

This column is there on a site with several languages: each language keeps its old addresses
separately.
