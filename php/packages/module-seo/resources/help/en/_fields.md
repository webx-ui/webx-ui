# fields

## seo-fields

What search engines and social networks are told about this page; on the page itself a visitor sees
almost none of it. Whatever stays empty the site fills in from the page's own name, text and
picture, so most pages need nothing here.

- «The page»: «Title» is what the browser tab and search results show, with whatever the title
  template on the «SEO» tab of the settings adds, and neither the counter nor the preview counts
  that. «Description» is the text under it in search results, though a search engine may pick its
  own, and a long line is saved all the same. Nobody sees «Keywords», and the big search engines
  ignore them. «Heading» is not printed by the standard pages of these sections: they show their own
  title, so fill it in only if whoever looks after the site asks.
- «Sharing», if the site has this tab: what a link to the page looks like in a messenger or on a
  social network. Left empty, it repeats the title, the description and the page's picture. An SVG
  picture will not do, because networks cannot show one.
- «Advanced»: «Keep out of the index» under «Indexing» takes the page out of search and out of the
  sitemap, and it still opens by a link. «Canonical address» pointing at another page does the same:
  search shows that page instead, and the field holds one address for all the languages of the site.
  «Structured data» is for a ready block from whoever does your SEO: the site writes the usual one
  by itself.

The card goes live when the form is saved, even if the rest of the page waits in a draft for
publication. A rule for this address in the SEO section wins over the card in every field the rule
fills in.
