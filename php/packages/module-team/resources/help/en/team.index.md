# about

Everybody the site shows as its people: the list on one side, the open person on the other. A person
has no page of their own: visitors see them in a Team block on a page of the site. The order of this
list is one for the whole site, and every block follows it.

# howto

## How to add a person

1. Press «New person» at the top of the list.
2. Fill in «Name», «Job title» and «Text», and add a «Photo».
3. Switch on «Published».
4. Press «Save».

The person appears at once in every Team block.

## How to show the team on a page of the site

1. Open the page and add the Team block.
2. Pick how it looks: a grid, a slider or a list.
3. Save the page the way it is saved.

If the block is not in the list, ask a developer to install it: a new site may not have it yet.

## How to change the order of the team

1. Clear the search.
2. Drag a person by their grip. The new order saves itself.

## How to show on a service's page only the people who provide it

This works on a site with the «Services» section.

1. In the form of each person, fill in «Services» and press «Save».
2. Open the service and add the Team block on its «Content» tab.
3. In the block, under «Only related to “Services”», choose the section and switch on «The record of
   the page it stands on».
4. Press «Publish».

The block remembers «the service of this page», so a copy on another service shows that service's
people. On a page that is not a service, it shows nobody.

## How to delete a person and bring them back

1. In the menu of the person, press «Delete» and confirm.
2. To bring them back, open the «Bin» view and press «Restore» in their menu.

The person leaves every block on the site. Restored, they come back to their place.
