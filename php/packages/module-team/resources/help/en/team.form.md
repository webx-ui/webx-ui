# about

The form of one person on the team. There is no draft here: whatever you save on a published person
is on the site at once. A published person is shown in every language of the site, whatever is
translated.

# howto

## How to add social links

1. Under «Social networks», press «Add a link».
2. Choose the «Network» and fill in «Address».
3. Drag the rows into the order you want.
4. Press «Save».

## How to translate a person

1. Switch the form to the language.
2. Fill in «Text», and «Job title» if it reads differently there.
3. Press «Save».

# fields

## photo

It stands on the card in a circle, so keep the face in the middle of the picture. Use only a picture
the person agreed to show.

## name

Without it in the default language the form will not save. Visitors see it on the card, and the
initials are made from it when there is no photo.

## job-title

Translate it only where it reads differently. Left empty, it leaves no gap on the card.

## text

A few lines about the person on the card; the line breaks are kept. The block has a setting that
hides the text of everybody at once.

## socials

Links stand on the card as icons and open in a new tab. Up to 20 links per person.

## social-network

The list is the site's own; a developer adds a network that is missing. Seven usual networks get an
icon, and any other is printed as its name.

## social-url

The full address of the profile, starting with http:// or https://: nothing else is accepted. A row
with only a network or only an address will not save. Visitors see the icon, not the address.

## services

The field exists on sites with the «Services» section. The card of the person links to these
services.

## published

Switch it off to take the person off every page at once: they stay in the panel with the mark «Not
published». There is no draft, so edits to a published person are not previewed anywhere first.
