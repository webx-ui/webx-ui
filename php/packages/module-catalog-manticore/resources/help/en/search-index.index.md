# about

A separate fast store that the site's catalogue searches, filters and counts in. Products reach it by
themselves after saving, so usually there is nothing to do here. Open this page when search or filters
on the site show something out of date.

# howto

## How to check that the index is healthy

1. Look at «Server»: it should say «Answers».
2. Look at «Tables»: the state of each language should be «Up to date», and the numbers «In the index»
   and «In the database» should be close.
3. Look at «Waiting to be written»: the number should fall by itself.

## How to rebuild the index

1. Press «Rebuild» and confirm.
2. Wait: the page shows how far it has got.

Buyers can keep using the site meanwhile, because it works from the old data until the new data is
ready. A rebuild is needed when the page says the index is out of date.
