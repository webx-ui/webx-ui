# about

Here you decide which products the collection holds and how the site shows them. Products can be
picked by hand or follow a filter, so the block stays fresh by itself. The preview on the «Products»
tab shows what will reach the site.

# howto

## How to make a collection that fills itself

1. On «Main», set «Filled» to «By a filter».
2. On the «Products» tab, choose a ready-made variant or set your own conditions.
3. Check the preview on the right and press «Save».

New products that fit appear in the collection by themselves.

## How to find out why a product is not shown

1. Open the «Products» tab.
2. Look at «Will not reach the site» under the preview.

Each product there has its reason: over the limit, not published, cannot be bought.

# fields

## title

Buyers see it as the heading of the block, so it should say what the products have in common: «New
arrivals», «For winter».

## key

Blocks, texts and templates find the collection by this word, which is why it stays the same.
Buyers do not see it.

Type it any way you like: «Summer tyres» becomes `summer-tyres`.

## source

«By hand» gives you a fixed list in your order, which needs care when products sell out. «By a filter»
follows the catalogue by itself, and you do not choose the order product by product.

## limit

Keep it to what the page has room for: a carousel on the home page rarely needs more than a dozen.
Products over the limit do not reach the site.

## view

A grid shows all the products at once and takes more of the page. A carousel fits a long row into one
line, and buyers scroll through it.

## link

Lead it to the page where buyers can see more: a category or a landing. Without a link the block
simply ends after the last product.

It can also be «A file» of the media library — the catalogue in PDF; tick «Download instead of
opening» to have it saved under the name it was uploaded with.

## link-text

Say where the link goes when the block is about something specific: «All winter tyres» works better
than a general phrase.

## is-enabled

Use it to take a seasonal block away without deleting it: the places keep their settings, and
switching it on brings it back everywhere.

## only-purchasable

Keeps buyers from meeting a product they cannot order. Switch it off when the block should also show
what is out of stock: a showcase of a new line, for example.

## products

Buyers see exactly these products. One that goes off sale stays in the list here, and the preview
tells you it no longer reaches the site.

## category

Narrows the collection to one branch: the category brings everything under it. Use it to make, say, a
block of new arrivals for one department.

## set

The same choices buyers have in the catalogue filter: brand, price, properties. Products must fit
all of them. If a value is deleted later, the collection is marked «Needs attention».

## on-sale

Handy for a block of offers: it follows prices by itself, and a product leaves the block when its
discount ends.

## sort

Decides who gets into the block when more products fit than the limit allows, and in what order
buyers see them. For new arrivals, choose the newest first.
