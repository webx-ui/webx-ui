# about

A collection is a named block of products: «New arrivals», «Our picks». You fill it once, and the site
shows it wherever you place it: on a page, in a text or in a template. It has no address of its own.

# howto

## How to make a collection and put it on a page

1. Press «New collection», fill in the title and save.
2. Choose how it is «Filled» and pick the products on the «Products» tab.
3. Add the block «Product collection» to a page and choose this collection.

## How to find out where a collection stands

1. Look at «Where» in its row.

The column counts blocks and regions. Insertions in a text and tags in templates are not counted, so
a collection with no places may still be in use.

## How to bring back a deleted collection

1. Open «Bin» over the list.
2. Press «Restore».

It comes back in every place it stood. «Delete for good» cannot be undone: those places stay empty.
