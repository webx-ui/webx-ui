# about

Every agent anybody let into this panel through a browser, with who let it in, what it may do and
the button that ends it. Each person also sees their own on the page «Connect an agent». Visitors to
the site see nothing of this.

# howto

## How to cut an agent off

1. Find it by «Client» and the line under it, «Answers go to…».
2. Press «Disconnect» and confirm.

It loses access at once, and it cannot refresh its way back. What it already changed stays changed:
disconnecting undoes nothing. The row stays in the list, greyed, because the agent calls point at
it.

Nothing stops the person from connecting it again. To keep it out for good, change their roles or
untick «Active» on their account.

## How to change what a connected agent may do

1. Press «Disconnect» and confirm.
2. Ask the person to connect the agent again from their program.
3. On the screen that asks for permission, they set «Read only» the way it should be and press
   «Allow».

The row comes back with the new terms.

# fields

## Client

The name is the program's own choice and proves nothing. The line under it says where answers go,
and that part cannot lie: if you do not recognise the address, disconnect first and ask afterwards.

## What is allowed

«Reading only» refuses every change, whatever the person may do. «Everything … can do» follows the
person's roles from call to call, so taking a role away narrows the agent at once.

## Last used

Accurate to the minute. An agent left unused for a month has to be connected again, and until then
its row looks the same as any other.
