# about

Here you connect an AI agent, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, so that it works in this panel as
you. It can do only what you can do, everything it does is written down under your name, and what it
changes on the site is what visitors will see. Any administrator may open this page: it asks for no
permission of its own.

# howto

## How to connect an agent

1. Press «Copy the address». The address is no secret: it opens nothing by itself.
2. Under «How to add it», open the tab of your program and follow the steps. For Cursor and VS Code,
   the buttons under «One click» do it for you.
3. Your program opens this site in a browser. Sign in with your usual password, which the program
   never sees.
4. Read who is asking and where the answers go. For a start, turn on «Read only».
5. Press «Allow». The agent appears under «Your connections».

If the panel says it cannot connect the program, ask whoever looks after the site: only programs
from an approved list of addresses are let in. If you are a super administrator, your agent can do
everything too, so connect it from an account with a role.

## How to disconnect your agent

1. Find it under «Your connections».
2. Press «Disconnect» and confirm.

It loses access at once, and what it already changed stays changed. Connecting it again takes the
same few clicks. An agent you have not used for a month asks you to connect it again by itself.

## How to let an agent write after starting with read only

1. Press «Disconnect» beside it and confirm.
2. Connect it again from your program.
3. Leave «Read only» off this time and press «Allow».

## How to give a program a key of your own

1. Under «Your keys», press «Issue a key».
2. Fill in the form and press «Issue».
3. Copy the key at once: it is shown only this time.

A key is for a program that runs with nobody at the screen. If you are the one sitting in front of
the agent, connect it instead.

# fields

## The address of this panel for agents

The same for everybody and safe to read out on a call or paste into a chat: an agent still has to be
let in by somebody with an account.

## Your connections

Only your own agents. A greyed row is one that was disconnected, and it stays for the record.

## Your keys

Only your own keys. A key cannot do more than you may, and it stops if your account is switched off
or deleted.
