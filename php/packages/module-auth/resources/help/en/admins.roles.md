# about

A role is a named list of permissions: what a person may open and change in the panel. A person may
hold several, and what they give adds up. Visitors to the site see nothing of this.

# howto

## How to make a role

1. Press «Add».
2. Fill in «Name». «Code» may stay empty.
3. Under «Permissions», tick what the role allows. A tick on the name of a section takes all of its
   permissions.
4. Press «Save».
5. On the «Administrators» tab, give the role to people: until then nobody holds it.

## How to change what a role allows

1. Click the row of the role.
2. Tick or untick permissions and press «Save».

Everybody who holds the role gains or loses at once, and their agents and keys with them.

## How to delete a role

1. Press «Delete» in the «···» menu of the row.
2. Read the window: it names everybody who would lose the role.
3. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

The people stay, without what this role gave them. A ready-made role that was deleted does not come
back by itself.

# fields

## Name

What the role is called in this list and in the form of an administrator. A new name changes nothing
about who may do what.

## Code

Agents and programs name a role by its code, so a role in use is better left with the one it has.
Its people and permissions stay the same either way.

## Permissions

A permission you do not hold yourself is greyed out: nobody hands out more than they have. A section
installed later is added to the ready-made roles by itself, a permission you took away stays away,
and a role you made yourself gets nothing new until you tick it.

## Yours

You hold this role, so it opens read-only and has no «Delete»: nobody edits what they hold. Ask
another person who may edit roles, or a super administrator.

## From modules not installed here

Permissions of a section taken off this site. They open nothing now. Untick them if the section is
not coming back.
