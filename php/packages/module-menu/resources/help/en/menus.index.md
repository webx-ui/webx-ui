# about

The menus of the site on the left, the items of the open one on the right. There are no drafts here:
everything you save, drag or delete reaches the site at once. A menu shows on the site where a
template of the site prints it, so a new menu stays invisible until that is done.

# howto

## How to add an item to a menu

1. Choose the menu on the left and press «New item».
2. In «Where it goes», choose something on this site, type an address, or choose «Nowhere».
3. Fill in «Label» if the item should be called differently.
4. Press «Save». The item is on the site at once.

## How to change the order of the items

1. Drag the item to the place it should take.
2. To put it under another item, drop it inside that item.
3. Check the menu on the site: the new order is saved by itself.

## How to hide an item without deleting it

1. Open the item.
2. Switch off «Shown on the site» and press «Save».

The item is marked «Hidden» in the list and leaves the site along with everything under it. Switch
it back on to return it as it was.

## How to show an item in one language only

1. Open the item.
2. In «Languages», choose the languages it belongs to.
3. Press «Save».

The other languages of the site simply do not get the item, or anything under it.

## How to create a menu of your own

1. Press «New menu».
2. Fill in «Name» and «Key» and press «Create».
3. Add the items.
4. Ask whoever maintains the site to print this menu by its key.

A menu marked «A template of this site asks for it» cannot be deleted, and its key cannot be
changed: it can only be renamed or emptied. Deleting your own menu takes all its items, but not what
they point at.

## What to do if a change has not reached the site

1. Open the site in a fresh tab and check again.
2. In the ··· of that menu, press «Reset the cache of this menu».
3. Reload the page on the site.

The site keeps a ready copy of each menu so as not to assemble it for every visitor. A save usually
refreshes it by itself; the button is for the rare case when it did not. «Reset every menu» in the
··· at the top does the same for all of them.

# fields

## Where it goes

Pick a page or anything else on this site rather than typing its address: the item then follows it
through renaming and moving. «An address» is for what the list does not have, such as another site;
write a path of this site without the language part. «Nowhere» makes a label with no link.

## Label

Worth filling in only when the item should differ from the name of what it points at, for example a
short «Blog» for a long title. An empty label follows that name in every language it has, including
when it changes. An item with no words in a language is left out of that language.

## Look

How the site draws this item, such as a link or a button. The choice comes from the site's templates
and is offered only where there is more than one.

## Languages

Use it for what exists in one language only, such as a link to a section that was not translated.
Other languages get neither the item nor what stands under it.

## A heading for the items under it

The site draws the item as the title of its group rather than as an ordinary item. What exactly
changes depends on the template of the menu.

## Shown on the site

Off, the item and all the items under it leave the site, and everything filled in here stays.

## Open in a new tab

Good for links to other sites, so the visitor does not lose yours. For pages of your own site it
usually gets in the way.

## Relationship

Notes for search engines, not visible to visitors. «Pass no link weight» is for links you do not
vouch for, «Paid placement» for advertising, «Written by a visitor» for links from comments and
reviews.

## Not on the site

What the item points at is a draft or has been taken off the site. Visitors do not see the item, or
what stands under it, until that is published: the menu can be built in advance.

## What this pointed at is gone

The page or material was deleted, and the item no longer shows on the site. Point the item somewhere
else or delete it.
