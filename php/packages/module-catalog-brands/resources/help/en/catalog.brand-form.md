# about

A brand has a page of its own, where buyers see its logo, its description and all its products. It is
also a choice in the catalogue filter. Here you fill in that page; a product gets the brand in its
own form.

# howto

## How to take a brand off the site but keep its products

1. Switch off «Published» and press «Save».

The products stay on sale, only without the brand. Switch it on again to bring the page back.

## How to show a brand among the popular ones

1. Switch on «Featured» and press «Save».
2. Check the order in the list of brands: it decides who comes first.

The place where popular brands appear depends on the design of your site.

# fields

## title

Buyers see this name on the brand page, in the filter and on products. Write it the way the brand
spells it, because people search for those exact words.

## slug

Changing it moves the brand page and the filter links with this brand. The old address leads to the
new one, but do not change it without need: search engines take time to catch up.

## logo

Shown at the top of the brand page and in the list of brands. A logo on a transparent background
looks right in both.

## is-visible

Use it for a brand you stopped working with but whose products still sell. Nothing is lost: the
products remember their brand, and switching it on brings everything back.

## is-featured

A mark for the design of your site: nothing changes unless a page shows popular brands. Keep it for a
handful of brands buyers recognise.

## description

A page with nothing but a list tells search engines little about itself, so a few sentences about the
brand help it be found. Leave it empty if you have nothing to add: buyers will see only the logo and
the products.

## seo

How the brand page looks in search results and when a link to it is shared. Left empty, the name, the
description and the logo are used.
