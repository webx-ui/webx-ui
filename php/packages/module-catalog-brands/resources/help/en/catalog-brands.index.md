# about

Every brand has a page of its own on the site with its products, and it is a choice in the catalogue
filter. Here you add brands, decide which are published and set their order. A product gets its brand
in its own form.

# howto

## How to add a brand

1. Press «New brand», fill in the name and save.
2. Add a logo and a description in the form that opens.
3. Choose the brand in «Brand» in the forms of its products.

## How to change the order of brands

1. Drag the brands in the list.

The filter, the popular brands and the list of brands on the site follow this order.

## How to delete a brand

1. Press «Show its products» and take the brand off them.
2. Delete the brand. It goes to the bin, and its page tells visitors it is gone.

A brand that products belong to cannot be deleted. To hide it, switch off «Published» in its form.
