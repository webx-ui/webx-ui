# about

The whole catalogue of services on one screen. A service is a page of the site made of blocks: it
sits in one or several categories and reaches visitors only once it is published. The list has an
order of its own, and each category has its own too.

# howto

## How to add a service

1. Press «New service».
2. Fill in «Title» and press «Create».
3. On the «Content» tab, build the page out of blocks.
4. On the «Settings» tab, choose «Categories» and fill in «Announcement» and «Cover».
5. Press «Publish».

Until then, the service is a draft and visitors cannot open it.

## How to change the order of services

1. Clear the search and stay on the «All» view.
2. Leave «Category» empty to change the order of the whole list, or pick a category to change the
   order inside it.
3. Drag a service by its grip. The new order saves itself.

The two orders do not touch each other. A service newly put into a category takes its place there by
the whole list.

## How to take a service off the site for a while

1. Open the menu of the service in the list.
2. Press «Take off the site».

Its page stops opening and the service moves to the «Off the site» view with everything written.
«Publish» brings it back at the same address.

## How to delete a service and bring it back

1. In the menu of the service, press «Delete» and confirm.
2. To bring it back, open the «Bin» view and press «Restore» in its menu.

The service leaves the site and its address becomes free. Restored, it gets the address back; if
somebody has taken it in the meantime, the panel refuses, and the address has to be freed first.
