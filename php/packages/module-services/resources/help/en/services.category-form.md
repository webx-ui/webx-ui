# about

A category is a page of the site: a heading, an introduction and the services in this category's own
order. It has no draft: everything reaches the site as soon as you press «Save».

# howto

## How to hide a category from the site

1. Switch off «Shown on the site».
2. Press «Save».

Services that have no other visible category move to the end of the catalogue, under «Other
services». Everything written stays here, and switching it on brings the page back.

## How to change the address of a category

1. Change «Address» in every language it should change in.
2. Press «Save».

The old address goes on working and leads to the new one. The addresses of services do not change:
the category is not part of them.

# fields

## title

Visitors see it as the heading of the page, in the catalogue and in the breadcrumbs. With an empty
SEO card, the title for search engines is made from it too. Changing it leaves the address alone.

## slug

The last part of the address of the page; the addresses of its services do not depend on it. A
category and a service cannot share an address, so one that is taken will not be saved. With the
address empty in a language, the category has no page in that language.

## is-visible

Switch it off to take a section away for a while: everything written stays in the panel. The
category also stops appearing in the breadcrumbs of its services.

## lead

The first thing visitors read: it stands right under the heading of the page. With an empty SEO
card, the description for search engines comes from it too.

## cover

The standard catalogue lists categories as headings without pictures, so on a site without its own
template visitors meet the cover only in links shared in social networks. The page of the category
does not print it.

## blocks

These blocks stand on the page of the category, between the introduction and the list of services:
the place for a longer story about the section. Like everything here, they reach the site on «Save».
