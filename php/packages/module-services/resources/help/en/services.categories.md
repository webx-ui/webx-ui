# about

Categories are the sections of the catalogue: each has a page of its own on the site, and a service
can be in several at once. The order of this list is the order of the catalogue on the site. A row
says how many services the category holds.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category».
2. Fill in «Title» and press «Create».
3. On the page that opens, fill in «Introduction» and «Cover» and press «Save».
4. Put services into it with the «Categories» field of each service.

## How to change the order of categories

1. Drag a category by its grip.
2. The new order saves itself and is on the site at once.

## How to change the order of services inside a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its services».
2. In the list that opens, drag the services by their grips.

Only this category changes: the whole list and the other categories keep their order.

## How to delete a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its services» and take the category off each of them in
   their forms.
2. Back here, press «Delete» in its menu and confirm.

A category with services in it cannot be deleted. Its page leaves the site, and this section has no
way to bring a deleted category back, so if it may be needed again, switch off «Shown on the site»
instead.
