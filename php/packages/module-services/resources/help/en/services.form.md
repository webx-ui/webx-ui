# about

The page of one service: its blocks on «Content», the title, address, announcement, cover and
categories on «Settings», then the SEO card and the history of publications. What you write waits in
a draft, and the site goes on showing the published version until you press «Publish». The
categories and the SEO card are the exception: they take effect as soon as they are saved.

# howto

## How to edit a published service

1. Make the changes. They save themselves after a short pause; «Save» does it at once.
2. Press «Preview» and look at the draft.
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

While the mark «edits» stands beside the state, visitors still see the previous version.

## How to throw away the edits

1. In the «···» menu of the head, press «Discard changes».
2. Confirm.

The service goes back to the published version. The categories and the SEO card stay as they were
saved.

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab.
2. Press «Restore» beside the version and confirm.
3. Check the draft and press «Publish».

The version only becomes the draft: the site changes after the publication.

## How to open a service in another language

1. Switch the form to that language.
2. On the «Settings» tab, fill in «Title» and «Address».
3. Press «Publish».

A new service gets an address only in the language it was created in, and without an address in a
language it has no page there.

# fields

## blocks

The page of the service is made of these blocks: visitors see them under the title, in this order.
Changes go into the draft and reach the site with the publication.

## title

Visitors see it as the heading of the page, on cards and in the breadcrumbs. With an empty SEO card,
the title for search engines is made from it too. Changing it leaves the address alone.

## slug

The last part of the page address. A new address starts working with the publication and the old one
leads to it, so links already shared do not break. With the address empty in a language, the service
has no page in that language.

## lead

Visitors read it under the title on the card, in the catalogue and in the blocks of services. The
page of the service itself does not print it.

## cover

The picture on the card in the catalogue and in the blocks of services, and in social networks when
the SEO card has none. The page of the service does not print it: a picture there is a block.

## categories

The service is listed on the page of each category chosen here. This field does not wait for the
publication: the change reaches the site when it is saved. To make another category the main one,
drag it to the first place.
