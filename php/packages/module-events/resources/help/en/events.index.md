# about

Every workshop, meeting and webinar of the site, each with a page of its own. Visitors see in the
lists only what is still to come; an event that is over moves to «Past» by itself and keeps its
page. Nothing reaches the site until it is published.

# howto

## How to add an event

1. Press «New event», write the «Title» and press «Create».
2. In the editor that opens, fill in the date, the place and the price on the «Event» tab.
3. On the «Settings» tab, check the «Address» and choose the «Categories».
4. Press «Publish» and confirm.

Until then the event is a «Draft», and visitors do not see it.

## How to run the same event again

1. Open the menu of the row and press «Duplicate». The copy opens as a draft.
2. Set its new dates.
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

The copy keeps every field, the categories and the services, and its address gets a number at the
end. There are no series here: each date is an event of its own, so the last one is the best start
for the next.

## How to find an event

1. Choose the tab: «Upcoming», «Past», «All» or «Bin».
2. Type part of the title or the address into the search.
3. To narrow it further, open «Filters» and choose a «State» or a «Category».

An event with no date stays on «Upcoming» for good, at the top of the list.

## How to take an event off the site

1. To hide it for now, press «Take off the site» in the menu of the row. Its page answers «not
   found»; «Publish» brings it back at the same address.
2. To remove it, press «Delete» and confirm. It goes to the bin, and its address can be taken by
   something else.
3. If you change your mind, open the «Bin» tab and press «Restore» in the menu of the row. If the
   address has been taken in the meantime, the event cannot come back.

An event that is over needs neither: its page stays with the line «This event is over», and with its
photos it works as a report.
