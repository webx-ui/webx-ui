# about

The page of one event: when it is, where, how much it costs and what to expect. What you write is
saved as a draft by itself, and visitors see it only after «Publish».

# howto

## How to put your changes on the site

1. Make the changes. They are saved a moment after you stop typing, or press «Save».
2. Press «Publish».
3. Read the address in the question and confirm.

Until then visitors see the published version, and the badge «edits» says something is waiting. To
drop the changes, open the menu at the top of the editor and press «Discard changes»: they cannot be
brought back.

## How to set an event of several days

1. Turn on «All day».
2. Pick the «First day» and the «Last day».
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

Visitors see only the dates. The same happens with hours, if «Starts» and «Ends» fall on different
days: an event of several days never prints its time.

## How to announce an event with no date yet

1. Leave the date fields empty.
2. In «Date in words», write what you know: «Dates to be announced», «Every Saturday».
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

Such an event stands first among the upcoming ones and never goes into the past. It has no «Add to
calendar» button and search engines get no event details, because they need a date.

## How to make an online event

1. In «Attendance», choose «Online». The place fields disappear.
2. Fill in the «Link to book», if people have to sign up.
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

The page says «Online» in place of the venue. There is no field for the link to the stream: it would
be public, so send it to the people who signed up.

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab.
2. Press «Restore» next to the version you want and confirm. It becomes the draft.
3. Check the fields and press «Publish».

# fields

## all-day

Turn it on for an event without hours, such as a fair: the fields below it then ask for days. The
event counts as going on until the end of its last day.

## starts-at

You pick the time on your device's clock, and the page prints it on the site's clock: if you work
from another time zone, the hour printed differs. Empty means no date at all: the event stays
upcoming and has no «Add to calendar» button.

## starts-on

The upcoming list goes by this day, nearest first. Empty means no date at all: the event stays
upcoming and has no «Add to calendar» button.

## ends-at

With an end on the same day, visitors see the hours as a range: 10:00–12:30. The event stays
upcoming and keeps its «Book» button until this moment. An end needs a start, and it cannot come
before it.

## ends-on

The event stays upcoming and keeps its «Book» button until this day is over. Visitors see the dates
as a range. It cannot come before the first day.

## date-note

For a date a calendar cannot say: «Every Saturday», «Dates to be announced». The words stand in the
lists and on the page in the place of the date, in each language as you write them there.

## attendance

Decides what is printed under «Where». «In person» and «Both» print the place, and only «Online»
prints that word instead. Visitors cannot tell «Both» from «In person», so say in the description
that one can join online.

## venue

The name of the place. Visitors see it on the event's page and on its card in the lists, where the
address is not printed.

## address

Printed under the venue on the event's page, and goes into the calendar file a visitor adds. It is
not on the card in the lists.

## map-url

Becomes the link «Map» next to the place, and opens in a new tab. It shows only if the venue or the
address is filled in. The link has to start with http:// or https://.

## price

Words of your own in each language: «Free», «On request», «480 per person». Visitors see them under
«Price» on the event's page; with the field empty, that line is not there.

## price-amount

Visitors do not see it: the page prints the words from «Price». Without a currency set for the site,
the number does not reach search engines, except that 0 still marks the event as free.

## booking-url

Becomes the button «Book», which opens in a new tab: a form, a ticket office, a messenger. The link
has to start with http:// or https://.

## gallery

The order here is the order on the page: the first photo large, the others as a strip under it. With
no photos the event's card and page have no picture.

## description

The main text, printed under the «Book» button. The date, the place and the price stand above it by
themselves, so there is no need to repeat them here.

## highlights

Printed under the description, up to 12 cards, in a block with this title. With no cards the block
is not there. The cards are the same in every language; only their text is written separately.

## highlight-title

The heading of the card, a few words: «Bring nothing», «Small group». A card with only a heading is
shown too.

## highlight-text

The line under the heading. A card with only this text is shown too, without a heading.

## title

The heading of the page, and the name in the lists, the breadcrumbs and the calendar file. Changing
it does not change the address.

## slug

Made from the title when the event is created, and does not follow it later. A new address starts
working with «Publish», and the old one then leads to it. A language with no address has no page.

## lead

The sentence that makes someone read on: visitors see it under the heading on the event's page. It
also becomes the description in the calendar file.

## categories

The event appears on the page of each category chosen; drag to put the main one first. A hidden
category does not show. A change reaches the site with «Publish», like everything else.

## services

Printed as cards at the end of the page, in the order you choose. A service that is not on the site
is left out by itself.
