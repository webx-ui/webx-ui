# about

A category is a kind of event the site runs, with a page of its own that lists what is coming.
Unlike an event, it has no draft: what you save is on the site at once. An event can be in several
categories.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category», write the «Title» and press «Create».
2. On the page that opens, fill in the rest and press «Save».
3. Open an event, choose the category on its «Settings» tab and press «Publish».

Its address is made from the title, and it can be changed on the same page.

## How to change the order of the categories

1. Drag a category to its new place.
2. The order saves by itself.
3. Open the events page of the site and check the links at the top.

## How to delete a category

1. Look at the number in the row. If it holds events, press «Show its events» in the menu of the row
   and move them to other categories.
2. Press «Delete» in the menu of the row.
3. Confirm.

Its page comes off the site, and its events stay. This list has no bin, so the category cannot be
brought back from here. To take it off the site for a time, turn off «Shown on the site» in it
instead.
