# about

The page of one kind of event: its heading, picture and introduction above the events to come. There
is no draft here: «Save» puts everything on the site at once.

# howto

## How to hide a category for a while

1. Turn off «Shown on the site».
2. Press «Save».
3. Open the events page of the site and check that its link has gone.

Turn it back on and save to return it, at the same address.

## How to change the address of a category

1. Change the «Address». A line under the field says that the old one will keep working.
2. Press «Save».
3. Open the old address and check that it leads to the new one.

# fields

## title

The heading of the category's page, and its name in the links of the events page, on the event's
page and in the breadcrumbs. After «Save» it changes in all of them at once.

## slug

Made from the title when the category is created. The old address keeps working and leads to the new
one, starting from «Save». It stands on one level with the events, so an address an event has is
refused.

## is-visible

The link to it also goes from the events page and the events' pages, and it drops out of their
breadcrumbs. It works at once after «Save», and old links to the category then lead nowhere.

## lead

A few lines about this kind of event, with formatting. Visitors see them only on the category's
page; if it is empty, the list of events follows the heading directly.

## cover

Printed large under the heading. It does not appear on the events page or on the events' cards.
