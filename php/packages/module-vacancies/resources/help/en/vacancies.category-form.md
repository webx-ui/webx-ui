# about

One group of the careers page and its link in the filter. There is no draft here: «Save» puts
everything on the site at once.

# howto

## How to hide a group for a while

1. Turn off «Shown on the site».
2. Press «Save».
3. Open the careers page of the site and check the groups.

Vacancies that were only in this group move to «Other vacancies». Turn it back on and save to return
the group.

# fields

## title

The heading of the group, its link in the filter and its name on the vacancy's page. After «Save» it
changes in all of them at once.

## slug

Made from the title when the category is created, and each language has its own. Change it with
care: old links to the filter still open the careers page, but with no vacancies in the list.
Another category of vacancies cannot have the same key.

## is-visible

A vacancy in no other visible category moves to the last group, «Other vacancies». It works at once
after «Save», and old links to this filter show an empty list.
