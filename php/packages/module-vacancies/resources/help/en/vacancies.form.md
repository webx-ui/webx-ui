# about

The page of one vacancy: where the work is, the terms, the pay and what the job is about. What you
write is saved as a draft by itself, and visitors see it only after «Publish».

# howto

## How to put your changes on the site

1. Make the changes. They are saved a moment after you stop typing, or press «Save».
2. Press «Publish».
3. Read the address in the question and confirm.

Until then visitors see the published version, and the badge «edits» says something is waiting. To
drop the changes, open the menu at the top of the editor and press «Discard changes»: they cannot be
brought back.

## How to show the salary

1. To print your own words, fill in «Salary»: the page and the card show them.
2. For search engines, fill in «From» or «To», or both.
3. Choose «Per» and «Currency».
4. Press «Publish» and confirm.

With the figures and no words, the page prints a line made of the figures, and the card prints no
salary.

## How to close a vacancy by a date

1. On the «Settings» tab, pick the day in «Open until».
2. Press «Publish» and confirm.
3. Open the vacancy on the site and check the line «Open until».

There is nothing else to do on that day: the vacancy leaves the lists by itself, and its page stays,
marked as closed.

## How to get into job search

1. Check that the settings of the site name the organisation or the project: without a name search
   engines get nothing about any vacancy.
2. Fill in «Country», the «Employment» and the salary figures.
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

Only an open vacancy is described for search engines; a closed one drops out.

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab.
2. Press «Restore» next to the version you want and confirm. It becomes the draft.
3. Check the fields and press «Publish».

# fields

## workplace

Decides what is printed under «Where». «On site» prints the city and the address, «Remote» prints
that word and hides both fields, and «Hybrid» prints the city and «remote work possible».

## city

Visitors see it under «Where» on the page and on the card in the careers page. Search engines use it
as the place of the job.

## address

Printed after the city, on the vacancy's page only: the card does not show it.

## country

Visitors do not see it. A new vacancy gets the site's country, if one is set. For remote work it
tells search engines where the candidates may live.

## employment-types

You can tick several. Visitors see them in words on the page and on the card; with none ticked,
there is no such line. Search engines read them too.

## salary

Visitors see these words on the page and on the card, and they win over the figures below. Each
language has its own, so leave none empty if the salary matters to you.

## salary-min

The figures work only with «Per» and «Currency» chosen, and one of «From» and «To» is enough. A
figure without «Per» is refused on saving.

## salary-max

With only this figure, the line says «up to». It cannot be below «From», and such a save is refused.

## salary-unit

Printed at the end of the line made of the figures: «per month». It is required as soon as a figure
is filled in, and search engines need it too.

## salary-currency

The list is set up for the site, and a new vacancy gets the first currency of it. Without a currency
the figures are not used. A currency taken out of the list stays on the vacancies that have it.

## description

The main text, printed above the duties. Search engines get it together with the three lists, so do
not repeat those here.

## duties

Printed as a list under the heading «Duties», up to 30 lines. With no lines the section is not
there. Drag to set the order, which is the same in every language.

## duty-text

One task in a line, up to 500 characters. Visitors see it as it is, without formatting.

## requirements

Printed as a list under the heading «Requirements», up to 30 lines. With no lines the section is not
there. Drag to set the order, which is the same in every language.

## requirement-text

One requirement in a line, up to 500 characters. Visitors see it as it is, without formatting.

## benefits

Printed as a list under the heading «What we offer», up to 30 lines. With no lines the section is
not there. Drag to set the order, which is the same in every language.

## benefit-text

One advantage in a line, up to 500 characters. Visitors see it as it is, without formatting.

## title

The heading of the page, and the name on the card and in search results. Changing it does not change
the address.

## slug

Made from the position when the vacancy is created, and does not follow it later. A new address
starts working with «Publish», and the old one then leads to it, so links from job boards stay
alive. An address taken by another vacancy or a page is refused.

## lead

Visitors also see it under the heading on the vacancy's page, so write the one thing that should
make a candidate read on.

## categories

A vacancy stands in each group chosen, and its names are printed on its page without links. With no
visible category it goes to the last group, «Other vacancies». The order of the groups is set in the
«Categories» section.

## form

Choosing it does not put the form on the page by itself: the template of the site has to print it,
so open the vacancy on the site and check. A closed vacancy shows no form, and neither does one
whose form is switched off.

## is-closed

Like everything here, it reaches the site only with «Publish»; «Close the hiring» in the list does
it at once. The application form goes too, and search engines stop getting the vacancy.

## valid-through

Visitors see this day on the vacancy's page. It cannot come before «Posted on». With the field empty
the vacancy stays open until you close it.

## posted-at

Visitors do not see it; search engines read it as the date of the vacancy. Later publications leave
it alone, so when the hiring opens again, change it by hand.
