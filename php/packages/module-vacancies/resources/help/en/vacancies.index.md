# about

Everyone the organisation is looking for. The careers page of the site lists only the open
vacancies, in groups by category and in the order you set here. A closed vacancy leaves the lists
and keeps its page, so links from job boards still lead somewhere.

# howto

## How to add a vacancy

1. Press «New vacancy», write the «Position» and press «Create».
2. In the editor that opens, fill in where, the terms and the description on the «Vacancy» tab.
3. On the «Settings» tab, check the «Address» and choose the «Categories».
4. Press «Publish» and confirm.

Until then the vacancy is a «Draft», and visitors do not see it.

## How to change the order on the site

1. Open the «Open» or the «All» tab, and clear the search and the filters.
2. Drag a vacancy by its handle.
3. The order saves by itself.

A vacancy has one place, the same in every group of the careers page.

## How to close the hiring

1. In the menu of the row, press «Close the hiring».
2. Confirm.
3. Open the vacancy on the site and check the mark.

It works at once, without «Publish». The menu offers it only for a vacancy on the site. If it has
edits waiting, publish or discard them first, or closing would put them on the site too.

## How to open the hiring again

1. For a vacancy marked «Closed», open the «Closed» tab, press «Reopen the hiring» in the menu of
   the row and confirm. That is all: it works at once.
2. For one marked «Expired», open it and set a new «Open until» on the «Settings» tab.
3. Then press «Publish» and confirm.

Check «Posted on» as well: it stays the same unless you change it.

## How to add the same position in another place

1. In the menu of the row, press «Duplicate». The copy opens as a draft.
2. Change what differs, such as the «City».
3. Press «Publish» and confirm.

The copy stands right after the original, is open, and gets a number at the end of its address.

## How to take a vacancy off the site

1. If the position is filled, close the hiring instead: the page stays, marked as closed.
2. If the vacancy should not have been there, press «Take off the site» in the menu of the row. Its
   page answers «not found», and any link to it stops working.
3. To remove it, press «Delete» and confirm. It goes to the bin, and its address can be taken by
   something else. «Restore» on the «Bin» tab brings it back, if the address is still free.
