# about

A category is a group on the careers page and a link in the filter above it, such as development or
sales. It has no page of its own. There is no draft here: what you save is on the site at once.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category», write the «Title» and press «Create».
2. On the page that opens, check the «Key» and press «Save».
3. Open a vacancy, choose the category on its «Settings» tab and press «Publish».

A category without open vacancies is not printed, so the group appears with the first of them.

## How to change the order of the groups

1. Drag a category to its new place.
2. The order saves by itself.
3. Open the careers page of the site and check the groups.

## How to delete a category

1. Look at the number in the row. If it holds vacancies, press «Show its vacancies» in the menu of
   the row and move them to other categories.
2. Press «Delete» in the menu of the row.
3. Confirm.

Its group leaves the careers page, and its vacancies stay. This list has no bin, so the category
cannot be brought back from here, and its key stays taken. To take a group off the site for a time,
turn off «Shown on the site» in it instead.
