# about

A label marks a product: a badge on its card, a choice in the catalogue filter, or both. Here you set
what the label says and how it looks. A product gets the label in its own form.

# howto

## How to make a badge in a colour of your own

1. In «Tone», choose «Own colour» and pick the background.
2. Leave «Text colour» on «Automatic», or pick a colour.
3. Save and check the badge on a product card.

## How to make a label buyers do not see

1. Switch off «Badge on the card» and «In the filter».
2. Save.

The label stays in the panel and in the list of products, and your templates can still use it.

# fields

## title

The word on the badge and in the filter. A badge is small, so one or two words are enough: «Sale», not
«Seasonal sale of last year's models».

## code

Do not change the code of a working label without need: links to the filter that are already in
adverts and bookmarks will stop picking it. Buyers see the code only in the page address.

## color

Gives the badge its meaning at a glance: a sale usually stands out in «Danger», a new arrival in
«Success». A tone follows the look of the site, and «Own colour» stays the same whatever the site's
design does.

## text-color

Pick a colour only if the automatic one looks wrong on your background. A pale text on a pale
background is hard for buyers to read.

## is-badge

With it on, buyers see the label on the product card. Switched off, the card stays clean, while the
label goes on working in the filter and in the list of products.

## is-visible

Switch it on for labels people want to narrow the catalogue by, such as «Sale». Service labels are
better left out, so the filter stays short.
