# about

Labels are the short marks on product cards: top, sale, new. A label can also be a choice in the
catalogue filter, or a service mark that only picks products. Here you make labels and set their
order; a product gets them in its own form.

# howto

## How to add a label

1. Press «New label», fill in the name and choose the tone.
2. Save the label.
3. Open the products and add it in «Labels», or tick them in the list of products and use «Actions».

## How to change the order of the badges

1. Drag the labels in the list.

Cards and the filter follow this order: a product cannot have an order of its own.

## How to delete a label

1. Press «Show its products» and take the label off them.
2. Delete the label. It goes to the bin.

A label that is on products cannot be deleted.
