# about

A landing is a category's list with filters chosen in advance, under an address, texts and SEO of its
own. Choose the base and the set, and the page is there. Use it for what people really search for:
«Black laptops», «Winter tyres under 100».

# howto

## How to make a landing

1. Fill in «Name» and choose the «Base».
2. Build the «Set»: the number of products is counted as you go.
3. Check the suggested «Address» and press «Create».
4. Fill in the «Texts» and «SEO» tabs.
5. Publish it.

## How to link a landing from its category

1. Switch on «In the category’s collections».
2. Set «Order» if the category has several and press «Save».

Buyers will see the link in «Collections» on the category page.

# fields

## name

Buyers do not see it as the heading unless «H1» is empty: then it stands at the top of the page. Keep
it short, since it is also the text of links.

## category

Decides where the products come from and which filters the set can use. Choose the narrowest category
that holds all the products you want.

## set

The filters already applied on this landing: brands, prices, properties. They work like the ones
buyers tick, and two landings on one base cannot have the same set.

## slug

This is what people see in search results and adverts, so write what the page is about. If you change
it, the old address leads to the new one.

## sort

Pick the order that sells this page best: cheapest first for a page about low prices, for example.

## on-category

Turn it on for the landings worth a buyer's attention. Too many links on a category page push the
products down.

## position

Matters only when a category has several landings. Give the most important one the smallest number.

## h1

The main heading of the page, and search engines read it first. Write what a person would search for:
«Black laptops».

## text-above

Keep it short: it pushes the products down the page. A sentence or two about the choice is enough.

## text-below

A place for a longer text for search engines and curious readers: it stands under the products, so it
does not get in their way.

## recommended

Use it to lead buyers to what you want to sell here first. Products in the strip stay in the list as
well.

## seo

How the landing looks in search results and when a link to it is shared. This matters most on a
landing: it exists to be found. An empty landing stays up but is closed from search engines.
