# about

A landing is a catalogue page with a filter chosen in advance, under an address and texts of its own:
«Black laptops» instead of a laptop page with a ticked filter. Search engines and adverts lead to it.
Here you see all landings, how many products each has and which need attention.

# howto

## How to make many landings at once

1. Press «Create in bulk».
2. Choose the «Bases» and what to go through, such as every brand.
3. Fill in the «Templates» for the address and the headings.
4. Press «Preview», check the rows that will be skipped and why, then run it.

## How to deal with «Needs attention»

1. Filter the list by «Needs attention» and open the landing.
2. Look at the reason in its row: a value of the set was deleted, or the set became another landing's.
3. Fix the set and press «Save». The mark goes away.

A landing whose set is empty or taken by another one is unpublished by itself.

## How to bring back a deleted landing

1. Open «Bin» over the list.
2. Press «Restore».

«Delete for good» cannot be undone: the address stops answering, and no redirect is left.
