# about

The form of one review: who wrote it, what they wrote, and where it is shown. There is no draft
here: whatever you save on a published review is on the site at once.

# howto

## How to prepare a review in advance

1. Fill in the form and leave «Published» off.
2. Press «Save».
3. When it is time, switch on «Published» and save again.

## How to translate a review

1. Switch the form to the language.
2. Fill in «Text». «Name» and «Job title» can stay as they are unless they read differently there.
3. Press «Save».

# fields

## photo

It stands on the card of the review, beside the name, cut to a square. Use only a picture the author
agreed to show.

## name

Visitors see it on the card, the initials are made from it when there is no photo, and the panel
names the review by it. One language is usually enough.

## job-title

Visitors read this line to judge who is speaking: «Owner of an online spare parts shop». Left empty,
it leaves no gap on the card. Not written in a language, the default one is used.

## profile-url

It opens in a new tab, and search engines are told not to follow it, so the site passes no weight to
it. Visitors see only the name, not the address.

## text

The main part of the card; the line breaks are kept. No other language stands in for a missing one,
and the row of the list shows the languages where the review is seen.

## rating

Stars stand on the card, for visitors only. They do not reach search results: the site prints no
review markup, on purpose.

## reviewed-on

In the standard Reviews block it stands with the name and job title. Left empty, it leaves no gap on
the card.

## published

Switch it off to take the review off every page at once: it stays in the panel with the mark «Not
published». There is no draft, so edits to a published review are not previewed anywhere first.

## categories

A review with no category is seen only in a block showing all of them. A hidden category still picks
reviews for a block, it just gets no button. Each category keeps its own order of reviews.
