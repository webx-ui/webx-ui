# about

A category is a button of the filter in a Reviews block and a way to pick reviews for it. It has no
page of its own and no draft: what you save is on the site at once.

# howto

## How to make a category only for picking reviews

1. Fill in «Title»: «On the home page», for example.
2. Switch off «Shown on the site» and press «Save».
3. Put the reviews into it, then choose this category in the block.

Visitors never see such a category as a button, and the block still shows its reviews.

# fields

## title

The word visitors see on the button of the filter, so keep it short: «Delivery», not «Reviews about
delivery». With the filter off, it does not appear on the site.

## is-visible

Switch it off for a category that only picks reviews for a block. Whether the reviews themselves are
published does not change.
