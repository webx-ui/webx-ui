# about

A category groups reviews: a Reviews block shows the chosen ones, and in its filter each category is
a button. A category has no page or address of its own. The order of this list is the order of the
filter buttons on the site.

# howto

## How to add a category

1. Press «New category».
2. Fill in «Title» and press «Create».
3. Put reviews into it with the «Categories» field of each review.

## How to change the order of the filter buttons

1. Drag a category by its grip.
2. The new order saves itself and is on the site at once.

## How to change the order of reviews inside a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its reviews».
2. In the list that opens, drag the reviews by their grips.

Blocks showing exactly this one category follow this order.

## How to delete a category

1. In the menu of the category, press «Show its reviews» and take the category off each of them.
2. Back here, press «Delete» in its menu and confirm.

A category with reviews in it cannot be deleted. Its button leaves every filter, and this section
has no way to bring a deleted category back.
