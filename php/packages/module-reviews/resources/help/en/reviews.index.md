# about

All the reviews of the site: the list on one side, the open review on the other. A review has no
page of its own: visitors see it in a Reviews block on a page of the site. A row shows the stars and
the languages the review is seen in.

# howto

## How to add a review

1. Press «New review» at the top of the list.
2. Fill in «Name» and «Text», and give it a «Rating» if there is one.
3. Choose «Categories» and switch on «Published».
4. Press «Save».

The review appears at once in every block that shows its categories.

## How to show reviews on a page of the site

1. Open the page and add the Reviews block.
2. In «Categories», choose the ones you need, or leave the field empty to show every review.
3. Pick how it looks: one review, a grid, a slider or a running ribbon.
4. To give visitors buttons by category, switch on «A filter by category above the list».
5. Save the page the way it is saved.

If the block is not in the list, ask a developer to install it: a new site may not have it yet.

## How to change the order of reviews

1. Clear the search.
2. Leave «Category» empty to change the order of the whole list, or pick a category to change the
   order inside it.
3. Drag a review by its grip. The new order saves itself.

A block with one category shows that category's order. A block with none or several shows the order
of the whole list, each review once.

## How to find out why a review is not on the site

1. If the row says «Not published», open the review and switch on «Published». The same mark with a
   tooltip on a published review means it has a text in no language.
2. Check that «Text» is filled in, in the language of the page.
3. Check that the block on the page shows a category of this review.

## How to delete a review and bring it back

1. In the menu of the review, press «Delete» and confirm.
2. To bring it back, open the «Bin» view and press «Restore» in its menu.

The review leaves every block on the site. Restored, it comes back to its places.
