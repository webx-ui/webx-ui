# about

The types of block the site's content is built from. A type is made here whole, with its fields,
template and styles, and editors then fill it in on pages. A published change reaches every page the
block stands on, so this section is for whoever looks after the site's markup.

# howto

## How to make a new block

1. Press «New block» and choose its «Kind»: «Block» for pages, «Component» for calling from other
   templates.
2. Fill in «Name», «Identifier» and «Group» and press «Create».
3. In the editor, write the template, the styles and the fields, and fill in the sample.
4. Press «Publish». Only now can editors add it.

## How to change the order in the list of blocks

1. Open the tab of the group, or «All groups», and clear the search.
2. Drag the cards into the order you need.
3. Open «Add a block» on a page and check: editors see the same order.

The order is saved by itself and does not affect the styles of blocks on the site.

## How to carry blocks over to another site

1. Press «Export», tick the types and press «Download». Components they call go with them.
2. On the other site, press «Import» and «Choose a file».
3. Read what will happen to each type: new, differing, unchanged or refused.
4. Press «Import». New types arrive as drafts.

A type the other site already has stays as it was. To take the version from the file, switch on
«Replace» in its row.

## How to customise the standard look of a section

1. Open «Components» and find the card with «Customise».
2. Press «Customise»: a draft is made from the standard look.
3. Change it and press «Publish». Until then the site shows the standard look.

To go back, press «Reset to standard» in the editor of that component: its versions are deleted and
the standard look returns.

# fields

## Hidden from editors

The type cannot be added any more, but pages that have it keep showing it. The switch is «Offered to
editors» on the «Settings» tab of the type.

## Not published

The type has never been published, so editors cannot add it and the site does not show it. The card
of a published type shows its version on the site and the draft waiting.
