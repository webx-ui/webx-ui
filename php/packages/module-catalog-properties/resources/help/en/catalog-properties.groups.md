# about

Groups are the headings of the specifications on a product page: «Screen», «Processor». They keep a
long list readable. Here you make groups and set their order; a property joins its group in its own
form.

# howto

## How to add a group

1. Press «New group», fill in the name and save.
2. Open the properties that belong there and choose the group in «Group».

## How to change the order of the headings

1. Drag the groups in the list.

Every product page follows this order at once.

## How to delete a group

1. Move its properties to another group in their forms.
2. Delete the group. It goes to the bin.

A group with properties in it cannot be deleted.
