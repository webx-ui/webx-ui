# about

A property is one thing products are described by: colour, weight, Wi-Fi. Here you decide what kind
of value it has and where buyers meet it: in the filter, on the card, in the specifications. The
value itself is filled in on each product.

# howto

## How to give a property its list of values

1. On «Main», choose the type «Reference book» and press «Save».
2. Open the «Values» tab.
3. Type a value and press Enter, then the next one.

## How to merge two values that mean the same

1. On the «Values» tab, find the extra value and press «Merge with…».
2. Choose the value to keep and press «Merge».

Products move to the value you kept, and its old address leads to the new one. This cannot be undone.

## How to filter a number by ready-made steps

1. Switch on «In the filter» and set «Filter by» to «Intervals», then save.
2. Open the «Intervals» tab and press «Add an interval».
3. Fill in the label and the ends of each step.
4. Press «Save the intervals».

## How to open pages of one value to search engines

1. Switch on «In the filter» and «Pages of one value open to search engines».
2. Fill in «Page title pattern», or leave it for the catalogue's own.
3. Press «Save».

The page exists inside a category: for example, black laptops.

# fields

## title

The word buyers see in the specifications, on the card and over the filter. Name it the way buyers
say it: «Screen size», not «Display param. 2».

## code

Changing it changes the addresses of filter pages with this property, and search engines take time to
catch up. Buyers see it only in the address.

## type

The most lasting choice on this form. A reference book gives buyers a list to pick from, a number can
be a slider, a text is only read, and yes / no is one switch in the filter.

## group-id

Sets the heading this property stands under in the specifications on a product page. With no group
it stands last, with no heading.

## is-multiple

Switch it on when a product can really have several: a set of colours, a list of suitable models.
With it off, a product holds exactly one value and the form offers one choice.

## is-tree

For values that nest: country and city, make and model. Buyers open the filter level by level, and
you drag values inside each other on the «Values» tab.

## leaves-only

With it on, a product can hold only a value with nothing under it: a city, not the country. That keeps
the filter exact.

## value-order

«Alphabetical» follows each language by itself and needs no care. Choose «By hand» for values with a
natural order, such as S, M, L, XL: then you drag them on the «Values» tab.

## has-color

Each value gets a colour, and the filter draws it before the name. Useful for a property like
«Colour», where buyers look rather than read.

## has-image

Each value gets a small picture, and the filter draws it before the name. Useful for materials and
patterns that are hard to describe in words.

## unit-prefix

Printed right before the number on the site: a currency sign or «from», for example. Most properties
do not need it.

## unit-suffix

Printed right after the number on the site: «kg», «cm», «W». The example under the fields shows what
buyers will read.

## precision

Decides how the number is rounded on the site. For a weight of 1.25 kg, 2 digits show it as it is,
and 0 digits show 1.

## toggle-slug

Buyers see this word only in the address of the filter page. Changing it changes those addresses, so
leave it alone once the filter is working.

## is-filterable

Buyers can narrow the catalogue by this property, in the categories that have it. A filter on
something nobody chooses by only lengthens the column: leave it for what really helps to pick.

## filter-mode

A slider suits a smooth range, such as weight. Intervals suit ready-made steps: buyers click «1–2 kg»
instead of dragging. Their steps are set on the «Intervals» tab.

## is-indexable

Lets a page such as «black laptops» appear in search results. Open it only for values people really
search for: hundreds of nearly empty pages do the site harm.

## seo-pattern

The title of a page of one value in search results and in the browser tab. Put the words in the
order a person would search for them.

## is-searchable

The site's search finds products by the value of this property as well: someone who types «graphite»
gets the products with that colour. Switch it on for words buyers really type.

## in-card

The value is printed on the product card in the catalogue. A card has little room, so keep this for
the one or two properties people choose by.

## on-page

The value is printed in the specifications on the product page. Most properties should be here;
switch it off only for service values buyers do not need.

## in-list

Adds a column to the list of products in the panel, for your work. Buyers do not see it.
