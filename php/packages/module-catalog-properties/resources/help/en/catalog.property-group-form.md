# about

A group is a heading in the specifications on a product page: «Screen», «Processor». Here you name it
and decide whether buyers see the heading. Properties join the group in their own forms.

# fields

## title

Buyers read it over the properties of the group. One or two words are enough: «Screen», «Dimensions».

## is-visible

Switch it off when the properties should stay together in their place but need no title of their
own: a short block of two or three lines, for example.
