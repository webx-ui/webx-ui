## How to choose which properties a category has

1. Save the category, if it is new.
2. Open the «Properties» tab.
3. Press «Add a property…» and choose one, then the next.

Products of this category and of everything under it get these properties in their forms. Properties
from the categories above come by themselves. To decide which of them buyers can filter by, use the
«Filters» tab.
