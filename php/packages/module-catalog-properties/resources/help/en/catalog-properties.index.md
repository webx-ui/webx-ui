# about

Properties are what products are described by: colour, weight, screen size. They fill the
specifications on a product page and, when you want it, the catalogue filter. Here you make
properties and set their order; a category decides which of them its products have.

# howto

## How to add a property and show it in the filter

1. Press «New property», fill in the name and choose the type. The type cannot be changed later.
2. Switch on «In the filter» and press «Save».
3. Open the category and add the property on its «Properties» tab.
4. Fill in the value in the forms of the products, on the «Specifications» tab.

## How to change the order in the filter

1. Clear the filters over the list, if any are set.
2. Drag the properties in the list.

A category with its own order on its «Filters» tab keeps it.

## How to bring back a deleted property

1. Open «Deleted».
2. Find the property and press «Restore».

The values of the products were kept, so the filter and old addresses come back with it.
