# about

Everything a price card says is written here: the name, the price, what is included and the button.
There is no draft: what you save reaches every «Tariffs» block that shows this tariff at once. A
published tariff is seen in every language of the site.

# howto

## How to show words instead of a price

1. Clear the «Price».
2. Fill in the «Price in words»: «On request», say.
3. Press «Save».

A zero in «Price» is a number and prints as zero, so for «Free» leave it empty and write the word.

## How to add a button

1. Fill in the «Label».
2. Choose the «Link»: a page of the site or an address.
3. Choose the «Look».
4. Press «Save».

A label without a link is refused.

## How to translate a tariff

1. Switch the form to the language.
2. Write the «Name», the «Badge», the «Period» and the «Price in words» if they should differ.
3. Write the lines of «What is included», the «Description» and the «Label» of the button.
4. Press «Save».

The first four are taken from the default language when empty. The lines, the description and the
button are not: unwritten, they are missing from the card in that language.

## How to show what a service costs

1. If the site has services, choose the service in «Services» and press «Save».
2. Open that service and add the «Tariffs» block to its content.
3. Narrow the block to the service of the page it stands on.

The block remembers «the service of this page», so a copy on another service shows that service's
tariffs.

# fields

## name

The main line of the card, the one visitors compare tariffs by, so keep it short. The same name
stands in this list and in the search.

## badge

Empty, the card has no such line. It is plain text and does not follow the price: after changing the
price, read the badge again. Not written in a language, it is taken from the default one.

## featured

The card gets the look of the recommended one, and a star appears in the list. The word over it
comes from the block, from its «Featured label» field. The order of the cards stays as it is.

## price

Zero prints as zero; for a free tariff leave it empty and write the word below. The number is
written the way the language of the page writes numbers. A change is on the site after the save, in
every block at once.

## currency

Visitors see the symbol beside the number, not the code. Changing it does not recalculate the price.
A new tariff starts in the first currency of the list.

## period

Without it a price can read as a single payment, so fill it in for anything paid regularly. It is
shown only beside a number, not beside the words. Not written in a language, it is taken from the
default one.

## price-text

It does nothing while «Price» has a number: visitors see the number. Not written in a language, it
is taken from the default one.

## features

Drag the lines to change their order on the card. With more than four lines the list takes two
columns. The line over the list comes from the block, from its «Above the list» field.

## feature-text

One line is one item of the list on the card, so keep it to a few words. Visitors see it as written
here.

## description

A paragraph on the card under the list, with its line breaks kept. It is optional: most cards say
everything in the lines.

## button-label

The words on the button, so say what the visitor gets: «Get started». A label without a «Link» is
not saved.

## button-link

A link without a «Label» prints nothing. The button goes quietly: if the page is later taken off the
site, the card loses it with no warning, so look here after moving pages around.

The full price list in PDF is «A file» of the media library; with «Download instead of opening» the
browser saves it under the name it was uploaded with. A file deleted from the library takes the
button off the card the same quiet way.

## button-variant

It changes only how the button looks, not where it leads. The looks are set up for the whole site;
if one is taken away, the button takes the first look of the list.

## categories

A tariff can be in several groups or in none. In none, it stays in the blocks that show every tariff
and is missing from the blocks narrowed to groups.

## services

This field exists only on a site with services. The chosen services also appear on the card as
links.

## published

Switched off, the tariff leaves every block at once and stays in the list. There is no draft, so
edits to a published tariff also reach the site with the save.
