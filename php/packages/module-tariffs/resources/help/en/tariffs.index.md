# about

A tariff is a price card: a name, a price, what is included and a button. It has no page of its own
and reaches the site in the «Tariffs» block, on any page. The order of this list is the order of the
cards on the site.

# howto

## How to add a tariff

1. Press «New tariff», the first line of the list.
2. Fill in the «Name» and the «Price», or the «Price in words».
3. Add the lines of «What is included» and the button.
4. Choose the «Groups» and switch on «Published».
5. Press «Save». The tariff is created by this first save.

A published tariff appears at once in every «Tariffs» block that shows it.

## How to change the order of the cards

1. Clear the search.
2. To order the whole list, leave «Any group». To order one group, choose it in the filter.
3. Drag the tariff to its new place.
4. The new order saves itself.

The whole list and each group keep an order of their own, so a drag in one does not move the others.

## How to take a tariff off the site for a while

1. Click the tariff in the list.
2. Switch off «Published».
3. Press «Save».

It leaves every block at once and stays in the list with the mark «Not published».

## How to delete a tariff and bring it back

1. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
2. Confirm. The tariff goes to the bin and leaves every block on the site.
3. To bring it back, open «Bin», press «···» in its row and then «Restore».

A restored tariff returns to the blocks and the groups it was in.
