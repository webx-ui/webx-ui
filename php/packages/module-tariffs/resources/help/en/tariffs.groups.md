# about

A group gathers tariffs: «For individuals», «For business». It has no address and no page of its
own; a «Tariffs» block uses it to pick which tariffs to show. A tariff can be in several groups.

# howto

## How to add a group

1. Press «New group».
2. Fill in the «Title» and press «Create». The group opens.
3. Check «Shown on the site» and press «Save».
4. Open a tariff and add it to the group in its «Groups» field.

## How to change the order of the groups

1. Drag the group to its new place.
2. The new order saves itself.

It matters where a site's template shows the groups one after another, as tabs for example.

## How to see the tariffs of a group

1. Press «···» at the end of the row.
2. Press «Show its tariffs». The list of tariffs opens, narrowed to this group.

There you can also drag the order of the tariffs inside the group.

## How to delete a group

1. Open each of its tariffs and take the group out of «Groups». The row shows how many are left.
2. Press «···» at the end of the row and then «Delete».
3. Confirm.

A group that still holds tariffs is not deleted. This list has no «Restore», so a deleted group is
made again from scratch.
