# about

A profile turns an administrator who writes into a person visitors can see: a name, a role, a photo
and, if wanted, a page with all their articles. Without a profile the articles are signed with the
name of the account. There is one profile per account.

# howto

## How to add an author

1. Press «New author».
2. In «Account» choose whose articles these are. This is chosen once and cannot be changed later.
3. Fill in «Name on the site» in the main language.
4. Press «Save». The name under this author's articles changes at once.

## How to give an author a page

1. Choose the author in the list.
2. Turn on «Page on the site» and fill in the «Address».
3. Press «Save». The list now shows the mark «Page on the site» instead of «No page».
4. Press «Open the page» to check it.

## How to remove a profile

1. Choose the author in the list.
2. Press «Delete» and confirm.

The articles stay on the site and are signed with the name of the account. The author's page stops
answering.
