# about

The text of the article is on the «Content» tab, everything about it on «Settings». «Save» keeps a
draft the site does not show, and «Publish» puts it on the site. Rubrics, tags, the related articles
and the pin are the exception: they reach the site with «Save».

# howto

## How to schedule an article for a later day

1. On the «Settings» tab set «Date and time» to a moment ahead.
2. The button at the bottom now says «Schedule»: press it and confirm.
3. The status becomes «Scheduled», and the head of the article says when it goes out.

Nobody has to be in the panel at that moment: the article appears by itself. To cancel, press «Take
off the site» under the date.

## How to change an article that is already on the site

1. Change the text or the settings and press «Save».
2. The article stays «Live» with the mark «edits»: visitors still read the previous text.
3. Press «Preview» to see the new one.
4. Press «Publish» and confirm.

To drop the edits, choose «Discard changes» in the «···» menu at the top. The published text comes
back, and what was changed since cannot be recovered.

## How to change the address of a published article

1. On the «Settings» tab change the «Address» and press «Save».
2. Press «Publish»: the question names the new address and the old one.
3. Open the old address and check that it leads to the new one.

## How to go back to an earlier version

1. Open the «History» tab and find the version.
2. Press «Restore» beside it and confirm. The version becomes the draft.
3. Check the article and press «Publish». Until then the site does not change.

## How to take the article off the site

1. Press «Take off the site» at the top of the article, or under the date on the «Settings» tab.
2. Confirm.

The text, the history and the rubrics stay. «Publish» puts the article back at the same address.

# fields

## blocks

This is the body of the article: what the visitor reads between the heading and the tags. Changes
stay in the draft until «Publish», so you can work on a live article without visitors noticing.

## title

Visitors see it as the heading of the page, on the cards in every list and in the breadcrumbs. With
an empty SEO card it also becomes the title in search results. Changing it does not change the
address, and on a live article the new title appears after «Publish».

## slug

It cannot repeat the address of another article or of a rubric: such an address is refused. On a
live article the new address starts working after «Publish», and the old one keeps leading to it.
The rubric is not part of the address, so moving the article between rubrics breaks no links.

## lead

The article's own page does not print it by default: visitors read it on the cards in the feed, in
a rubric and in «Read next», and in the RSS feed. With an empty SEO card it becomes the description
of the page.

## cover

Stands on the article's page and on its card in every list. With an empty SEO card it is also the
picture shown when the link is shared. Without a cover the article and its card simply have no
picture.

## published-at

On an article that is on the site or scheduled, the date changes with «Save», without «Publish»: a
day ahead takes it off the site straight away until that day comes. Left empty on a draft, the
article goes out at the moment «Publish» is pressed. Visitors see this date on the page and on the
cards.

## author

At first it is whoever created the article. Visitors see the name on the article's page, and with
«Nobody» no name is printed. The change reaches the site with «Publish».

## pinned

Keep it for one or two articles: a forgotten pin leaves an old article above everything new. The
date does not change. The same switch is in the menu of the row in the list of articles.

## rubrics

An article without a rubric still goes out: it is in the common feed but on no rubric's page. If the
main rubric is hidden, it drops out of the breadcrumbs and the article stays on the site. Moving
between rubrics does not change the address of the article.

## tags

Visitors see the tags as links at the bottom of the article, each leading to a page with all its
articles. They also decide what «Read next» offers. Pick a word that already exists: two spellings
are two pages, and tidying them up afterwards takes a merge in the «Tags» section.

## related

These go first in «Read next» under the article, in the order you drag them into. An article that
is not on the site is skipped. The block has a fixed length on each site, so pin that many and
nothing is added automatically.
