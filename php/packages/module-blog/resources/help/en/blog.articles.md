# about

All the articles of the blog in one list: what is live, what is waiting for its day, what is still a
draft and what has been taken off the site. Visitors see an article only once it is published;
until then it exists only here. Pinned articles are marked and stand first in the feed on the site.

# howto

## How to write and publish an article

1. Press «New article», write the «Title» and press «Create». The address is made from the title.
2. In the article that opens, build the text on the «Content» tab.
3. On the «Settings» tab fill in the «Lead» and the «Cover», and choose «Rubrics» and «Tags».
4. Press «Save», then «Preview» to see the draft as the site will print it.
5. Press «Publish» and confirm.

## How to find an article

1. Type part of the title or the address in the search box.
2. Press «Filters» and choose a «Rubric», a «Tag» or an «Author».
3. To see one state only, use the tabs over the list: «Live», «Scheduled», «Drafts», «Off the site».

## How to publish straight from the list

1. Open the menu of the row.
2. Press «Publish».

The article goes out at once, with no question asked and under the present moment, even if a later
day is set in its settings. To schedule an article, open it and publish from there.

## How to take an article off the site without deleting it

1. Open the menu of the row.
2. Press «Take off the site». There is no question: it happens at once.

The article now answers «not found», but its text, history and address stay. «Publish» puts it back
at the same address, under the present moment, so it returns to the top of the feed.

## How to delete an article and bring it back

1. In the menu of the row press «Delete» and confirm. The article leaves the site and its address
   becomes free.
2. To bring it back, open the «Bin» tab.
3. In the menu of the row press «Restore».

An article that was live goes back on the site. If its address has been taken in the meantime, the
restore is refused: free the address first.

# fields

## Status

- «Draft»: never published, visitors have not seen it.
- «Scheduled»: has a date ahead and goes out by itself when that moment comes.
- «Live»: on the site. The mark «edits» beside it means something is saved but not yet published,
  and visitors still read the previous text.
- «Off the site»: it was published and has been taken down.
