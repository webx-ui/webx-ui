# about

A rubric is a section of the blog with a page of its own, and one article can be in several. The
order of this list is the order of the rubrics on the site. A rubric has no draft: what you save is
on the site at once.

# howto

## How to add a rubric

1. Press «New rubric», write the «Name» and press «Create». The address is made from the name.
2. On the rubric's page add the «Introduction» and the «Cover» if you want them.
3. Press «Save».
4. Choose the rubric in the «Rubrics» field of an article: a rubric gets its articles there.

## How to change the order of the rubrics

1. Drag a rubric by its handle to the new place.
2. The order saves itself.
3. Open the blog on the site and check the rubrics over the feed.

## How to hide a rubric for a while

1. Open the rubric.
2. Turn off «Show on the site» and press «Save».

In the list it is marked with a crossed-out eye. Its articles stay on the site.

## How to delete a rubric

1. In the menu of the row press «Show its articles».
2. Move those articles to other rubrics: a rubric that holds articles cannot be deleted.
3. Back in the list, press «Delete» in the menu of the row and confirm.

The rubric leaves the site, its address becomes free, and this list offers no way to bring it back.
If it may be needed again, hide it instead.
