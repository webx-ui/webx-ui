# about

Here you set what a rubric is called, where its page lives and what stands at the top of it. A
rubric has no draft: «Save» puts the change on the site at once. Which articles are in the rubric
is decided in each article.

# howto

## How to change the address of a rubric

1. Change the «Address». A line under the field warns that the address is moving.
2. Press «Save».
3. Open the old address and check that it leads to the new one.

The addresses of the articles do not change: the rubric is not part of them.

## How to give a rubric a name in another language

1. Switch the language at the «Name» field and write the name.
2. Do the same for the «Address»: without it the rubric has no page in that language.
3. Press «Save».

# fields

## title

Visitors see it over the feed among the rubrics, as the heading of the rubric's page and in the
breadcrumbs of articles where it comes first. With an empty SEO card it is also the title in search
results. Changing it does not change the address.

## slug

The last part of the rubric page's address, and it cannot repeat the address of an article or of
another rubric. If you change it, the old address keeps leading to the new one. Left empty in a
language, the rubric has no page in it.

## is-visible

Use it to take a rubric off the site for a while without losing anything: switched back on, it
returns at the same address. While hidden it also drops out of the breadcrumbs of its articles.

## lead

Visitors read it at the top of the rubric's page, and the text can have formatting and links. With
an empty SEO card it becomes the description of the page. Left empty, the page starts straight with
the list.

## cover

The standard blog pages do not print this picture: it appears only where the site's theme lists the
rubrics with their pictures. With an empty SEO card it is also the picture shown when the link is
shared.
