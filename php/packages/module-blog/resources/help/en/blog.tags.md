# about

Tags are usually made while an article is being written, and this is where they are tidied up:
renamed, merged and deleted. Each tag has a page with all its articles. New tag pages are normally
kept out of search engines, so that a hundred thin lists do not count against the site.

# howto

## How to merge tags that mean the same thing

1. Tick two or more tags in the list.
2. Press «Merge into one» in the bar at the bottom.
3. In «Which tag to keep» choose the word that stays.
4. Leave «Redirect the old addresses» on, so that links to the old tag pages keep working.
5. Press «Merge».

The articles move to the tag you kept and the others are deleted. This cannot be undone.

## How to let search engines see a tag page

1. Open the menu of the row.
2. Press «Index».

Do this for tags that hold enough articles to be worth finding. To do it for several, tick them and
choose the same command in the menu of the bar at the bottom. «Keep out of the index» closes a page
again.

## How to rename a tag

1. In the menu of the row press «Rename».
2. Write the new word and save.

Visitors see the new word at once, and the address of the tag stays as it was.

## How to clear out unused tags

1. Open the «Nothing filed» tab.
2. Tick the tags, or tick them all in the heading of the table.
3. In the menu of the bar at the bottom press «Delete» and confirm.

## How to delete a tag

1. In the menu of the row press «Delete».
2. Read how many articles carry it and confirm.

A tag has no bin: it comes off all its articles and its page stops answering. If the page has links
pointing to it, merge the tag into another one instead.

# fields

## Indexing

- `indexed`: search engines may show the tag's page.
- `indexed — SEO rule`: a rule in the «SEO» section covers this address, so the page is open
  whatever is set on the tag. To close it, remove the rule.
- `noindex`: visitors can open the page, but search engines are asked not to show it.
