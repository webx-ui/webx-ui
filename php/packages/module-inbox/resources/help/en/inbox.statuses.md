# about

A status says where a submission stands: new, in progress, done. The statuses are common to all the
forms, and each one is a tab over the list of submissions, in the order of this screen. They are for
your team: visitors never see them.

# howto

## How to add a status

1. Press «New status».
2. Fill in «Title» and «Key»: small Latin letters, digits and hyphens, such as `waiting-for-reply`.
3. Choose «Colour». It changes only the look of the badge beside a submission.
4. Press «Save». The status gets its own tab in the list of submissions.

Change the «Key» of a working status only if you must: a saved link to its tab stops working, and
earlier lines of a submission's history show the old key as it was. «Nothing further expected» marks
a final status such as «Done». For now it is only a mark in this list: such submissions stay in
«All» and on their tab.

## How to choose the status new submissions get

1. In the «···» menu of the status, press «Settings».
2. Tick «Given to a new submission».
3. Press «Save». The previous status loses this mark.

Submissions already in the list keep their status. The status also goes to submissions added by hand.
If no status has the mark, new submissions get the first one in the list.

## How to keep submissions out of «All»

1. In the «···» menu of the status, press «Settings».
2. Tick «Spam» and press «Save».
3. Move unwanted submissions to this status, one by one or with the ticks in the list.

They leave «All», «Unread» and the count of unread beside the form, and stay on their own tab.
Nothing is moved here by itself: what the antispam of a form catches is not kept at all, so this is
for what got past it. The site may be set up to delete old spam by itself.

## How to change the order of the tabs

1. Take a status by its grip.
2. Drag it to its place.
3. Let go. The new order saves by itself.

## How to delete a status

1. Move everything out of it, in the list of each form: open the tab of the status, tick the rows
   and choose another status in «Move to».
2. In the «···» menu of the status, press «Delete».
3. Confirm.

A status with submissions in it says «In use», and «Delete» is not in its menu.
