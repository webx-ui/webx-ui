# about

One submission: the answers in the words the form had when it arrived, the files sent with it and all
that has happened to it. The status, the assignee and the notes are for your team, and the visitor
sees none of them. Opening a submission marks it as read for everybody.

# howto

## How to take a submission into work

1. Choose the next step in «Status». It saves at once.
2. Choose a colleague in «Assignee». Nobody gets a message about it: the avatar simply appears beside
   the submission in the list.
3. Write what matters on the «Notes» tab.

Each change becomes a line on the «What has happened» tab, with who made it and when. You can change
all this if you may work with submissions.

## How to reply to the visitor

1. Press «Reply by mail». Your mail program opens with the address and the subject filled in.
2. Write the letter and send it.
3. Leave a note on the «Notes» tab and move the status on.

The button is there when the submission holds an e-mail answer. The panel does not keep your letter,
so the note is what your colleagues will see.

## How to see where a submission came from

1. Open the «Details» tab.
2. Look at «How it arrived»: «From the site», or «Typed in» for one added by hand.
3. Read «Page», «Came from», «Placement» and «Language». A line with nothing to say is left out.
4. A visitor who came by an advertising link has its `utm_*` lines, «Landing page» — the page they came in on — and, if they agreed to statistics, «First visit · utm_*»: the campaign of their first visit, up to 90 days before.

## How to send a notification again

1. On the «Details» tab, find «Notifications». It says how it went for each recipient.
2. If it failed or is still waiting, have the mail of the site put right first.
3. In the «···» menu, press «Send the notification again».

The letter goes to everybody the form names now, including those who already got it. If the form names
nobody, the panel refuses and says so. If the form tells through channels, there is a «Channels» tab
with a line for each recipient, and only what did not deliver is sent again.

## How to go through submissions one by one

1. Open a submission from the list.
2. Press the arrows beside the heading. They go through the same tab and search, from newest to oldest.
3. Press the back arrow to return to the form, the tab and the page you left.

## How to mark a submission as unread or delete it

1. Press «···».
2. Press «Mark as unread» to return to the list with this submission unread again.
3. Or press «Delete» and confirm. It goes with its attachments, and this cannot be undone.

For an unwanted submission a spam status is safer than deleting: it leaves the list and can be
brought back. You can do both if you may work with submissions.
