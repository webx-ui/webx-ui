# about

Everything visitors send through the forms of the site arrives here. The forms are on the left, and
what came in through the chosen one is on the right, with a tab for each status. Spam has its own
tab and stays out of «All» and «Unread», so those two show only what needs an answer.

# howto

## How to see what has come in

1. Choose a form on the left. A coloured number beside it counts what nobody has opened yet.
2. Open the «Unread» tab. Rows nobody has opened stand out in heavier type.
3. Click a row to open the submission. From then on it counts as read for everybody.

A form with a struck-through name is switched off. A warning sign beside a form means nobody is told
about its submissions: they are kept, but nobody hears of them until somebody looks here. On a narrow
screen the forms fold away behind the «Forms» button.

## How to find a submission

1. Choose the form: the search looks inside one form at a time.
2. Type into «Search the answers» a telephone number, a name or a part of an address. The search
   covers every answer, not only the columns, and the number of a submission.
3. If nothing turns up, look under the tab of the spam status: «All» does not include it.

## How to deal with several submissions at once

1. Tick the rows.
2. In the line that appears over the list, choose a status in «Move to», or press «Delete».
3. If you are deleting, confirm: the submissions go with their attachments, and this cannot be undone.

The ticks are there if you may work with submissions.

## How to add a submission that came by telephone

1. Choose the form and press «New submission».
2. Fill in the questions of the form. What is required on the site is required here too.
3. Press «Save». The new submission opens.

It counts as read, and nobody is notified about it. Files cannot be attached by hand. The button is
there if you may work with submissions.

## How to download submissions as a table

1. Choose the form and the tab, and type a search if you need only a part.
2. Press «Export».
3. Open the CSV file in a spreadsheet program.

The file holds everything under the current tab and search, on all pages, with every question of the
form and not only the columns.

## How to add a form

1. Press «+» over the list of forms. On a narrow screen, press «Forms» and then «New form».
2. Fill in «Title» and «Address»: a short word such as `contact`.
3. Press «Save». The form opens, ready for its fields and its recipients.

To start from a form you already have, press «Duplicate» in its «···» menu. The copy opens switched
off, with the questions and the recipients of the original and none of its submissions. The «+», the
«···» menu and the «Statuses» button are there if you may manage forms.

## How to take a form off the site

1. In the «···» menu of the form, press «Settings».
2. On the «General» tab, switch off «Active».
3. Press «Save».

The form disappears from the pages it stands on, and its submissions stay here. A form that has
submissions cannot be deleted. «Delete» appears in the menu only for a form nothing has come through.
