The section «Payments» of the panel (payments spec §13, §20.7, stage П6) and its npm half, the new package `@webx-ui/module-payments`: «Methods» in the order of the checkout with a method's words, rule of delivery, limits of the amount, the settings of its type and gateway, the hold switch where the type can capture later, and the gateway's keys as «set / replace / remove» — a key never reaches the browser, a blank field keeps it, a key in the site's environment is shown as such; «Test the connection» asks the gateway with the stored keys and anything typed over them, journaled as `out/connection`; the addresses of notifications and return to copy into the gateway's account. «Attempts» — Laravel pages with the tabs «All», «In progress», «Failed», «Problems», a method and a period; an attempt with its events and «Check with the gateway». «Gateway exchange» — every event newest first in keyset pages, filtered by gateway, method, attempt, order, kind, direction, signature errors, errors and period; an event shows its headers and bodies (formatted on screen only) and «Download» as an attachment only for the new permission `payments.exchange` (super-admin by default), and the other events of its trace; no route changes or removes an event. The tab «Payment» of the order's card — the method with its bank details, the state, the attempts with their events by time, and for `orders.manage` «Mark as paid», «Check with the gateway», «Change the method», the payment link with the amount the buyer sees, and «Problem resolved». The ready-made role «Order manager» gets `payments.view`. The demo of the module switches the two built-in methods on.
