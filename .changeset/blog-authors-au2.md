`webx-ui/module-blog` / `@webx-ui/module-blog`: author pages, AU2 — the panel (§19.5 of the blog spec). On a site with `WEBX_BLOG_AUTHORS=true`, the blog group gets «Authors» (permission `blog.authors.manage`): the profiles beside their form `blog.author-form` — the account chosen once, the name for readers, role, photo, bio, «Page on the site», the address and an SEO tab from `module-seo`. Under «Author» in the article editor, a link to the author's profile, or one to make it. API under `/api/cms/blog/authors`; the playground has two profiles.
