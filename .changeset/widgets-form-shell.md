The shell of a form (WIDGETS §17.3, §17.4, W6.2). `webx-ui/widgets`: **`<x-webx-form :action method>`** with **`<x-webx-form.submit>`** prints `@csrf`, `@method` and a hidden `_webx_form` (which form of the page was sent); `:csrf="false"` for a door that guards itself, `method="get"` stays a plain form, a file field makes it multipart. Without JavaScript it is a plain POST and the page comes back with each error under its field, the errors of no field in the form's alert, the answer in the form that was sent, and a `<x-webx-dialog>` around it printed open. With it (`dist/form.js|css`, ≈3 KB gzip, only where a form stands) the same form is sent in place: `X-XSRF-TOKEN` from the cookie, on a 419 a fresh token from `GET webx/widgets/csrf` and one retry, a 422 into its fields by `data-webx-field` (now on every field's root) with the focus on the first, `is-sending`, `aria-busy` and the buttons held back, files by XMLHttpRequest with progress, the answer in a live region; in a dialog the thank-you takes the fields' place with a Close button. Events `webx:form:submit` (cancelable), `webx:form:success`, `webx:form:error`. **`WebxUi\Widgets\Forms\FormResponse`** — `::message($heading, $html)`, `::redirect($url)`, `::html($view)`, `::ok()`: JSON `{ ok, heading, message, redirect, html }` to the script, a redirect with the answer in the session to a plain POST. Words `widgets.form.*` in ten languages; `webx.openDialog` from the runtime. `webx-ui/theme-default`: Kitchen sink › Forms › Sending (en/ru/pl) — one form on the page and in a dialog, sent to a door of the widgets that answers only while the demo is on the site.
