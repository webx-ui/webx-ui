New module `webx-ui/module-payments`, its base (payments spec §5, §20, stage П1): tables of methods (gateway keys encrypted and hidden), the buyer's choice for a cart, attempts and the journal of the exchange with gateways `payment_gateway_events`; `ExchangeJournal` writes every event in one row through its own connection — the raw half before anything is parsed, the decision once — so a rolled-back status change keeps its event, and a failed write is logged without failing the payment (`recordOrFail()` for a notification, which is then answered 503); `ExchangeMasker` wipes the method's keys by value in every encoding (as is, url-encoded, JSON-escaped, inside base64), secret headers, secret fields and run-time secrets of a trace and card numbers, keeping the signature; bodies over 1 MB are cut with their full size and sha256; `GatewayHttp` — the only HTTP client of gateway satellites, Laravel `Http` and a PSR-18 client journaling every attempt of a request, transport errors included; the model of events refuses every update and delete; `webx:payments:exchange-prune --days=N` removes only events older than 365 days; `webx:doctor` notes a journal past `webx-payments.exchange.warn_size`; `Credentials` print as `[hidden]`; the module `payments` in `webx:setup`; the dictionary `webx-payments` in ten languages.
