`webx-ui/widgets`: the map — `<x-webx-map :lat :lng :zoom marker height title link>` and `<x-webx-map from="settings">`, the main address of the Contacts tab of `module-settings` (its text, coordinates and link to a map; an address without coordinates is printed alone, an empty tab prints nothing). Leaflet over the tiles of OpenStreetMap, which need no key; another provider of raster tiles is an entry of `webx-widgets.map.providers` (MapTiler is there, its `{key}` from the site's config) named in `webx-widgets.map.provider`. The server prints the place — the address and "Open in maps" — in a frame of the map's height; before the visitor agrees to `media` it carries a line saying where the map loads from, "Load" (this map) and "Always load maps" (the consent, without a reload), and the page asks for no tile. With consent the map goes in when its frame comes near the screen. The wheel and one finger move the page until the map is clicked or focused; the attribution of the tiles is printed in the corner of every map, with JavaScript or without; without JavaScript the map is its address and a link. `dist/map.js|css`, about 45 KB gzip with Leaflet, only on pages with a map. The `map` block, offered through `BlockOffers` beside `gallery`, `logos` and `video`: a heading, the contacts or coordinates with an address of their own, a zoom and a height (low, medium, tall). `webx-ui/theme-default`: Kitchen sink › Map.
