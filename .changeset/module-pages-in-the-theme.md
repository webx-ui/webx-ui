Module pages in the theme (THEMES §10, TH3.2–TH3.3). `webx-ui/module-blog` and `webx-ui/module-services` print `webx-blog-*` and `webx-services-*` classes and carry a base look on site tokens; their cards are the extension points `blog.card` and `services.card`, so a theme's `views/parts/<module>/card.blade.php` replaces a card without replacing the listing. `webx-ui/module-menu`: a base look for `<x-webx-menu::menu>` — every level on the page, nothing drops down. `webx-ui/module-events`, `webx-ui/module-vacancies` and `webx-ui/module-press` keep their `wx-*` classes, add the ones their views lacked and carry a base look too; the cards of events and vacancies are the points `events.card` and `vacancies.card`, and the lists' own inline look is printed only on a site without a theme. `webx-ui/themes`: `theme_active()`, which a module asks before it prints a style inline. `webx-ui/module-admin`: `webx:setup --modules=+blog,services` — the suggested set and these on top. One change of markup beyond classes: an article's date and author share one line, `webx-blog-article__meta` (the author was a `<p>` of its own). `webx-ui/theme-default`: showcase «Kitchen sink › Modules › Menu»; its demo page «Services» is «What we do» now (`/what-we-do`), because `/services` is the services module's index. A site that published a module's views keeps its copies; a site without a theme sees no change.
