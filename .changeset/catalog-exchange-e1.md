`module-catalog` imports and exports CSV and XLSX (E1 of the exchange series, the server side). A column of the file is a codec between a cell and a field of the product form, and every row is written by `ProductForm::save()` — the screen's checks, the satellites' parts and the journal are the ones the editor gets. The core's columns: `id`, `sku`, `barcode`, `external_id`, `name`/`slug`/`summary`/`description` with `@<locale>` for a translation, `category` and `categories` by a path of names (`Electronics/Phones`) or `#id`, `price`, `old_price`, `unit`, `priority`, `is_published` and `images` by address, downloaded on the queue once per address. An import keys rows by `sku`, `external_id`, `barcode` or `id`; creates and updates, only updates or only creates; leaves a field alone on an empty cell unless `empty_clears`; can create missing categories; unpublishes the published products that were not in the file within its categories (`absent: unpublish`); and stops after `exchange.max_errors` bad rows. A chunk is one transaction with a savepoint per row, so a bad row is an error by row, column, value and reason and its neighbours are written; `dry_run` runs everything and writes nothing. Large files go to the queue (`ProcessExchangeChunk`), the run is one entry of the journal with the source `import`, and the engine gets one mark per chunk (`Catalog::deferTouch()`). The API lives under `/api/cms/catalog/exchange` (`columns`, `inspect`, `import`, `export`, `runs`, their errors — as a CSV too — and files, `profiles`); a finished export is handed out by a signed link. Uploads go in pieces under the purpose `catalog.exchange`; `webx:catalog:exchange-prune` removes old files and runs every hour. The registries `ExchangeColumns` and `ExchangeFormats` are open to satellites and to packages of other formats. New dependency: `openspout/openspout`.
