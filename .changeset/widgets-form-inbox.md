The Inbox's form on the form engine (WIDGETS §17, W6.4 — W6 closed). `<x-webx-inbox::form>` prints an `<x-webx-form :csrf="false">` — the intake guards itself — with an `<x-webx-field>` per field of the panel (the eleven types are the widgets' own; a consent's words with their link are the field's slot; «half width» is `webx-inbox-form__field--half`), the honeypot, the timestamp and the captcha inside it. The form is checked in the browser by the intake's own rules through `FormRules` (the labels of the panel in the validator's words, the page's language), sent in place, its errors under its fields, its thank-you in the live region — and in a dialog (`FormDialogs`) in place of the fields with the form's own «Close». The intake answers with `FormResponse`: the form's thank-you (`::message()`, «Thank you…» when none is written) or its address (`::redirect()`), a redirect with the thank-you without JavaScript. `inbox.js` keeps only the captcha — an invisible reCAPTCHA or Turnstile and v3 run on `webx:form:submit`, every captcha reset after `webx:form:success`/`webx:form:error` — and is printed only by a form with a captcha; a captcha's refusal goes to its place (`data-webx-field="captcha"`). `webx-ui/module-inbox` now requires `webx-ui/widgets`.

**Breaking for a site that painted the form:** the classes `wx-form*` are gone — the fields and messages are `webx-field-*` and `webx-form-*`, the inbox's layout `webx-inbox-form`, `--<placement>`, `__fields`, `__field`, `__field--half`, `__captcha`, `__actions`, `__honeypot` (the table from old to new is in the guide «Inbox», «The look of the form»). **A `form.blade.php` the site published before** — any view of the form without `<x-webx-form>` — keeps working for an alias period: it is handed the variables it was, the old partials stay, its script is served as `{path}/inbox-legacy.js`, and the new audit check **`inbox.legacy_views`** (warning, ten languages) names the view until it is moved.

`webx-ui/widgets`: a field inside `<x-webx-form>` takes the old input and the errors in the session only when its own form was the one sent (`_webx_form`) — a contact form refused in the body no longer fills or reddens the subscription in the footer that has an `email` too. The dialog of a form (`FormDialogs`) renders the form first and is printed open when the form says it came back. `webx-ui/module-admin`: «The first rule» of the site's root `AGENTS.md` says that any form of the site is `<x-webx-form>` with `<x-webx-field>`, answered by `FormResponse` (`RootFile::FORMS_GUIDE`). `webx-ui/theme-default`: Kitchen sink › Forms › Inbox (en/ru/pl). The site skeleton keeps its empty `tests/Feature`, so `php artisan test` of a new site finds the directory its `phpunit.xml` names.
