New package `webx-ui/module-catalog-landings`: landing pages of the catalogue — a category's list, or the whole catalogue's, with a set of filters chosen in advance under an address, an SEO card and two texts of its own. The filter's links and the category's spelling of a set lead to its landing; a recommended strip; links between categories, landings and products; a count of products kept by the index; sets that follow merged and deleted values; the panel's API. The core fires `ProductsIndexed` after every batch of the index.
