Orders (`module-orders`, stage 2 of webx-ui#89): the new package `webx-ui/module-orders` with the tables of an order — `orders`, `order_items`, `order_adjustments`, `order_tenders`, `order_benefits`, `order_milestones` (each milestone once per order), `order_refunds`, `order_status_changes` — and the two sets of statuses, `order_public_statuses` (what the buyer sees, by a stable `code`) and `order_statuses` (the shop's stages, each in one public group, a colour from the closed palette `StatusColor`, optionally setting a milestone), both soft-deleted. The milestones are the enum `WebxUi\Orders\Milestone`. A site starts with a default set — «Accepted», «In progress», «Shipped», «Completed», «Cancelled» with eight internal statuses — seeded by the migration in every language of the site from the dictionary `webx-orders` (ten languages). The section «Orders» in the panel with the permissions `orders.view`, `orders.manage`, `orders.refund`, `orders.statuses`, and `webx:modules` offers `orders` (needs the cart). First release of `@webx-ui/module-orders` — the section and an empty list; the `Orders` service, the screens and the checkout come next.
