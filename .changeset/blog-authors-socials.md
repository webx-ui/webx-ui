`webx-ui/module-blog`: an author's networks — the «network + address» rows of the site's contacts, shown as icons on their page and named as the `sameAs` of their `Person`, which now has an `@id` shared by every article it wrote. A `BlogPosting` names its rubric, its tags, its language and the site's `Organization` as `publisher`; the feed is a `Blog`.
