`webx-ui/module-payments`, stage П2 (payments spec §6.1–§6.2, §7, §10, §12): the registry `MethodTypes` with the shop's own two types — `store.on_receipt` (does not await payment, «Pay at the counter» at the shop's point, the carrier's fee noted for cash on delivery) and `store.bank_transfer` (awaits payment; recipient, account, purpose with `{number}` and a note by language) — and their methods `on-receipt` and `bank-transfer`, created switched off; `Methods::available()` offers the enabled methods by amount, currency, the delivery rule (`any`, `on_hand`, `methods`, `except` against the checkout's `ShippingChoice`), the type's limits and an online method's keys, from the cache group `payments`, and `explain()` gives the reason of each; the checkout's slot «payment» `PaymentsCheckoutSlot` with the step `webx-payments::step`, the choice in `payment_selections` through `GET /api/payments/methods` and `PUT /api/payments/selection`, «Choose delivery first» while the delivery is not chosen and a choice the delivery took away dropped with a word; the order's block `payment` with the bank details, shown on «Thank you» and the order's page while the order awaits payment (`<x-webx-payments::instructions>`, `<x-webx-payments::status>`); `Payments::markPaid()` — an attempt `paid`, the milestone «paid» and an event `internal/manual` in the exchange journal; `webx:payments:selections-prune` nightly. `module-orders`: a block's owner may add HTML under its line on the order's page and «Thank you» (`BlockDefinition::$details`, `OrderBlocks::details()`).
