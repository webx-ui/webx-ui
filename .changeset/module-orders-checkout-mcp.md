module-orders: the checkout for an agent and its funnel. Events `Checkout\Events\CheckoutStepSaved(cartId, step)`, `CheckoutPayableChanged(cartId, from, to)` — on every divergence of the amount, counted by the draft in `payable_changes` (a new migration) — and `CheckoutPlaced(orderId, cartId)` after the commit; a draft that placed an order keeps `last_step = confirm`. MCP tools, only while the checkout is on: `orders_checkout_steps` (with `cart_id` — what is shown and why not), `orders_checkout_preview` (the confirmation of a cart tried without writing: the same 422 errors and 409 codes as `POST /checkout/place`), `orders_checkout_funnel` (day, week, month by the last step, orders, divergences), `orders_checkout_drafts_list` (contacts masked: `B*** A`, `+800 *** **78`, `b***@example.com`), `orders_checkout_settings_get` / `_set` (`orders.checkout.settings`; the dry run says what changes on the page); resource `orders://checkout`. `CheckoutAnswers` — the codes of the confirmation's refusals; `Steps\NamesErrors` — the codes of a step's errors; `Orders::orderable()` is public; `CheckoutContexts::of(Cart)`. Budgets: `GET /checkout` — 3 queries whatever the cart.
