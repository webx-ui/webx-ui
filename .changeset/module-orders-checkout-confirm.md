module-orders: the checkout's confirmation — `POST /checkout/place` with the key of a repeat (`place_key`: the same confirmation twice, from another tab or after a lost answer, is the same order; a key the draft does not know is `409 checkout.stale`), an amount that went down placed again at once with it and told on «Thank you», one that went up — `409 checkout.payable_changed` and the same key for the next press. The antispam of the checkout: a honeypot in the contacts and the confirmation (an order of nobody, no error), the mark of time `webx_ts` (`min_seconds`), the origin, `throttle:webx-orders-checkout` (presses a minute an address, orders an hour a cart) and the steps' own limit. The agreement with the terms becomes a line of the order's history (the document, its address and version, the time) in the order's transaction, and the draft keeps the contacts alone after the order. «Thank you» — `GET /checkout/thanks/{number}`, only for the session that placed the order (its signed-in owner is sent to the order's page): the number, «We sent a confirmation to …» while a channel of `orders.placed` writes to the buyer, the order and the link to its page.
