`webx-ui/widgets`: the pages of a list, "Show more" and the notice bar (spec §14, W5.4). **`<x-webx-pagination :paginator around>`** — previous, the numbers around this page with the first and the last, next, for what `->paginate()` answered; a stylesheet and no script, the first page the list's own address rather than `?page=1`. **`<x-webx-load-more :paginator pages>`** — the list and those links, which a page without JavaScript and a search engine get; the button fetches the next page as it is, adds the items of the same list, focuses the first new one, says what came to a screen reader and replaces the address with `?page=N`; `pages="shown"` keeps the links under the button. **`<x-webx-notice-bar>`** — a bar above the header with a close button: the first banner of the place `notice` of `webx-ui/module-banners`, else its slot; closed, it is remembered by the version of its words and hidden by a line in the head before the page is painted. Audit check `widgets.load_more_link` ("Show more" without a link to its next page). Words in ten languages. `webx-ui/module-banners`: the declared place `notice`, a place of words — a declared place with `'image' => false` takes a banner without a picture; the demo puts a switched-off banner of words in it. `webx-ui/theme-default`: the notice bar in the layout above the header; Kitchen sink › Show more and › Notice bar.
