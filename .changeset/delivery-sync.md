Delivery: carriers' directories on the site (delivery spec §7). A satellite's carrier implements `SyncsDirectories` and returns pages of `LocalityRecord`s and `PointRecord`s; `Synchronizer` writes each page in one transaction with the run's cursor (a page written twice makes no second row), matches localities with the geography's `Matcher` (hand-made towns are bound to, not duplicated), lets points wait for their binding and follow it, removes what a complete pass did not see — or holds the run past `webx-delivery.sync.max_removal_share` (10 %) until `confirm()` — and recounts `geo_localities.weight` after points, keeping a weight set by hand (the geography's `Editor` now marks it). A failure part-way removes nothing. The journal `delivery_sync_runs` with the base's words for a failure; the queued job `SyncDirectory` (3 tries, 60/300/900 s, from the same cursor); `webx:delivery:sync`; the schedule by each directory's cron, localities before points; a stale or held directory in `webx:doctor`; the first carrier to sync a country leads it in every process; `Schedule` and `Limits` of a point; `Testing\FakeCarrier` for tests and the playground.
