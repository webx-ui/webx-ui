The `video` block of `webx-ui/widgets`, offered through `BlockOffers` beside `gallery` and `logos` (`webx:setup`, or `php artisan webx:blocks:offered --install --module=widgets`): a heading, a link of YouTube or Vimeo or a video of the media library (a switch says which), a poster (empty: the video's own preview, fetched once into the library), a caption under the frame and a shape — 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16, the last two held to a phone's width in the middle of the column. It prints `<x-webx-video>`: a link waits for the visitor's consent to media, a file plays on the site with no consent asked. An address of no known provider, a file gone from the library or nothing at all prints nothing. `theme-default`'s Kitchen sink › Video stands on the real block now.

**Behaviour change in `webx-ui/module-catalog`:** the videos of the product gallery play through `<x-webx-video>`, and **a YouTube video now waits for the visitor's consent to media** — before an answer the page shows the poster with "Load" and "Always load videos" instead of a ▶ that loaded the player on a click. The picture stays the poster and gives the frame its shape; a file of the site is a `<video controls preload="none">` with no consent asked; `ProductMarkup` (`VideoObject`) is unchanged. The inline script, `data-webx-embed` / `data-webx-video` and the `webx-catalog-product__play` button are gone: a site that overrides `product.blade.php` should print `<x-webx-video>` the same way (see the catalog guide). The catalog now requires `webx-ui/widgets`, and a provider registered only in the catalog's `VideoProviders` is handed to the widgets' registry, so its videos play on the storefront as before.
