Widgets: a `tabs` block on `<x-webx-tabs>` — a heading and tabs of a title, a picture of the library and a text each; one tab open, a click or the arrow keys open another. The picture stands above the text or beside it where the column has room; the tabs at the start or in the middle, scrolling sideways in a narrow column. Without JavaScript every tab stands under its title. `php artisan webx:blocks:offered --install --module=widgets` installs it; types a site already has are not touched.
