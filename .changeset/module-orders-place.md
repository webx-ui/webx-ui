Orders (`module-orders`, stage 3 of webx-ui#89): `Orders::place(PlaceOrder)` makes an order of a cart — counted again with `Purpose::Order` under the lock of the cart's row, refused as `CartNotOrderable` (empty, another currency, lines that cannot be bought — with the cart's warnings) or `PayableChanged` (the fresh state, when the buyer saw another amount), frozen into the order (lines with absolute quantities, adjustments, tenders, benefits, the contacts with the first and the last name apart, the recipient, the blocks `delivery` and `payment` checked by the module that registered them in `OrderBlocks`), numbered from its id (`webx-orders.number`), put into the initial status with the milestones `placed` and `awaiting_payment` and the first line of history. `OrderPlacing` runs inside the transaction, where a listener refuses with `OrderRefused` and everything is rolled back; `OrderPlaced` with the plain `OrderData` comes after the commit. A repeat with the same idempotency key returns the same order, and `Orders::findByIdempotencyKey()` finds it. The cart (`module-cart`): `CartService::empty()` takes the lines and the declined rules and leaves the cart's row, its token and cookie; every write of `CartService` takes the lock of the cart's row first (`CartService::lock()`), so a line added in another tab while an order is made waits for it and stays in the cart.
