The panel's API asks the database far less (webx-ui#16, stage 4). Lists hash each row's revision out of what the page loaded: `HasCategories::categoryKeys()` reads a loaded category relation (and `syncCategories()` drops it), `Relations::loadIds($records, ...$roles)` reads the ids of `cms_relations` roles for a whole list in one query, blog articles load `seo` and the ids of `related` with the page — blog 90 → 12 queries a page of 20, recipes 158 → 10, vacancies 94 → 6, services 71 → 6, events 68 → 9; MCP `articles_list` 68 → 10 and `vacancies_list` 109 → 7; landings read their addresses and the labels of their facets once a page (`LandingResource::prime()`); the inbox reads its statuses once a list; pages read the home page once and `Editors::of()` one version per page. `RelationTarget::names()` — the id and title only, for the service filters of events and recipes; `EntityVersion::scopeWithoutPayload()` for the history lists. `module-blocks`: `Usage::counts()` lives on the new `block-usage` cache group (moved by every entity of `webx-blocks.entities` and the regions, and by `Renamer`, `LanguageShapes`, `StrayValues`). `module-admin`: the words of the manifest (titles, branding, the home's address, the languages) live on a versioned key per language — what a module says about the person (`meta`) is worked out on every request; the panel's dictionary answers with an `ETag` and `private, no-cache`, so a reopened panel gets a 304 without a body. `localization`: `DictionaryBuilder::version()`. New indexes: `media_files (created_at)`, `inbox_submissions (form_id, status_id)`. Every main list, form and MCP list has a query budget test.
