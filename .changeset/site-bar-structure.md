The panel on the site, fifth stage (SB5 of `WEBX_UI_SITE_BAR.md`): the structure of the blocks. The «Blocks» tab adds a block at the end of the page, the header or the footer, below another or inside a container — with the constructor's picker and the same rules of the place — and duplicates, moves up and down, hides, shows and deletes one (with a confirmation), from the `···` of its row and over its form; what cannot be done right now is greyed with the reason. Everything goes into the draft of the record or the region, and the page follows without a reload: a new block is put in, drawn from the draft with a dashed «Draft» frame and chosen, with the styles and script of a type the page did not have; a removed or hidden one is taken out; a moved one is carried. While picking, the chosen block has «Add below» on its lower edge. The draft's notices of the record and of a region link «Preview draft». `webx-ui/module-blocks`: `ContentOps` — the one dictionary of operations the agent's `blocks_edit_content` and the bar both apply; `POST /api/cms/blocks/content/{type}/{id}/edit` takes `add`, `duplicate`, `move`, `hide`, `show` and `remove` besides `set` (a new block's key is made on the server; only a published block type may be added; `allow`, `max`, `max_per_entity`, a region's declaration and the nesting depth are held to), takes `bundles` — the hashes of the page's sets — and answers `steps` (`place`, `move`, `remove`) and `assets`; a region's state carries `preview`. `webx-ui/module-admin`: `SiteBar::previews()` and `preview` on the context's record; the script on the page takes `insert`, `move`, `remove` and `assets`, reports its sets, frames a block by the boxes of what stands between its markers and follows blocks that change inside. `@webx-ui/module-admin`: `RowAction.hint`, «Preview draft» in the drawer's head, the new bridge messages. `@webx-ui/module-blocks`: the structure in `WxBlocksSiteBarTab`.
