themes: the critical CSS of the first screen (THEMES §13.3). `"critical": ["/", "/blog/first-article"]` in theme.json names the pages; `php artisan webx:theme:critical` opens them in Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844 (the site's `playwright` and `lightningcss`), keeps the rules of what is seen a quarter of a screen down, of what is fixed or sticky, the rules that hide windows, `@font-face`/`@keyframes`/`@property` and the `@media` that hold, and writes them minified with prefixes to `theme/critical.css` (`webx-themes.critical`). With the file, `@webxTheme` prints it inline and every stylesheet of the head — the widgets', the modules', the layers', Vite's, the blocks' — is preloaded and applied without blocking (`media="print"` + `onload`, a `<noscript>` link); without it the head is as before. `--check` compares the first screen with the critical CSS alone against the full CSS, pixel by pixel. Fonts: `"preload": true` on a face preloads the files of the page language's script only, and `"fallback"` prints a `"<Family> Fallback"` face with `size-adjust` and the ascent, descent and line-gap overrides, put after the family in every font token; rule 39 says so.
