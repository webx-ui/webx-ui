Modules in the theme (THEMES §10, TH3.1). `webx-ui/themes`: `webx:theme:sync` writes the `resources/theme/*.css` of every installed module into one `public/themes/modules/<hash>/modules.css` (`ModuleStyles`), and `@webxTheme` links it under the theme layers, so a theme restyles a module by its classes; `Testing\ModuleThemeContract` is the test a module runs on its base look and the classes its `AGENTS.md` lists. `webx-ui/module-admin`: `@webxPart('<point>', …)` prints `parts/<point>.blade.php` from the chain of views — a theme adds to a module's page without overriding it — before the module's partial; «Customise» starts from it. `webx-ui/module-blocks`: an offered block type stands back from a slug the theme carries (`webx:blocks:offered` says so; `webx:theme:sync` puts the theme's). `webx-ui/module-inbox`: the form's base look on site tokens — fields two to a row by the form's width, 44px targets, a visible error and thank-you — and the theme's demo forms (`demo/inbox/*.json`). `webx-ui/module-seo`: the trail, the page FAQ and the interlinking block get a base look and new classes (`webx-breadcrumbs__list`, `__item`, `__item--current`, `__link`, `webx-links__item`, `__link`). `webx-ui/theme-default`: showcase «Kitchen sink › Modules». Without a theme nothing is linked and nothing changes.
