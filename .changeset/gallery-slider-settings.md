Widgets: the `gallery` block's slider has its settings — 1, 2 or 3 slides at a time (by the width of the column), slide or fade, arrows, dots, thumbnails, starting over after the last, and turning on its own every N seconds (5 by default; never for a visitor who asked for less motion). A click on a slide still opens the lightbox. A block saved before the fields is the slider it was. An installed `gallery` type gets the fields when the type is updated from the package.
