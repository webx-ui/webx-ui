`webx-ui/module-debug`: the bar on the page is whole. The summary line opens a panel over the lower part of the window with the tabs SQL (statements with values, time, the place that issued them, transactions indented; repeated shapes grouped with «likely N+1», «show these»; slow ones marked; filter, order or by time, «Copy» and «Copy EXPLAIN»), Timeline (nested spans with the statements as marks; a click on a span narrows the SQL tab to its window), Blocks (regions and blocks with own and total time and statements, the theme layer of the type, the views and whose they are, «Open in panel» where the administrator may edit), Cache, Request, Other and History (the administrator's recent reports). Its height is dragged by the top edge; open, line or hidden to a dot, the tab, the height and the order are kept in `localStorage`. It stays left of the open drawer of the panel on the site — `webx-ui/module-admin`'s `site-bar.js` now sends `wx-bar:layout` with the width the drawer takes and marks its root with `data-open` — and a window narrower than 640 px gets a short line, the panel at full height and rows as cards. The words are in all ten languages; the report carries `links` to the panel.
