Theme-default: a new block, «Text and tiles on a photo» (`text-tiles`). A photo across the section under a shade, and over it the words — a line above the heading, the heading with its accent (`==words==`), a text, a button on `wx-link` (a page, an address, a new tab, a file to download) — beside up to four glass tiles, each an icon or a figure with its unit, a label and a line. Wide: the words on the left, the tiles two by two on the right; narrow: the words above and the tiles under them, two across from a phone up and one a row in a sidebar. The shade is dark or light, from the tokens, and the dark scheme turns it round; it is even, so the words keep WCAG AA on any photo. The glass is more of the shade with the photo blurred through it, a denser tile where the browser has no `backdrop-filter`; an SVG icon is painted in the colour of the words, so one file reads on both shades. «Parallax» and its strength are the hero's; the block is edge to edge or a card in the column. A tile with nothing in it is not printed, and a block with neither words nor tiles prints nothing. Its own variables `--text-tiles-veil`, `--text-tiles-glass`, `--text-tiles-glass-solid`, `--text-tiles-blur` are what a site's theme turns. A site takes it with `php artisan webx:blocks:import --theme --publish`; the showcase has Kitchen sink › Blocks › Text and tiles.
