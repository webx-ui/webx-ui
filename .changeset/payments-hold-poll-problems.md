module-payments: the hold — `Payments::capture()` whole or in part and `void()`, with a dry run; a refusal of the gateway is the problem `capture_failed`, a hold without a term from the gateway lasts the type's `maxHoldDays()`. The background poll `webx:payments:poll` every five minutes: a lost notification or an approval the buyer did not come back from comes to its end, an attempt past its deadline expires with an internal event of the journal, a hold that ends within a day is told once a day (`hold_expiring`). A new attempt asks and closes the earlier ones at the gateway first and is refused when one is paid or money is held. The events `PaymentSucceeded`, `PaymentFailed`, `PaymentProblem`; «problem resolved» (`resolveProblem()`); «Top up» of a paid order that owes more and «Pay online now» of an order on receipt (`switchMethod()`) on the page of payment; `cashOnDelivery()` for the waybill.
