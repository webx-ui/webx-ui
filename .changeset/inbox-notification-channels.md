"Notify through" on the Notifications tab of a form: the channels of the notifications module, chosen with search, beside the recipients of the letter — shown only when the server says the module is installed. The submission's card gets a «Channels» tab with a row per recipient and a warning when a channel did not deliver; "Send the notification again" resends those rows too. New types `FormChannel` and `ChannelDelivery`.
