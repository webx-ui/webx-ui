widgets: what a visitor does not see before they act no longer holds the first paint. The form in a closed dialog (its `form`, `steps`, `field`, `datetime`, `summary` stylesheets) and the hidden «Confirm your e-mail» banner of `module-users` load their CSS with `media="print"` switched on when loaded, and in `<noscript>` as before; a widget the page also shows at once, or a dialog printed open, stays blocking. New: `Widgets::later()`, `@webxLater(<when>)` … `@endwebxLater`, `Widgets::claimedLater()`.
