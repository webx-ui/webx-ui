Delivery in the panel (delivery spec §14, decisions 40 and 42). Six sections in the group «Delivery»: the methods in the checkout's order (dragged; a new one starts from its type; the kind of points, or a new kind, for a pick-up; the localities of the shop's courier; the price in units), the shop's own points (a table by kind and state; the hours of the week and the exceptions), the carriers (each directory with its last success, a run going on, a held pass, staleness, records and remarks; «Run» to the queue, «Confirm removal»; settings and keys — a key is never sent back, only «set», and typed anew to replace), the geography by hand (countries switched on from the ISO list and ordered, the leading carrier of a country; regions; localities by country, region, source and state — made, edited, switched off, or deleted when nothing holds them; a locality like an existing one is offered instead, 409 with the similar ones, or made anyway), the popular towns of the checkout's dialog (added by search, dragged, removed) and the binding queue (bind, make a shared locality or leave unbound — by the rows ticked or by the whole filter). A town the search does not find is added right from the point's form and the courier's list. Permissions `delivery.view`, `delivery.manage`, `delivery.ship` (the word «ship» in `webx-auth::permissions`); described screens `delivery.method-form`, `delivery.point-form`, `delivery.locality-form` with the fields `wx-delivery-locality`, `wx-delivery-localities`, `wx-delivery-region`, `wx-delivery-country`, `wx-delivery-schedule`. The npm half is the new package `@webx-ui/module-delivery`: `...delivery()` in the panel.
