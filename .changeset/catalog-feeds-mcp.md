Product feeds reach agents: `catalog_feeds_*` tools (list, get, presets, create, update, delete, preview, rebuild, reissue_key, builds, build_get, exclusions, exclusions_set, mappings, mappings_set, taxonomy_search, taxonomy_import) and the resources `catalog://feed-variables` and `catalog://feed-rules`, under the catalogue's scopes and permissions. Every write takes `dry_run`, and the address with its key goes only to a connection that may write. An unknown variable in a template is now answered with the similar names, in the panel too.
