The six blocks of the default theme (THEMES §9.1, TH2.3). `webx-ui/theme-default` carries `blocks/hero`, `text`, `media-text`, `cards`, `cta` and `contact`, which `webx:theme:sync` puts on every site with the theme: CSS on the site tokens and `@container` only, every word a visitor reads a translatable field. `hero` — on the page, a tint, the accent or over a picture, left or centred, the column or edge to edge, one or two buttons; `text` — rich prose in a reading column or the whole column (a body of plain lines prints as paragraphs); `media-text` — a picture on either side of its words; `cards` — two to four across at most, a photo or an icon, the whole card a link; `cta` — a band with one or two buttons; `contact` — the contacts of Settings › Contacts and an Inbox form by slug, labels in ten languages. The starter's showcase has Kitchen sink › Blocks: a page per block, every variant and a narrow column. `webx-ui/module-blocks`: the demo no longer seeds a slug the theme carries, and **`webx:theme:sync --adopt=hero,text`** hands the theme a demo type nobody changed since it was seeded — anything else under the slug is still refused. `webx-ui/themes`: `Contracts\SyncStep::sync()` takes the adopted slugs as a fourth argument (`array $adopt = []`); a step of your own adds it to its signature.
