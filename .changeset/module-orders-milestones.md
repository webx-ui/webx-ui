Orders (`module-orders`, stage 4 of webx-ui#89): milestones and statuses through the service, each call one transaction with the order's row locked. `Orders::mark()` sets `paid`, `received` or `cancelled` once — a repeat answers with the first mark (`MilestoneMark`, `already_marked`) and dispatches nothing, so two marks of «paid» at one moment make one `OrderPaid`; `paid` takes down `awaiting_payment` and `payment_failed_at`; a cancelled order is neither paid nor received (`OrderCancelledAlready`); `cancelled` marks the tenders left on the order given back and carries them in `OrderCancelled`. `paymentFailed()` writes a history line and `payment_failed_at` (`OrderPaymentFailed`); `stopAwaitingPayment()` takes the flag down with a history line and no event, refused for a paid or a cancelled order. `moveTo()` moves freely between active statuses, sets the milestone of the status with the source `status#<id>` or refuses the whole move, answers a `StatusMove` (with `dryRun: true` — writing nothing) and dispatches `OrderStatusChanged`, then `OrderPublicStatusChanged` only when the public status changes, then the milestone's event. `patchBlock()` merges a patch into a block checked by its owner (`OrderBlockUpdated`), `note()` writes a line of history, `updateDetails(OrderDetails)` changes the contacts, the recipient, the comment and the manager note, into the panel's journal (`orders.order`) and `OrderDetailsChanged`.
