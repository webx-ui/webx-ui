`webx-ui/module-payments`: the payment step of the checkout is a list group → method, `<x-webx-payments::methods>`. Choosing a group chooses its first method at once; a group of one method is one row that speaks for the method; one group on the site is the flat list it was, without heads. A group's pictogram and the marks of the payment systems stand in the rows, and on a step narrower than 480 px an open group leaves its marks to its methods. The buyer will notice one thing: methods of one group now stand together, so an order like «card, on receipt, instalments» becomes «card, instalments, on receipt». Without JavaScript all of it works by the stylesheet and the form: `PUT /api/payments/selection` also takes `group`, read by `Methods::picked()`. `GET /api/payments/methods` answers `groups`, and the new script `methods.js` is served beside `step.js`. For a theme: `webx-payments__methods`, `__method`, `__method-text`, `__method-title` and `__method-note` are now `webx-payments__groups`, `__option`, `__row-text`, `__row-title` and `__row-note`, the rest is under `### Classes` in `AGENTS.md`; a copy of `step.blade.php` keeps working, but loses the look of those classes and should print `<x-webx-payments::methods>`.
