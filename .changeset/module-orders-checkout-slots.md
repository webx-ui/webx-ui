module-orders: delivery and payment in the checkout as slots of their modules. `WebxUi\Orders\Checkout\Slots`: the contracts `Slot`, `ShippingSlot` (`totalsLine()` — the line «Delivery cost»: an amount, «Free» with the old price crossed out, «at carrier rates» with an estimate, or «choose a method»; `choice()` — `ShippingChoice` for the payment step through `CheckoutContext::shippingChoice()`) and `PaymentSlot` (`awaitsPayment()`, `next()` — a gateway right after the order, `payAgainUrl()` — «Pay» on «Thank you»); the registry `CheckoutSlots` (`delivery` at 200, `payment` at 300, once each) a module fills from `callAfterResolving`; `SlotStep` puts a slot among the steps. Without a line that goes by mail there is no delivery step. A slot that throws while the buyer looks leaves the page alive — its section says it could not be loaded and the error goes to the log; on the confirmation it makes no order (422). The page marks the event `webx:checkout:slot-changed` and the address of the column for its script. The fakes `FakeShippingSlot` and `FakePaymentSlot` and the contract tests `SlotContractTestCase` that the modules of delivery and payment extend.
