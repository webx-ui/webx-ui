module-cart, module-delivery, module-payments: the HTML their JSON APIs draw for the page to put in place speaks the language of the page that asked, not the application's (`APP_LOCALE`). The cart's panel (`X-Webx-Cart-View`), the delivery step drawn again (`X-Webx-Delivery-View`), the delivery methods and the payment step came back in English on a site whose own languages were others, because the APIs' addresses carry no language. A new middleware of the cart, `SpeakTheLanguageOfThePage`, on the cart's, the delivery's and the payment step's routes takes `X-Webx-Locale` (or `?locale=` on a ready address) when the site has that language, otherwise the site's main language. The cart's checkout button leads to the checkout of the same language (`checkout.localised` behind the prefix). `dist/delivery.js` and the payment step's `step.js` send `X-Webx-Locale` from `<html lang>`, as `cart.js` does; the step's ready addresses of towns, points and streets carry `locale=`, so the browser's cache of a list is kept per language.
