Landings of the catalogue to an agent: `catalog_landings_list`, `_get`, `_facets`, `_count`, `_create`, `_update`, `_publish`, `_unpublish`, `_delete`, `_restore`, `_generate` (`dry_run` is the preview) and `_generation`, served as the catalogue's tools with its scopes; a set is written in facet codes and value slugs and answered with both the slugs and the ids it is stored by. `catalog://landings` holds the house rules. `catalog-landings` is offered by `webx:setup` and carries its npm package and panel registration for `webx:panel --sync`.
