`webx-ui/module-tasks`, T3 — the panel: the list of tasks with its views («Waiting for me», in work, drafts, closed), one task with its specification, stages, reports and journal and the buttons the rules allow (put to work, accept, send back, answer a question, release, cancel), the form as the described screen `tasks.form` with the rules of §3 laid over it, and the projects. `@webx-ui/module-tasks` is its npm half; the section is in the `webx:setup` catalogue, off by default. `@webx-ui/module-admin`: a module may put a count under `meta.badge` of its manifest, and the menu shows it beside the entry — tasks show what waits for a person.
