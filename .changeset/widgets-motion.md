`webx-ui/widgets`: what moves on a page (spec §10, §14, W5.2). **`<x-webx-video variant="background" :file :poster ratio>`** — the backdrop of a first screen behind whatever the tag wraps: a file of the site only, muted, looping, no controls; it plays only on screen with the tab in front, never by itself under reduced motion or on a connection saving data (then the file is not even asked for), the poster stays until the first frame, and a pause button is always in its corner (WCAG 2.2.2) — a pause is remembered for the next page. In `dist/video.js`, no file of its own. **`<x-webx-counter :value prefix suffix decimals duration>`** — the final number printed as the page's language writes it, counted up to when a counter below the screen comes into view; reduced motion and no JavaScript: the number at once. **`<x-webx-countdown to ended>`** — a timer to a wall time of the site's time zone (the opening hours' on the Contacts tab, else `app.timezone`) or a moment with its offset, ticking from the clock; at the end the `ended` text, or it goes with the nearest `[data-webx-countdown-scope]`; without JavaScript the line "Ends on …". Offered blocks **`counters`** and **`countdown`** (a date and a time of the site's zone, a text or hide at the end). Audit checks `widgets.video_pause` (a background video without its pause button) and `widgets.counter_number` (a counter whose markup lost its number). Words in ten languages. `webx-ui/theme-default`: Kitchen sink › Background video and › Counters and countdown.
