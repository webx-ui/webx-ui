Letters in the theme (THEMES §7.5, TH4.6). `webx-ui/theme-default`: **`views/mail/layout`** — the frame every letter of the site stands in: the site's name, the letter on a sheet, a "Sent from" line in the ten languages; tables and `style="…"` with values from `theme_token()`, so a preset or the owner's accent repaints the letters with the site. A module's letter asks for it with `@component('mail.layout', ['title' => …])`; a local theme overrides it in `theme/views/mail/layout.blade.php`. Its showcase gets «Kitchen sink › Mail». `webx-ui/module-inbox`: **the letter about a new submission stands in the theme's frame** with its colours from the tokens (`Mail\Look`); a site without a theme gets a frame of the package's own in theme-default's colours. `SubmissionReceived::sample($form, $answers)` builds the letter for answers nobody sent, nothing saved — for a preview. `webx-ui/themes`: **`theme_token()` resolves a value that refers to another token** (`font-heading` → the family of `font-body`); **`webx:theme:check` reads the letters** — the views under `mail/` of the layers and `resources/views/mail/` of the modules — and names a `var(` or a literal colour there (rule 25).
