Delivery: a carrier's settings take their options from its own account. A carrier implements `ProvidesSettingOptions` and marks a field `options: 'carrier'` (with `depends` and `search`): the carrier's dialog draws dependent selects — sender → contact, town → branch of sending — waits for the key and for the parent, empties the children when a parent changes, searches as the admin types, keeps the label of the chosen value in `settings._labels` (shown when the carrier is down) and says a value the account no longer has; «Refresh lists» goes past the ten-minute cache. A save asks the carrier again and takes only a value its list has. Agents: `delivery_carriers_options` (behind `delivery.manage`), and `delivery_carriers_update` checks as the panel does. The waybill: `WaybillRequest` has the recipient's first and last name apart (`recipientName()` joins them), `carrierLocality` — the carrier's own id of the town — and `extra`; a type that implements `DescribesWaybill` adds its own fields to the dialog and to `delivery_shipments_create`, and `supportsCod(): false` hides and refuses the cash on delivery. `CarrierLocalities::externalIds()` lets a satellite walk its towns. Breaking for a satellite reading `WaybillRequest::$recipientName`: it is `recipientName()` now; the order tab and the tool take `recipient_first_name` and `recipient_last_name` in place of `recipient_name`. A key the carrier gave no `label` is shown as «Access key», never by its code name.
