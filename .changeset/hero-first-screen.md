theme-default: the `hero` block takes an eyebrow over the H1, a height of the first screen with the words in the middle or at the top, and a badge under the words — the first screen of a home page and of an inner page on one block. Under a header over the page the words keep clear of it; under a header in the flow the two of them are the window. Blocks saved before print byte for byte what they printed; a site gets the fields with `webx:blocks:import --theme --replace=hero`.
