`module-catalog-properties` on the storefront (P3 of the properties series). A card lists the properties marked «in the card» (`webx-catalog-properties::card`, in `catalog.card.meta`); the page of a product gets «Specifications» by group (`webx-catalog-properties::table`, in `catalog.product.tabs`), where a value links to its page of one value if that page is open — a page of cards reads the values in one query. The filter draws a value's colour or picture before its label. A page of one property value is open to search only in a category (`noindex, follow` on a brand's page and at the root), titled by the property's `seo_pattern` (`{category}`, `{property}`, `{value}`) where it has one, and listed in the `catalog-filters` sitemap when it has products. Templates get `products()->property('color', 'black')`, `->property('weight', min: 1, max: 2)`, `->property('wifi')` and `$product->properties()`. The catalogue's core gains three optional facet interfaces for this — `ContextualIndexing`, `TitledFacet`, `SwatchedFacet` — and `FilterOption::$swatch`, drawn by the new `webx-catalog::filter.swatch` partial; facets that implement none behave as before.
