Banners: the video of a banner is the widgets' background video — muted, looping, a pause button, played only on screen, never under reduced motion or on a connection saving data. New fields `video_mobile` (played below the breakpoint instead of `video`) and `poster` (the picture where a video plays, eager on the first slide); a banner without them looks as before. The `hero` place takes an `h1` text layer. Widgets: a background video in a slide of `<x-webx-slider>` plays only while its slide is shown.
