The payments for an agent and in the letters (payments spec §16, §11.5). With `webx-ui/mcp` installed the section brings the tools `payments_*` — the methods (`_methods_list` says which keys are set, never what they are; `_methods_save` and `_methods_reorder` go through the panel's form and refuse a key), the trial of the step `_available`, the attempts `_list` and `_get` (an order's answer is its tab «Payment», by the same class), the actions of the order's card `_check`, `_mark_paid`, `_switch_method`, `_pay_link`, `_resolve_problem` behind `orders.manage` with a dry run that writes nothing, the summary `_problems`, and the exchange journal read only: `_exchange_list` without bodies, `_exchange_get` with the headers and bodies (text up to 64 KB or base64) behind `payments.exchange` — and the resource `payments://catalog`. With `webx-ui/module-notifications` every event of an order gets `payment_url`, `payment_instructions` and `payment_method`, and a problem of a payment is told to the manager as the new event `payments.problem` through the channels chosen on the new tab «Payments» of the site's settings (`payments.notify.problem`). For that, `NotificationEvents::extend()` lets a module add variables to events it does not own — in the channel's editor, the examples and every send, by hand included — and `Notifications::render()` takes the record and the event to show them in a preview. «Check with the gateway» can be asked as a dry run: the gateway is asked and journaled, nothing of the attempt is written.
