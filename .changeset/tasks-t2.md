`webx-ui/module-tasks`, T2 — the queue by MCP: eighteen `tasks_*` tools over the operations of T1, scopes `tasks:read`, `tasks:write`, `tasks:work`, `tasks:review`, and the resources `tasks://rules` and `tasks://projects/{slug}`. An agent names itself with `agent`; without it the caller must be a person on a person's connection, so a stage marked `human` and the answer to an agent's question never go to a key issued for a program. `webx-ui/mcp`: a tool's scope may be a list, any one of which lets a token in, and a resource whose address has a `{variable}` is served as a resource template.
