module-cart: the widget — `<x-webx-cart mode="drawer|dialog|dropdown">` with the server's counter, the panel drawn by the server (`html` in the API's answer for `X-Webx-Cart-View`), buy buttons sent in place by `data-webx-cart-add`, `window.webx.cart` and the `webx:cart:change` event, the base look `cart.css`; the widgets' dialog gains `drawer` (a panel at the edge of the window, its whole height); `theme-default` shows the cart among the header's actions when the module is installed.
