Orders: the checkout asks the extra fields. The fields of the tab «Checkout» stand in their places — `contacts` before the button of the contacts, `extra-before-comment` and `extra-after-comment` around the comment — each a field of the widgets with its hint, a list with its options, a required one with the browser's `required`. A field behind a checkbox shows only while it is ticked, without JavaScript too, and the server neither checks nor keeps it otherwise. The step `comment` is now «Additional» while it has fields and stays with the comment switched off; without fields it is «Comment» as before. Saving a step keeps the answers of its own places only, in the draft's `steps.fields`; the confirmation checks them against the definitions as they are then (`fields.required`, `fields.invalid`, `fields.option_gone`) and the order keeps a snapshot — the label and the value of every filled field, in the order of the page — in the new column `orders.fields` (`php artisan migrate`), also in `OrderData::$fields` and `PlaceOrder::$fields`. A step of a project prints a place with `@include('webx-orders::checkout.parts.fields', ['place' => …])` and needs nothing else; no step may be called `fields`. A theme with its own copy of `checkout/steps/contacts.blade.php` or `comment.blade.php` adds that line to show the fields. The order's pages, letters and card show the fields in the next releases.
