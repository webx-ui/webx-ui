themes: `webx:theme:critical` keeps the rules of the children of a block the edge of the first screen cuts when that block is drawn by its whole height — a texture `inset: 0; object-fit: cover` held to its bottom, a background `cover` or set off the top. A footer that starts in the first screen with its bottom row below it no longer comes out shorter with the critical CSS alone, and `--check` draws it pixel for pixel. The warning about rules in opposite orders on different pages names both rules and the screens that apply each order.
