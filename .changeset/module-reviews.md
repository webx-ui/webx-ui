`webx-ui/module-reviews`: reviews — a photo, a name, a job title, a rating and a text — in flat categories with two orders, on any page as the offered Reviews block (one, a grid, a slider or a marquee, with a filter by category) and in site templates through `reviews()`. No page, no address and no markup of its own. `webx:setup` offers the module, and `webx:doctor` says whether `reviews()` is the package's own.
