`webx-ui/module-reviews`: reviews — a photo, a name, a job title, a rating and a text — in flat categories with two orders, on any page as the offered Reviews block (one, a grid, a slider or a marquee, with a filter by category) and in site templates through `reviews()`. No page, no address and no markup of its own. `webx:setup` offers the module, and `webx:doctor` says whether `reviews()` is the package's own. For an agent, `reviews_*` and `review_categories_*` through the panel's own form and order code, and `reviews://catalog` to read first; `webx:demo` seeds two categories and eight reviews, a page `/reviews` with a grid and a slider in a demo service.
