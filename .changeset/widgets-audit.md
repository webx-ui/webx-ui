`webx-ui/widgets` in the site audit (with `webx-ui/module-audit`): `widgets.before_consent` (error) — a YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps or OpenStreetMap iframe, or a known counter or pixel, that a page asks for before the visitor answers the cookie banner; its fix "Make it wait for consent" rewrites code pasted into content where it is stored (an iframe's `src` becomes `data-src`, a script `type="text/plain"`, both get `data-webx-consent` of their category), written through the module's model. `widgets.banner_off` (warning) — the banner switched off while something third-party is on the site, one finding for the site with its pages; "Turn the cookie banner on" writes the Cookie tab's switch, which wins over the config. `widgets.lightbox_size` and `widgets.slider_pause` (warnings) — a lightbox link without `data-width`/`data-height`, a moving slider whose view lost its pause button; `widgets.contact_both` (notice) — the quick-contact button and the bottom bar on one page. A site names its own third parties in `webx-widgets.audit.third-party`. `webx-ui/module-audit`: page readers — `AuditPageReader`, registered into `PageReaders`, reads a module's own facts off each crawled page while the parser has it (the HTML is not kept); its checks find them in `$page->fact('<id>')`. The guide: Site widgets.
