Localization: the language of the first visit. `WEBX_LOCALE_FIRST_VISIT=uk` (a language code of the site) sends a person's first visit to a page of another language to the same page in that one, once — a 302, `private, no-store` — and keeps the cookie `webx_lang`; with it nobody is sent anywhere again. Robots, link previews and the site audit are never sent (`first_visit.bots`), nor non-GET, JSON, prefetch, the panel, sitemaps, feeds, `?no_redirect`, or a page that has only a home page in that language. New contract `Contracts\PageInLanguage`, bound by widgets to the language switcher's links; the switcher's markup does not change. `WEBX_LOCALE_FIRST_VISIT_OFFER=true` adds, on later visits to a page of another language, a "Switch to …?" card over the page beside the switcher (`language-offer.js`, words in all ten languages, `theme::theme.language_offer.*` to reword); declining keeps the cookie `webx_lang_offer`. `webx:doctor` warns when it is on but cannot work. Off by default: nothing changes on a site that does not set it.
