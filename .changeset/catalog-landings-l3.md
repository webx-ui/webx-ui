Landings of the catalogue in the panel: «Catalog» → «Landings» with the form `catalog.landing-form`, and «Create in bulk» — `POST catalog/landings/generate` fills slug, name, H1 and SEO templates over bases × the values of a facet, `dry_run` previews the rows with their conflicts (the address or the set taken, no products), a large generation runs in the queue with its progress. The count suggests the address of a set; a landing's recommended products come with their names.
