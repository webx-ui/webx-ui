Codes and addresses made of a name go through one generator, `WebxUi\Localization\Slug`: transliterated by the name's own language, the site's default language first. A label «Хіт» is `khit`, not `xit`; a name with no letters is prefixed by its kind (`−20%` → `label-20`). Labels, stock statuses, properties, values, brands and categories of every module share it.
