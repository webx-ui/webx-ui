The site's captcha (spec ORDERS §28.1): one provider and one pair of keys for every form that wants one. `widgets` — the service `WebxUi\Widgets\Captcha` (`provider()`, `configured()`, `check($request, $action)`, `message()`, `widget()`, `script()`, `status()`), the config `webx-widgets.captcha` from `WEBX_CAPTCHA_*` with the inbox's `WEBX_INBOX_*` (and a published `webx-inbox.captcha`) read when they are not set, `<x-webx-captcha action="…">` for a form with its `captcha.js` (an invisible widget and v3 on submit, held by `detail.wait`), and the tab «Captcha» of the settings: the provider (`off` — none), the kind of reCAPTCHA key, Turnstile's mode and the site key; the secret stays in the environment, `settings_get` of a `captcha.*` key says only whether it is set. `module-inbox` — the forms' captcha is the site's: `antispam.captcha: on` asks for its provider, a provider named by a form of before still means on and is drawn with its own; a data migration makes the provider of most switched-on forms the site's when the site has chosen none (`down()` takes it back), nothing in `.env` changes; the audit's new `inbox.captcha_provider_mismatch` names a form left with the other provider, and `inbox.captcha_keys` names the variables with the new names and the old in brackets. `module-orders` — `orders.checkout.captcha` (off) on the tab «Checkout»: the captcha on the confirmation and in «Buy in one click», refused by the settings while the site has no provider.
