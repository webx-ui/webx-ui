`webx-ui/widgets`: before and after, and the table of contents (spec §14, W5.3). **`<x-webx-compare :before :after start ratio before-label after-label>`** — the after picture over the before one in the shape the media library stored for them, cut at a divider that is a range input: the arrows move it, a screen reader hears a slider, a mouse drags it and so does a finger that moves sideways (up and down still scrolls the page); without JavaScript the two stand side by side with their labels. **`<x-webx-toc for depth title side fold>`** — the list is made by the server from the finished page, of its slot, an element by id or `<main>`, and the headings get their ids there, so every link works without JavaScript; wide, beside the text and sticky under the header; narrow, a bar above it that names the section being read and opens the list. Offered blocks **`compare`** and **`toc`** (the whole page, or a text of its own that the list stands beside). Audit checks `widgets.compare_range` (before and after without its slider) and `widgets.toc_target` (a link of a table of contents to a heading the page does not have). Words in ten languages. `webx-ui/theme-default`: Kitchen sink › Before and after and › Table of contents.
