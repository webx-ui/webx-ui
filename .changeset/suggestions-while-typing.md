Suggestions while typing (WIDGETS §18, W6.5). `webx-ui/widgets`: **`<x-webx-suggest :action :source>`** — a GET search to the results page, which the script (`dist/suggest.js|css`, ≈3.7 KB gzip, claimed only by the component) makes an ARIA combobox: it asks `source` after a pause, aborts the stale request and drops a late answer, and puts the HTML the site answers under the field as it is — the shell needs only `data-webx-option` on what can be chosen and `data-webx-section` + `data-webx-heading` on a group. Arrows, Home/End, Enter, Escape; «nothing found», a status line for a screen reader, the panel inside a phone's screen above its keyboard; recent searches in the browser with `:recent`. Without `action` it is a field to stand inside another form: a value chosen fills the field (`change`) and sends nothing. Events `webx:suggest:select` (cancelable) and `webx:suggest:results`; words `widgets.suggest.*` in ten languages, each a prop; icon `search`. Showcase door `GET webx/widgets/showcase/suggest` (only with the demo). `webx-ui/module-catalog`: **`GET /catalog/suggest?q=`** (`webx.catalog.suggest`) answers the view `webx-catalog::suggest` — products found with a price, their count, «see all» (`suggest.limit`, 6); the search page's box is `<x-webx-suggest>` over it. `webx-ui/theme-default`: showcase «Kitchen sink › Forms › Suggestions» — a search box and a field inside a delivery form, in English, Russian and Polish.
