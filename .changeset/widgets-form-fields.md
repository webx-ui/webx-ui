The fields of a form (WIDGETS §17.2, W6.1). `webx-ui/widgets`: **`<x-webx-field>`** — `text`, `email`, `tel`, `url`, `password` (a Show button with `aria-pressed`, never printed back), `number`, `textarea`, `select`, `radio`, `checkbox` (a group with `:choices`, one tick without), `consent` (its sentence in the slot), `date`, `file` (a button and a line in the page's words), `hidden` — in one markup: `webx-field`, `--<type>`, `__label`, `__control`, `__hint`, `__error`, states `is-invalid`, `is-required`, `is-disabled`; a group is a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, the control is described by its hint and its error and says `aria-invalid="true"`. The value is `old($name)`, the error `$errors->first($name)` (a named `bag`, or `error`): a dotted name (`fields.email`, `items.0.qty`) is sent with brackets and its error found by the dots, so a form posted without JavaScript comes back with each error under its field; a group that came back ticks only what it sent. `autocomplete`, `inputmode`, `enterkeyhint` default by type (`false` — none). The look is a skeleton on `--site-*` (`--webx-field-*` of its own): 44px controls, a 2px focus outline, the error in words and in the danger colour; a theme overrides `components/field/<type>.blade.php`. Words `widgets.field.*` in ten languages, each a prop. `dist/field.js|css`, about 1.7 KB gzip. `webx-ui/theme-default`: «Kitchen sink › Forms › Fields» — every type in every state, long German and Polish labels, a narrow column. The guide «Forms» starts with the fields.
