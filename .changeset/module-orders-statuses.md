module-orders: the screens «Orders → Statuses» and «Orders → Settings». Both levels of the statuses with the orders in each, dragged into order and into another public group — with the buyers who will see a new name counted first — written in every language, deleted softly with the consequences said first and brought back; refused when an order would have nowhere to go (the initial status, the last active stage of a group with orders, a public status with stages still in it). `OrderStatuses` does the same in code. The settings keep the number format with its trial number and the initial statuses in `module-settings`, the config `webx-orders` deciding without it. A move into a status that cancels now needs a reason from the panel and an agent, as `cancel()` does — in the dialogs of one order and of a selection too.
