theme-default: the header and the footer are the compositions `header` and `footer` — the fallbacks of their regions, as before, with every piece a node a theme patches by id in `theme/compositions/header/default.json` (or `footer/`): `header.contacts` (above the bar), `header.logo`, `header.nav`, `header.phones`, `header.cta`, `header.languages`; `footer.contacts`, `footer.menu`, `footer.consent`, `footer.copy` in the columns of `footer.inner`. The announcement bar goes into the header with `{"remove": ["notice-bar"]}` in `layout/default.json` and an `add` of `webx-notice-bar` to `header.topbar`. Without a patch the HTML is the same up to the whitespace between tags. A named slot of a component is a `tag` node `x-slot:<name>` among its children (elsewhere a finding). `ComparesHtml` counts the indent between a tag and its text as one space.

**A site or a theme with its own `components/header.blade.php` (or `footer`)** keeps printing it; `webx:theme:check` now notes the copy. To go over: `php artisan webx:composition:show header --blade` prints what the composition gives; write the differences as a patch — `replace` of `header.logo` for a logo of your own, `add` to `header.actions` or `header.topbar` for a node of your own, `remove` of what you do not show, `attrs`/`props` of `header` for its classes and stickiness —, delete the copy and compare the page before and after. A view of the theme in a node's place gets `$nav`, `$calls`, `$contacts`, `$hours`, `$email`, `$phones`, `$languages` (the footer: `$contacts`, `$address`, `$items`).
