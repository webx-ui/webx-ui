The panel on the site, third stage (SB3 of `WEBX_UI_SITE_BAR.md`): blocks. On a page that gets the bar the blocks are printed with their markers and the record's own pair around them (`<!--wx-entity:{type}:{id}-->`), with published values and no error notices; a visitor's page is byte for byte what it was. The pencil in the new «Blocks» tab turns picking on: a frame follows the pointer with the block's type, a nested block wins over its container, and a click chooses the block instead of following its link; `Esc` turns it off. The tab shows the record's blocks as a map and a chosen block's fields with the constructor's form and the draft's values; «Save to draft» writes the changed fields into the record's draft and puts the block, drawn from the draft, in place of the old one on the page with a dashed «Draft» frame and its script started again. The head of the drawer counts the draft's changes with «Publish» and «Discard». `webx-ui/module-blocks`: the `blocks` section of the bar (the record's section's permission and `blocks.view`), `GET /api/cms/blocks/content/{type}/{id}` and `POST /api/cms/blocks/content/{type}/{id}/edit` (`set` operations through `ContentEdit` into `saveDraft()` from the panel, the record's revision, 409 with who changed it, `locale` for a translated field), `Renderer::block()` and `Renderer::isMarking()`, and region markers on such pages. `webx-ui/module-admin`: `Insertion::marks()` — the bar's decision taken before the page is printed; `POST /api/cms/site-bar/publish` and `/discard`; `EntityLink::publish` takes a `Publishing` class, `DraftPublishing` for records with a draft, registered for pages, articles, events, recipes, services and vacancies; the context says `editing`, `revision`, and the draft's `changes` and `mine`; the script on the page draws the frames and swaps blocks. `@webx-ui/module-admin`: the draft's buttons in the drawer, `onSitePage()`, `useSiteBar()` and the new bridge messages. `@webx-ui/module-blocks`: the «Blocks» tab (`WxBlocksSiteBarTab`) and `createSiteBarBlocksApi()`.
