module-orders: notifications of the orders (spec §10), with `webx-ui/module-notifications` installed. Seven events — `orders.placed`, `orders.public_status`, `orders.paid`, `orders.payment_failed`, `orders.received`, `orders.cancelled`, `orders.refunded` — with the values of an order (number, links to the panel and to the buyer's page, contacts, the lines as a list, the money as strings in the order's currency, delivery and payment, the public status and, for the staff, the internal one) and default templates for e-mail, Telegram and any other provider in ten languages. A milestone's event goes to the channels chosen in the order settings (`orders.notify.*`), entering a public status to the channels of that status — chosen in its dialog; a move between the stages of one public status tells nobody. A site starts with three channels, made once by `migrate`: «Order accepted» and «Payment received» to the buyer, «New order» to the first e-mail of the site's contacts — switched off without one, and `webx:doctor` says so; without the notifications module the orders work as before and `webx:doctor` notes that nobody is told. The card shows what was sent about the order under its history, `orders_history` answers it as `notifications`, `orders_public_statuses_save` takes `notify_channels`, and `orders://catalog` lists the events. module-notifications: a variable may be declared `internal` — a channel that writes to an addressee and uses one saves with a warning.
