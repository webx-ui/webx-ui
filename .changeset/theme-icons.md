Icons of the theme chain (THEMES §13.1, TH4.1). `webx-ui/themes`: each layer's `icons/<name>.svg` becomes a `<symbol>` of one sprite, the higher layer winning by name (**`ThemeIcons`**); **`webx:theme:sync`** publishes it to `public/themes/icons/<hash>/icons.svg` («icons — the sprite of the chain»). **`<x-theme::icon name="phone" />`** prints an `<svg class="theme-icon">` one em wide in `currentColor` with a `<use>` of it, `aria-hidden` — or `role="img"` with `label="…"`; an unknown name or an unpublished sprite is an empty `<svg>` with a hint in debug, never an exception. **`webx:theme:check`** reads every icon file (a lowercase dashed name, a `viewBox`, no literal colour, no script, style or handler — the sprite drops them) and the views and block templates of the layers (an icon no layer has, an inline `<svg>`). `webx-ui/theme-default`: 27 icons (arrows, chevrons, check, close, plus, minus, menu, search, phone, mail, map pin, clock, calendar, user, share, link, download, globe, info, alert, star, play), `theme-icon` sized in `base.css`, icons in the labels of the `contact` block and on the «more» line of `cards`, and the page Kitchen sink › Icons.
